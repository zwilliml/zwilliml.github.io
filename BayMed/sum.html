Bayles-Medder Softball 1975 Summary and Tournament Results

1975 Season Summary

Bayles-Medder had an overall record of 7-10, going 6-9 in the regular season and 1-1 in the Charleston Tournament.

Mike Zwilling had a great year, getting 33 hits in 59 at bats for a team-leading .559 batting average, his second highest season batting average of his career.
