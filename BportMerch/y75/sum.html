Bridgeport Merchants Softball 1975 Summary and Tournament Results

1975 Season Summary

Bridgeport Merchants were league champions in 1975! You saw the newspaper clipping of their victory on the team home page.

The championship series against tough St. Francisville was best-of-5, with the Merchants losing the first 2 games to put their backs up against the wall. But they came back to win the last 3 and the championship.

The Merchants had an overall record of 9-6. Note: It is likely that there were many regular season games prior to game 1 listed on the schedule page, but there are no records because I was playing softball in Charleston for Bayles-Medder during summer school at EIU.

The Merchants won 5 of their 9 victories by exciting narrow 1-run margins, including 3 nail-biters during the championship tournament.

Tournament results:
Tournament Dates Record Result
League Championship Tournament August 10-17 6-2 Won league championship!
Bridgeport Tournament August 22-24 3-2 Finished in 4th place
