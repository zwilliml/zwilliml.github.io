Bridgeport Merchants Softball 1976 Summary and Tournament Results

1976 Season Summary

Bridgeport Merchants were county champions in 1976!

In the best-of-5 tournament finals against H & H, Bridgeport won the first 2 games, lost the next 2, and squeaked out an 8-6 victory in the last game to secure the title.

The Merchants had an overall record of 26-16.
