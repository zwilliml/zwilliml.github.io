Bridgeport Merchants Softball 1977 Summary and Tournament Results

1977 Season Summary

Bridgeport Merchants won the regional tournament in 1977! They went on to finish 33rd in the state.

The Merchants sported an overall record of 27-17, which includes a league record of 20-7.

The Merchants finished first in their division, but lost the league title to Chittick's.
