Bridgeport Merchants Softball 1978 Summary and Tournament Results

1978 Season Summary

The Merchants had an overall record of 31-20, which includes a league record of 16-7, although the results of 4 league games are missing.

The Merchants finished 2nd in their division, 2nd in the Olney Invitational Tournament, and notched historic wins over tough teams Lawrenceville Merchants, Olney Merchants, and Vincennes Merchants.
