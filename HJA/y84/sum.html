Harry Jerald Agency Softball 1984 Summary and Tournament Results

Harry Jerald Agency finished second in the Umpires Tournament, with 4 wins and 2 losses. Three of the wins were by one run.

In the Niles Tournament, the team had a record of 1-2.

We have no information on the regular season, other than some statistics that you can see on the game 1 scoresheet.

Mike Zwilling was named to the league's All Star Team, and played in the exhibition All Star Game. You can see the scoresheet for the game on the schedule page.
