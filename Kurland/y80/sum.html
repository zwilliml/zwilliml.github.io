Kurland Steel Softball 1980 Summary and Tournament Results

1979 Season Summary

Kurland Steel Softball had an overall record of 10-8 in 1980.

Mike Zwilling was named to the All-Star Team of the Wednesday Competitive League. He played in the four exhibition all star games, going 5 for 6 for an .833 batting average (statistics not counted). Scoresheets for these four games are on the schedule page.
