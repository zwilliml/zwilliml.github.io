Lawrenceville American Legion Baseball 1971 Season Summary

The 1971 Lawrenceville American Legion team started the season hot with a 4-game winning streak, and later added a 5-game winning streak. At one point in the season they sported an outstanding record of 10 wins and only 2 losses.

But they staggered at the end with a 6-game losing streak, including 2 straight losses in the district tournament.

They ended the season with a record of 14-10.
