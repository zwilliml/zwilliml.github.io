Dene's Marathon Softball 1972 Summary and Tournament Results

1972 Season Summary

Dene's Marathon sported an overall record of 8-6, going 6-5 in the regular season and 2-1 in the Sumner Tournament.

Dene's lost in the first round of the tournament, but roared back to win the Loser's Bracket with 2 consecutive wins. Gaffner's won the Winner's Bracket.
