Faculty Intramural Softball 1995 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Fall in Last Inning

Blazing Saddles staged a 5-run 7th inning rally to stun the Faculty 6-5 in the first Prof on-field game of the season held Thursday at Butler-Rodman Park. The Faculty record dropped to 0-2, having forfeited their first game Tuesday to Rat Pack when only 5 Prof players could be roused.

The Faculty seemed to have matters well in hand, forging a 5-1 lead in the first six innings on a typical Prof offense of 8 walks and 5 hits. Blazing Saddles lacked hitting punch, but the Blazers seemed to learn well from their teachers and started taking pitches in their half of the 7th, garnering several walks as the absence of retired Hall-of-Fame pitcher Bill Markley took its toll, although hurler Chris Garloch performed well overall. Mix in a few Prof errors and the rally happened very quickly.

The Faculty used 6 walks and one hit in the first 4 innings to produce one run, then Gregg Nieschwitz towered a triple over center field for the first Prof hit of the season in the 5th, scoring on a Blazer shortstop error. Dan Garloch followed with a triple but was stranded.

Gerry Wuchter hit another Prof triple in the 6th, this one slashed down the left-field line and scoring Jim Thoma who had singled. Wuchter then scored on Ben Frayser's rbi single to cap the Faculty scoring.
