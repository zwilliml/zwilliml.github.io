Faculty Intramural Softball 1995 Game Recap and Box Score

Phi Tau's Outclass Profs 10-3

The Phi Tau's used good hitting, consistent scoring, and fine pitching to defeat the Faculty 10-3 Monday, dropping the Prof record to 0-3 on the season.

The Greeks scored every inning but two, and struck out 8 Profs while issuing only 7 walks, effectively neutralizing an important segment of the Faculty offense. Only one Pedagogue run was earned, when Gregg Nieschwitz scored on a Mike DeMatteis sacrifice fly. The other two runs came on an errant throw by the Phi Tau third baseman in the sixth inning.

Jim Thoma led the Preceptors with two hits. The other Prof hits, all singles, came from DeMatteis, Mike Zwilling, Greg Carter, and hurler Jim Kimble. Jerry Brugh garnered two walks, while Zwilling, Nieschwitz, Jeri Thompson, Steve Cederbloom, and Gerry Wuchter had one bb each.
