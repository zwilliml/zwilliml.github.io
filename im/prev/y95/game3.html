Faculty Intramural Softball 1995 Game Recap and Box Score

Big Snakes Bite Profs 13-5

The Big Snakes (Sigma Nu A-team) built an early 13-run lead then coasted to a 13-5 pounding of a short-handed Faculty team Thursday at Butler-Rodman Park. The loss dropped the Prof record to 0-5, after a Wednesday forfeit loss to the Sigma Nu B-team Little Snakes.

Without retired Hall-of-Fame hurler Bill Markley, and with usual starter Jim Kimble unavailable, veteran Prof infielder/outfielder Mike Zwilling took to the mound for his first ever pitching appearance and promptly delivered a gopher ball on his first pitch that went for a Big Snake home run. The Greeks then followed with 5 runs in the 2nd and 7 more in the 3rd, capitalizing on the Faculty inability to field 10 players. Zwilling (0-1) walked 5 and struck out 4 in taking the loss.

Gerry Wuchter walloped a solo homer in the 6th to finally put a Prof run on the board. The Faculty then staged a 4-run rally in the 7th that the Snakes shrugged off. Zwilling tripled home 2 runs in the uprising, with Jim Thoma doubling in one run and Andy Boothe singling in the other. Wuchter, Zwilling, and Thoma also singled earlier in the game. Jerry Brugh and Ken Osborne added singles for the Preceptors.

The Faculty could manage only 3 walks in the game, as Big Snake hurler Chad Bielawski denied the Profs a large chunk of their usual offense. Bielawski revealed his good luck talisman as the game ended--folded under his hat was a print-out of the Faculty Softball Hall of Fame featuring Prof hurler Bill Markley.
