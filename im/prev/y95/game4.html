Faculty Intramural Softball 1995 Game Recap and Box Score

Tribe Scalps Profs 13-6

The Tribe scored 12 runs in the first inning, most after 2 outs, then coasted to a 13-6 pounding of a short-handed Faculty team Thursday at Butler-Rodman Park. The loss dropped the Prof record to 0-8, including 3 forfeit losses and an 8-2 loss to Frozen Ropes last Monday for which no statistics are available.

After using four different starters in the first four games, Prof manager Jerry Brugh tapped Steve Cederblom for his second consecutive pitching assignment. After the 12-run first inning eruption, Cederbloom settled down to hold the Indians to only one run, but the damage was done.

The Faculty managed 6 runs on a decent 9 hits but supplemented with only 4 walks. Undergrad pitching has improved noticably this year, denying the Faculty their usual quota of bases on ball. Big Snake hurler Chad Bielawski kept the Profs from another forfeit loss by volunteering his services in left field, and he led the Profs with 2 hits, including a double, and 2 ribbies. Mike Zwilling went 2-for-2 for the Preceptors, and added a walk and a sacrifice fly. Also contributing singles to the Prof offense were Dave Hudson, Jim Thoma, Greg Carter, Ben Frayser, and Cederbloom.
