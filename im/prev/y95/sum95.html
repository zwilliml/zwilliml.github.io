Faculty Intramural Softball 1995 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-Offs

1995 Season Summary

The dismal Faculty record of 0-9 reflected 4 forfeit losses as the Profs could not get enough players to show up. In the games they did play on the field, they often had to conscript last minute recruits from students, townies, and even opposing teams.

The students teams also showed an increased ability to throw strikes this year, as the Faculty did not garner anywhere near their usual quota of walks. Prof pitching also suffered during this first season without Hall of Fame hurler Bill Markley.

Jerry Brugh managed the team, since usual manager Mike Zwilling was on sabbatical.
