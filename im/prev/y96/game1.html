Faculty Intramural Softball 1996 Game Recap and Box Score

Rat Pack Slam Sinks Profs

The Faculty took an 18-13 lead into the final inning Tuesday, but ATO pitcher Bryan Sell crushed a huge grand slam home run over the 300 foot sign in dead center field to ignite an 8-run Rat Pack rally from which the Profs could not recover. The 21-20 loss evened the Faculty record at 1-1.

The Faculty threatened in the bottom of the 7th, scoring twice to pull within one run and loading the bases for Jim Thoma. But Thoma's hard liner was caught in center field to end the threat and the game.

The Faculty built their offense around 26 walks (no, not a record) and 9 hits, with Mike DeMatteis providing 5 of the 9 safeties. DeMatteis had a double and two triples, including a bases loaded 3-bagger that broke a 13-13 tie in the 5th. The D-Man also set a new team record by driving in 9 runs in a single game.

Hall of Fame hurler Bill Markley came out of retirement, teaching part time this semester, and promptly tied Len Epp's team record by garnering 6 walks in the game. Markley scored 5 times and added 2 rbi's.
