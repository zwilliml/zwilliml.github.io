Faculty Intramural Softball 1996 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Survive 19-18 Scare by Team 1

For the second straight day, the Faculty took a 5 run lead into the last inning, and again the opposition mounted a rally, but this time the Profs held on to beat Team 1 by a score of 19-18, raising the Prof record to 2-1.

The Preceptors used 13 hits and 19 walks to build the 19-14 cushion, scoring every inning, but strong Team 1 hitting and some Faculty defensive lapses combined to fritter away the comfortable margin. At last Mike DeMatteis and Gregg Nieschwitz stopped the onslaught by registering a force out at second to preserve the victory on the eve of MUC Sesquicentennial Day.

DeMatteis followed up his previous day's record 9 rbi's with 8 more this game, giving him more ribbies in the first two games than any other Faculty member has ever had in an entire season. Just to get his name in the record books again, he staged a magnificent 6-for-6 performance that included a 3-run double and a 2-run triple.

Jack Powers and Mike Zwilling each contributed a double and a single for the Profs, and Gerry Wuchter added two hits. Bill Markley garnered 4 walks and scored each time, also picking up the win.
