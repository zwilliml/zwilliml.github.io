Faculty Intramural Softball 1996 Game Recap and Box Score

Prof Bats Sizzle in 9-4 Romp

The Faculty ripped 16 hits, 6 of them for extra bases, as they won over the previously undefeated PHU's 9-4, elevating the Prof season record to 3-1.

The big win could have important playoff ramifications, since the victory over the now 4-1 Purple Headed Unit would presumably give the Faculty the nod by head-to-head competition in the event the two teams end the season with identical records.

The Preceptors were forced to eschew their favored offensive weapon, the walk, when the PHU pitcher exhibited her control by plunking the black strike mat 3 times, compeling the Profs to swing away. And swing away they did--swatting 10 hits in the 3rd and 4th innings, only 5 of them singles, in building a 7-1 lead. A 3-run PHU homer with 2 out in the bottom of the 7th only made the game appear tighter than it actually was.

Bill Davis and Jack Powers led the offensive barrage for the Profs with 4 hits each. Gerry Wuchter walloped a 2-run homer, the first Prof blast of the season, and also tripled. Len Epp and Mike DeMatteis each added 3 hits, with all 3 of the DeMatteis shots going for triples.
