Faculty Intramural Softball 1996 Game Recap and Box Score

Prof Comeback Stuns Rat Pack 20-17

It looked hopeless as the Faculty trailed mighty Rat Pack 17-8 with 2 outs in the final inning, but then the ATO roof caved in as the next 13 Profs reached safely, 10 of them on walks. Faculty skipper Mike Zwilling capped the rally with a 2-out, 2-run, bases loaded double as the one hour time limit was invoked to end the game. The win raised the Prof record to 4-1 and surely ranks as the greatest Faculty comeback of all time.

A few ATO's found the bitter pill hard to swallow as they have now watched the Faculty garner 50 walks in their 2 games this season. They may have their chance at revenge, since it now appears likely that these same two teams will have a third and final meeting in the Playoffs to determine the Division A Champion and send the winner on to the Big Show against the Division B Champs.

The 24 walks were not the only offense the Profs displayed, as they recorded 9 hits in their 21 at-bats and raised their collective team batting average to .427 for the season. Zwilling went 3-for-3 with 5 rbi's, and Steve Cederbloom was also perfect with 2 hits in 2 trips, adding 2 ribbies. Bill Markley also enjoyed a perfect 1-for-1 afternoon, and his last-inning walk forced in the go-ahead run.
