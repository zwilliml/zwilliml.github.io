Faculty Intramural Softball 1996 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Storm Into Playoffs 33-14

The Faculty Intramural Softball team clinched a spot in the playoffs for the first time since their 1992 Championship year by rolling to a 33-14 win over Team 1. The 33 runs was a team record, eclipsing the previous mark of 29 runs scored in a 1992 victory over Sigma Nu.

The Faculty now have the opportunity to set an all-time team best record for the regular season with a win over T & A's. Such a result would clinch a first place tie for the Profs in the regular season, their first ever (they finished 2nd in 1992 but won the playoffs).

The game Thursday was actually closer than the score would indicate, as the Faculty held onto a mere 17-14 lead going into the final frame. Team 1 had been busily conducting its own offense and the game was still in doubt. But a 16 run Prof explosion in the 6th (also a record) iced the game and paved the way for the 10-run mercy rule to be invoked. The gigantic inning saw all but two Profs bat 3 times each. Gregg Nieschwitz alone drew 3 walks in the inning, out of the 13 free passes distributed that frame (29 in the game).

The Faculty offense was more than mere base-on-balls: 16 hits in 31 at bats were recorded, including 5 hits for extra bases. Among those was Gerry Wuchter's grand slam home run in the 13-run 2nd inning. Wuchter had a record-tieing 9 rbi's on the day, and also picked up the win on the mound. Gerry pulled a hamstring on his homer and is questionable for the playoffs.

Truman Turnquist also had a day--3 hits, 3 walks, and 4 rbi's, including a line drive double down the 3rd base line. Andy Boothe also starred, walloping a solo home run and a double among his 5 hits, garnering 5 ribbies, and scoring 6 times.

Mike Zwilling went 3-for-4, scored 6 runs, and drove in 4. Greg Carter drew 6 walks and Tom Holtz 4 more. Every Prof in the line-up had at least 2 rbi's.
