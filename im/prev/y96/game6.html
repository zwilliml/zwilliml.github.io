Faculty Intramural Softball 1996 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Edge Rat Pack, Advance To Big Show!

The Faculty Intramural Softball team reached the Championship game for the third time in the last 7 years by surviving a 16-15 scare from Rat Pack. The Profs will face the Frozen Ropes for all the marbles. The Ropesters only loss this season was by forfeit.

The Faculty jumped all over the ATO's with 10 runs in the first inning, on 6 hits and 5 walks, including Mike Zwilling's bases loaded double. They stretched their lead to 15-1 in the third inning, prompting premature inquiries into the exact margin required to invoke the mercy rule.

But the perennial softball power Rat Pack was ignited from their slumber by a prodigious 2-run homer by pitcher Bryan Sell, directly over the 300 foot sign in dead center field, the same place his grand salami went that broke the Prof's backs in their only loss of the season. The ATO's made it 16-11 when darkness caused a suspension until October 16.

After the game resumed, the Greeks kept scoring, while holding down the Faculty offense, and had the tieing run on second when Jim Thoma hauled in a fly ball to end the threat and the game.

Mike DeMatteis had 3 hits for the Profs, and Gregg Nieschwitz added two more. Bill Markley slashed an rbi double down the right field line and added 2 more rbi's on his 3 walks. Other table-setters with 3 free passes were Thoma, Truman Turnquist, and Greg Carter.
