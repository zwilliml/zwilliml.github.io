Faculty Intramural Softball 1996 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Lose Finals to Frozen Ropes

The Faculty Softball team lost the Intramural Championship Game to the Frozen Ropes by a 17-7 score. The loss ended a fine season for the Profs, who were making their third appearance in the Big Show since 1990.

The Ropesters' strong offense opened the game with 5 runs, but the Faculty fought back. Gerry Wuchter's 3-run homer evened the score at 6-all in the second inning, making it a brand new ball game.

But the Frozen Ones' offense was not to be denied, piling on 7 more runs the next inning, mostly on the strength of their fine hitting. The Faculty defense, while not perfect, was not so poor as to be the difference in the game. The 10-run mercy rule was invoked after 5 innings.

The Preceptor offense managed only 4 hits, the big blow of course being Wuchter's blast. Mike DeMatteis, Bill Davis, and Jack Powers had the remaining 3 Prof safeties, and a respectable 12 walks were also drawn by the Faculty.
