Faculty Intramural Softball 1996 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-Offs

1996 Season Summary

The Faculty had one of their best seasons ever, finishing first in their division with a regular season record of 6-1, then winning the semifinal playoff game against Rat Pack before losing in the finals to Frozen Ropes.

It was the first time the Faculty had ever finished first in their division in the regular season, and it marked the second time the Profs lost in the finals (the other was in 1990).

The season featured three titanic contests against perennial softball power Rat Pack, with the Profs handing the ATO's their only two losses on the season.

On offense, the Profs were led by Mike DeMatteis with a batting average of .704, a slugging average of 1.222, 6 triples, and 24 rbi's. Close behind was Gerry Wuchter with 22 ribbies and 3 home runs, including a grand slam. Mike Zwilling led the team in runs scored, doubles, and times on base. Bill Markley drew a team-leading 19 walks, followed by Greg Carter's 17. Jack Powers and Bill Davis had fine years at the plate, batting .571 and .545 respectively.

The November 9, 1996, issue of The Dynamo contained the following article, presumably written by Sports Editor Erika Pribanic and 1996 Intramural Softball Commissioner Bill Eagle:

Intramural Softball

The intramural softball season has come to a close with the Faculty team (7-2) and the Frozen Ropes (8-1) placing first in their respective divisions, and Frozen Ropes earning the title of League Champions.

William Markley, professor emeritus of mathematics, led the Faculty with spectacular pitching. Division A runner-ups, the Rat Pack (6-2), also had a great season, including two out-of-the park home runs by team captain Bryan Sell. Phi Kappa Tau (6-2) was the runner-up in Division B.
