Faculty Intramural Softball 1997 Game Recap and Box Score

Zwilling's Clutch Single Lifts Profs

Score tied, bottom of the 7th, two outs, bases loaded--that's the kind of situation every batter wants to be in, and Mike Zwilling came through in the clutch with a sharp single up the middle to score Jim Thoma with the winning run in a stirring 16-15 victory over Nadz in the Faculty season opener.

The last inning started with Gerry Wuchter attempting the heroics, as he launched a drive into the gap in deep right center and proceeded to circle the bases. But a tremendous throw from the outfield and the huge Nadz catcher effectively blocking the plate resulted in Wuchter being tagged out at home as his head-first slide ended with his face against the catcher's leg.

Thoma then reached on a key Nadz shortstop error. After a pop-up for the 2nd out, Bill Markley and Jim Kadlecek drew walks to juice the sacks for Zwilling's blow.

The Profs got off to a shaky start in the game, allowing 5 runs in the top of the first and sending only 3 batters to the plate in the bottom of the frame. But the tide began to turn, especially with a 6-run outburst in the 4th that was highlighted by successive rbi singles from Jason Culbertson, Markley, and Kadlecek.

The Faculty offense featured 15 hits and 15 walks, and was led by Kadlecek's 4 hits and 5 ribbies. Markley was perfect on the day with a hit and 5 walks in 6 trips to the plate. Wuchter and Culbertson had 3 hits each, while Zwilling added a double to his game-winner.

Brandon Sheafor had 2 rbi's, 2 walks, and a sacrifice fly. Beth DeSantis drove home a run in the big 4th inning rally, and Thoma had 2 hits and scored 4 times

Brett Stalnaker had a big day for the Nadz, going 4-for-4 plus a walk. The Nadz may have suffered by failing to employ their famous rally cry during the game.
