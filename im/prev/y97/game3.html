Faculty Intramural Softball 1997 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Wilt in 17-13 Loss

The Faculty staged a 9-run rally in the second inning to build an 11-4 lead over the Phi Tau's, but the huge effort seemed to take its toll on the Profs as their offense wilted and the Greeks mounted ever increasing assaults in battling to a 17-13 come-from-behind victory, dropping the Faculty season record to 1-2.

The tide seemed to turn when the Phi Tau's replaced their starting pitcher at the end of the Faculty rally. The reliever walked only 1 and allowed just 2 Prof runs the rest of the way.

Meanwhile, the Greeks started hitting the gaps and tied the game in the 6th with a 6-run explosion, then put it away in the 7th with 4 more.

The Preceptor offense was led by Hall-of-Famer Bill Markley, who had 2 hits, 2 walks, and 3 rbi's. John Strefeler also starred with 3 hits, including a double, plus a walk and 2 runs scored. Greg Carter furnished 2 walks and a ringing 2-run double (the only 2 runs against the PKT reliever).

Beth DeSantis and Rich Dutson each drew a bases-loaded walk in the Prof big inning. Brian Martin slashed two singles, while Brandon Sheafor had a hit and a walk. Steve Cederbloom and Mike Zwilling each added a single.
