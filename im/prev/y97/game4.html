Faculty Intramural Softball 1997 Game Recap and Box Score

Frozen Ropes Dominate Faculty 20-1

The rematch of last year's softball playoff finalists was even more lopsided this year, as the champion Frozen Ropes scored every inning and decimated the Profs 20-1. The 10-run mercy rule was invoked in the 5th inning, preventing further Faculty embarrassment, and dropping their season record to 1-3.

The only moral victory for the Profs was that they avoided a shutout, with Jim Kadlecek's sacrifice fly in the final inning pushing across the lone run by Beth DeSantis, who had walked.

The Preceptors could manage only 4 hits and 6 walks, the scattered singles coming from Mike Zwilling, Jason Culbertson, Rich Dutson, and John Strefeler.

The Frozen Ropes certainly lived up to their name, supplying not only line drives but also towering shots in the gaps. Prof hurler Brandon Sheafor did a great job filling in for Hall-of-Famer Bill Markley, walking only one, and was not to blame for the Ropesters' fire power.
