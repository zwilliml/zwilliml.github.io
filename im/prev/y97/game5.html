Faculty Intramural Softball 1997 Game Recap and Box Score

Brew Crew Tops Faculty 16-8

The Faculty regular season ended with a 1-4 record as the Brew Crew's potent offense produced a steady stream of runs in their 16-8 defeat of the Profs.

The Brewer offense was just too much, as they hit several towering shots over the scrambling Faculty outfielders, scoring 4 runs in each of the first three innings.

The Faculty did have one offensive explosion in the 4th to briefly liven things up. The 8-run eruption made the score 12-8 and put a scare in the Brewers. The highlight of the inning was Gerry Wuchter's bases-loaded triple to the gap in right center. Six walks also figured into the scoring.

Brian Martin and John Strefeler had 2 singles each for the Profs. Getting one hit each were Jim Kadlecek, Rich Dutson, Brandon Sheafor, and Greg Carter. Michele Turner had a walk and an rbi, while Mike Zwilling garnered 4 free passes and Steve Cederbloom 2 more.
