Faculty Intramural Softball 1997 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Bow Out 19-18

Close Loss to Phi Taus Ends Playoff Hopes

The Faculty lost in the first round of the playoffs to PKT 19-18, and were eliminated, ending their season with a 1-5 record.

The Profs were trailing 16-5 going into the 4th inning, and it looked like the mercy rule might need to be invoked, but a 4-run rally followed by a 9-run eruption in the 5th put the Faculty in the lead 18-16.

But the Greeks pulled to within one run in the bottom of the 5th, and then stunned the Preceptors with a 2-run homer to deep left center in the 6th to take the lead.

Greg Carter gave the Profs a chance to tie it with his 7th inning double, but he was stranded on 2nd, and with him died the Faculty season.

Jim Thoma highlighted the big 5th inning with a 3-run homer, and Gerry Wuchter touched off a 2-run blast in the 4th. Wuchter also singled, doubled, and had 4 rbi's.

Jim Kadlacek had 3 hits for the Profs, and Brandon Sheafor drew 3 walks. Brian Martin tripled and scored to lead off the 4th, and John Strefeler whacked a double for the cause. Jason Culbertson, Rich Dutson, and Steve Cederbloom each added singles.
