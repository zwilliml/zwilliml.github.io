Faculty Intramural Softball 1997 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-Offs

1997 Season Summary

The Faculty was more competitive than their 1-5 record would indicate, losing badly in only one game - against the mighty Frozen Ropes, who were intramural champions once again.

The Profs lost a tough playoff game to Phi Tau's by one run, and lost a couple of slugfests. We missed the offensive power of Mike DeMatteis, who was unable to play this year.

Hall of Famer Bill Markley led the team in batting average this season at .750, and added 7 walks. Gerry Wuchter slugged a team-leading 1.364, including 2 triples and a homer. Rookie Jim Kadlacek batted .615 and drove in 9 runs to lead the Preceptors in that statistic.

Another rookie Brandon Sheafor did a great job taking over the pitching duties when Markley was unavailable, and he garnered 8 walks, the second most on the team. Mike Zwilling led that category with 11, and he provided the game-winning blow in the only Prof win of the season.

John Strefeler had a fine rookie season for the Faculty, playing third base and batting .467. Veterans Rich Dutson, Jim Thoma, and Steve Cederbloom provided stability and leadership on both offense and defense.

Beth DeSantis and Michele Turner had fine rookie seasons, and Brian Martin was among the team leaders in several offensive categories.

The October 24, 1997, issue of The Dynamo contained the following article:

Frozen Ropes earn softball championship

by Don Trefzger

The intramural softball team, Frozen Ropes, were champions for the fourth year in a row. The Frozen Ropes began four years ago and compiled an outstanding record of 39-0. (Zwilling's note: actually, they lost one game by forfeit.) The fourtime intramural softball champions consisted of 12 seniors and one junior.

Joe Buttari and Gene Harden are four year members on Frozen Ropes. "It's been fun every year," said Buttari. "We've had great sportsmanship and dedication during our championship seasons." Harden said, "I pretty much believe that the Frozen Ropes has become a dynasty team here at Mount Union."

A three year member, Jason Miller, gives credit to captain Buttari on his notorious effort in making the line-up for each game. There was a supply of two-year members on Frozen Ropes: Tim Banach, shortstop; Matt Ridgeway, right field; Jason Burkey, left field; Brad Jenson, junior catcher; and Don Trefzger, pitcher. "We went to each game early and took batting practice," said Banach. "That just shows our love for America's game!"

Tracy Sutherin, pitcher; Brian Sell, left center field; Scott Kitzmiller, right field/catcher; and Brett Bickar, first base/catcher were all first year players. "I was really happy to be part of the Frozen Ropes," said Bickar. "I was on the waiting list for a while, but it sure was worth it."

Out of eight games, Frozen Ropes put up seven run-ruled games (games won by ten or more runs after five innings). Frozen Ropes had a countless number of run-ruled games throughout four years. The Ropes even beat the faculty in the championship game last year.

A round of applause to the Frozen Ropes, who will retire a dynasty team here at Mount Union College.
