Faculty Intramural Softball 1998 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Edged in Season Opener

Lose to The Cooterbox 8-7

With two outs in the bottom of the seventh, the Faculty had the tying run on third, the winning run on first, and Mike Zwilling at the plate. Zwilling launched a drive for the left centerfield gap that seemed destined to win the game, but the Cooterbox centerfielder, a baseball player, made a spectacular diving catch to rob Zwilling and end the Faculty threat.

The Purple Graders had fought back from a 7-2 deficit with an offense of 8 singles and 14 walks, led by Allison Roberts who was a perfect 2-for-2 plus 2 walks. Gerry Wuchter had a hit, a walk, and a sacrifice fly, while Judy Maendel had a hit and 2 walks.

Cara Grisola had 2 walks and a sac fly for the Preceptors, and Jim Phillips, Zwilling, Miracle Conrad, and Bill Markley each had a single and a walk. Dawn Slavens, Jon Scott, and Gabriel King also drew free passes for the faculty.
