Faculty Intramural Softball 1998 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Bow to 1648

Ousted from Playoffs in 9-5 Semifinal Loss

The Faculty ended the 1998 season with a well-played but losing effort against team 1648, also known as Frozen Ropes II.

The Purple Grader offense sputtered a bit as the walks were there but the hits to drive them home were not. The Profs could manage only 5 hits while stranding 16 runners.

The Preceptors fell behind early and did not seriously threaten. A 3-run 5th inning kept the score respectable.

Gerry Wuchter had a double plus 2 walks for the Faculty, while Miracle Conrad and Mike Zwilling each had a single and a walk. Rich Dutson singled and hit a sacrifice fly, while Dawn Slavens stroked the remaining faculty hit.

Frank Tascone and Brandon Sheafor each drew 3 walks, while Allison Roberts got 2 free passes. Judy Maendel, Bill Markley, and Mike DeMatteis each had a bb.
