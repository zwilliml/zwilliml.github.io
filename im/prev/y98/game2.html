Faculty Intramural Softball 1998 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Salvage 14-14 Tie

Faculty and Nadz in First Ever Tie

The Faculty fell behind 7-0 in the top of the first, but stormed back with two big 5-run innings, and had a shot to win before settling for a hard-fought 14-14 tie against the Nadz. The one hour time limit forced the first-ever tie in Faculty history.

The Purple Graders had tied the score in the bottom of the 6th and had the bases loaded with two outs and Mike Zwilling up, but Zwilling flied out deep to left center to end the threat. Gerry Wuchter led off the Prof seventh with an even longer drive, but the Nadz left-center fielder made a fine running grab to save the game.

Wuchter led the Faculty with a grand slam home run in the second inning, a single, two walks, 4 rbi's, and 4 runs scored. Mike DeMatteis had 3 hits for the Profs, while Allison Roberts had 2 hits and a walk.

Bill Markley, Zwilling, and Jim Phillips each had a hit and 2 walks for the Faculty. Phillips pulled off a nifty unassisted double play to get the Profs out of a bases-loaded one-out jam in the 5th -- fielding a grounder, stepping on third for the force, and tagging out the runner straying from third.

Frank Tascone had 3 walks for the Preceptors, as did Miracle Conrad. Brandon Sheafor had a hit, a free pass, and a sac fly. Jon Scott singled, and Judy Maendel drew a bases-loaded walk.
