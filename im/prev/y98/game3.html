Faculty Intramural Softball 1998 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Edge Shockers 10-9

8-Run Inning Lifts Faculty

The Faculty trailed 4-2 late in what had been a close game, then exploded for 8 runs in the 6th inning and staved off the charging Shockers to register their first victory of the season by a 10-9 margin. The win evened the Prof record at 1-1-1.

Mike DeMatteis ignited the big inning with a 3-run homer down the right field line. The eagle-eyed Purple Graders then drew 5 walks, forcing home 2 more runs. Mike Zwilling capped the scoring with a bases loaded triple over the center fielders head.

But the Shockers came back with 4 runs in their half of the 6th, then tallied another in the bottom of the 7th with a lead off homer, but then meekly went 3-up, 3-down, to end the game.

Zwilling was perfect with 3 hits and 2 walks to lead the Preceptors. DeMatteis added a single to his blast.

Allison Roberts, Frank Tascone, Judy Maendel, and John Recchiuti each had a single for the Profs, with Roberts and Tascone each adding 3 walks.

Rich Dutson, Jim Phillips, Dawn Slavens, and Miracle Conrad each garnered 2 free passes.
