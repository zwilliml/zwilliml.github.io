Faculty Intramural Softball 1998 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Humble All MUC 19-5

10-Run First Inning Demoralizes Students

The Faculty used 8 walks in the first 9 batters to build a 10-run inning in the top of the first, then coasted to an easy 19-5 victory over All MUC. The win raised the Prof season record to 2-1-1.

The Purple Graders supplemented their patented 17-walk offense with 12 hits, 4 for extra bases. Because of the walks, there were usually runners aboard when the big blows were struck.

Mike Zwilling and Rich Dutson led the Faculty with 3 hits each. Dutson included a double, while Zwilling tripled over the left fielder's head. Zwilling also pulled off the second Prof unassisted double play of the season in the first as he fielded a grounder, tagged a runner, and stepped on second for the force.

Gerry Wuchter and Allison Roberts each had 2 hits. One of Wuchter's was a triple, while Roberts had a double.

Brandon Sheafor had a hit and 3 walks, while Bill Markley had 4 walks. Miracle Conrad drew 3 free passes, each with the bases loaded.

Jon Scott had a hit and a walk, and Judy Maendel drew a walk. Every Prof had at least one rbi.
