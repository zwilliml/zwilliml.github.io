Faculty Intramural Softball 1998 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Avenge Only Loss With 11-6 Win

Victory over Cooterbox is Third Straight

The Faculty used strong hitting and a smothering defense to subdue the Cooterbox 11-6, avenging a season-opening one-run loss. The Profs now own a 3-game winning streak, and a 4-game unbeaten streak.

Miracle Conrad was declared Star of the Game after making several dazzling defensive plays, including an astonishing over-the-shoulder back-to-the-infield running grab of a pop-up behind second base that squelched a Cooterbox rally.

Mike Zwilling anchored the Purple Grader offense, crushing a second inning three-run homer to start the scoring, and adding a triple and a single to total 5 rbi's.

Mike DeMatteis slashed 4 Preceptor singles, and Aaron Swartz cracked a double and a triple.

Steve Kramer last touched a bat on September 24, 1996, but two years later, with no warm-up, he proved his swing was as sharp as ever by slashing a single down the right field line.

Allison Roberts, Brandon Sheafor, and Gerry Wuchter also had singles, while Judy Maendel and Miracle Conrad each drew a walk.
