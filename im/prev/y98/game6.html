Faculty Intramural Softball 1998 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Buried by Nadz 22-2

No Contest in Tiebreaker

The Nadz hammered the Faculty 22-2, handing the Faculty their second loss of the season and breaking a Prof 4-game unbeaten streak that began with a 14-14 tie against these same Nadz.

This time around the Nadz were in complete control, scoring 15 runs in the bottom of the first to doom the Purple Graders.

The Profs knew about the 10-run rule after 5 innings, but today they discovered a seldom-used rule: if you are behind by 20 runs, you only get to play 4 innings.

The Preceptors could manage only 2 singles, by Dawn Slavens and Gerry Wuchter. Eight walks supplemented the meager Prof offense, two each from Judy Maendel and Bill Markley.
