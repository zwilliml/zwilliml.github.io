Faculty Intramural Softball 1998 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Burn Coronas 16-5

Win by 10-Run Rule

The Faculty steadily built a strong lead and were never headed by the Coronas in a win that brought the Prof record to 4-2-1 and guaranteed a regular season record at least at the .500 mark.

It all came together against the last place Coronas - hitting, walks, pitching, and defense. The Purple Graders muscled up for 13 hits and added 18 walks, both high-water marks for the season.

Mike DeMatteis led the Profs with 4 hits, including a double, and 2 rbi's. Brandon Sheafor had a day with 3 hits and 4 ribbies. Gerry Wuchter slashed a 3-run homer and added 2 singles.

Allison Roberts stroked 2 hits, while Miracle Conrad was perfect on the day with a hit plus 3 walks. Rich Dutson had 3 free passes, while five more players garnered 2 walks each.
