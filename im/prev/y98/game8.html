Faculty Intramural Softball 1998 Game Recap and Box Score

Shockers Avenge Themselves on Profs 24-5

More Than Make Up For Earlier Loss

The first-place Shockers avenged their only loss of the season, a 10-9 squeaker at the Hands of the Faculty on September 17, by drubbing the hapless Profs 24-5. The loss left the Faculty record at 4-3-1, with one game left in the regular season.

The Purple Graders had just used a 5-run rally to even the score at 5-5 in the 2nd inning, when the Shockers exploded for 17 runs in the third. The Profs had to struggle to avoid losing by the 20-run rule again.

The Preceptors could manage only 4 hits and 8 walks. Half the hits came from Mike DeMatteis, with Gerry Wuchter and Mike Zwilling providing the other singles. Judy Maendel and Allison Roberts added 2 walks each.
