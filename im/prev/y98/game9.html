Faculty Intramural Softball 1998 Game Recap and Box Score

Profs Handle Phi Tau's 18-10

Advance to Semifinals in Playoffs

The Faculty avenged last year's playoff loss to the Phi Tau's by trouncing the Greeks 18-10 to advance to the semifinals of the intramural softball playoffs.

The Purple Graders built a 16-3 lead by the 5th inning and there was premature talk of a 10-run rule win. But the Phi Tau's had a 6-run outburst that put them back in the game, and the Faculty was forced to go the distance to subdue the unruly Greeks.

The Profs scored in every inning as their patented offense was working to perfection - they drew 19 walks and let the big hitters drive them home.

And those big hitters were Gerry Wuchter and Mike DeMatteis. Wuchter hit better than the cycle (a super-cycle?) - a double, 2 triples, and a 3-run homer, not to mention 4 rbi's. DeMatteis was a perfect 5-for-5, including a double, a triple, and 5 ribbies.

Frank Tascone had a hit plus 3 walks, as did Bill Markley. Allison Roberts and Mike Zwilling each had a hit plus 2 free passes. Zwilling's hit was an rbi double in the gap.

Dawn Slavens drew 3 walks, while Judy Maendel and Rich Dutson got 2 bb's each. Miracle Conrad had a single plus a walk, and Brandon Sheafor had a walk and got robbed of hits on 3 line drives.
