Faculty Intramural Softball 1998 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-Offs

1998 Season Summary

The Faculty had a mixed season in 1998. Their 6-4-1 record featured a mid-season 3-game winning streak and 4-game unbeaten streak that included their first-ever tie, a playoff quarter-final victory over the Phi Tau's, and a place in the playoff semi-finals.
