Mount Union College

Faculty Intramural Softball 1999 Game Recap and Box Score

Shockers Shock Faculty 24-14

13-Run Inning Dooms Profs

The Shockers, last year's intramural softball champions, lived up to their name by unleashing an offensive explosion against the Faculty, scoring 13 runs in the 6th inning and turning a 14-11 Prof lead into a 24-14 laugher that had to be called by the 10-run mercy rule.

The Purple Graders seemed to be coasting along nicely going into the bottom of the 6th, as their patented offense had generated 14 runs on just 9 hits supplemented with 19 walks, while holding the Shockers to 11 runs on 14 hits. But the champs sent 16 men to the plate in the 6th, scoring 13 runs on 11 hits and 3 walks to end the game.

The Shockers totaled 25 hits, including 3 doubles, 6 triples, and 3 homers, giving the Prof outfield some much needed exercise in the process.

Rich Dutson led the Faculty with a perfect day - 2 hits and 3 walks in 5 appearances. Gerry Wuchter and Brandon Sheafor also had 2 hits each, with Wuchter supplying the only extra-base hit for the Preceptors - a triple.

Jen Johnston had 4 walks and 3 rbi's for the Profs, while Judy Maendel added 3 bb's and 2 ribbies. Frank Tascone, Allison Roberts, and Mike Zwilling each had a single. Doug Pode drew 3 walks and struck out 6 Shockers while on the mound for the Faculty.

Prof Notes: To save people from scurrying to the record books, the 13-run Shocker inning is not close to the worst Faculty inning in history. That occurred 10 years ago, on October 5, 1989, when Basketball House pummeled the Profs with an incredible 21 runs in the second inning and coasted to a 24-12 victory. Rich Dutson and Mike Zwilling played in that game as well as this one.
