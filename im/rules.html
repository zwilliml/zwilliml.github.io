Mount Union College

Intramural Softball Rules

  1. All of the games will be held at Silver Park. Each team will be responsible for their own transportation to and from the park.
  2. Umpires will be provided.
  3. Each team must have at least nine players to start a game. If a team doesn't have nine players the game will be a forfeit.
  4. A forfeit is declared if a team doesn't have nine players by 10 minutes after the scheduled start.
  5. No stealing or leading off.
  6. All pitches must have a 6 to 12 foot arc. If the ball hits the black rubber mat behind the plate it is considered a strike and the batter is out.
  7. A ten run rule is in effect after the 5th inning begins. A twenty run rule is in effect after the 4th inning begins.
  8. All scores and judgements are final.
  9. No metal cleats.
  10. One pitch per batter: a ball is a walk, a strike or foul is an out.
  11. Games are 7 innings long or one hour (whichever comes first).
  12. No designated hitters.
  13. Teams may have men and women but no more than three varsity or junior varsity players per team.
  14. Only roster players can play on your team.
  15. ANY PLAYER ARGUING WITH AN UMPIRE OR USING OBSCENE LANGUAGE TOWARD THE UMPIRE WILL BE EJECTED FROM THE GAME AND POSSIBLY THE REST OF THE SEASON.
