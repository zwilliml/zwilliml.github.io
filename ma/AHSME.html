University of Mount Union

The Mount Union AMC12 Scholarship

$1,500, renewable for four years, independent of need. Moreover, the recipients may qualify for additional academic scholarship funds.

Eligibility: Student must be a high school senior who:

  • scored among the top 200 seniors in Ohio on the American Mathematics Contest 12;
  • applied and was accepted for admission to Mount Union no later than May 15; and
  • enrolled as a full-time undergraduate student at Mount Union for the Fall term.
This scholarship will be awarded to one or more students selected by Mount Union from all students who meet the eligibility requirements listed above.

For more information, contact Professor Michael Zwilling, Chair, Dept. of Mathematics, University of Mount Union, Alliance, OH 44601.
