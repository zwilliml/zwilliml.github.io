Math Problem Answer

The answer is 250 pounds!

The original 500 pounds consist of 495 pounds of water and 5 pounds of solids. If we let x be the weight of the cucumbers after evaporation, then 2% of x must be the 5 pounds of solids, or .02x = 5, from which we get x = 250 pounds.
