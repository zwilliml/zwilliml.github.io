University of Mount Union

Advanced Placement in Mathematics

Calculus I

Students who earn a score of 4 or 5 on the Advanced Placement Calculus AB Exam receive credit for MTH 141 Calculus I.

Calculus II

Students who earn a score of 4 or 5 on the Advanced Placement Calculus BC Exam receive credit for both MTH 141 Calculus I and MTH 142 Calculus II. Students who take the BC test will receive an AB subscore. Students who don't get the 4 or 5 on the BC test, but do receive a 4 or 5 on the AB subscore, will receive credit for MTH 141 but not MTH 142.

Statistics

Students who earn a score of 4 or 5 on the Advanced Placement Statistics Exam receive credit for MTH 123 Elementary Statistics.
