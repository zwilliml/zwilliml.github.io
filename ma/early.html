Mathematics Requirement for Early Childhood Teachers

Students who intend to become Early Childhood Teachers (preschool through grade 3, ages 3 - 8) must pass MA 101 Mathematics for Early Childhood Teachers, and must pass MA 110 or higher with a course grade no lower than C-.

These students should consult their advisors and the Education Department for additional requirements.
