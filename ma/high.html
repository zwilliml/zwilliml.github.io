Mathematics Requirements for High School Mathematics Teachers

Students who intend to become High School Mathematics Teachers (grades 7 - 12, ages 9 - 21) must major in mathematics with the mathematics education track, and must minor in Adolescence to Young Adult Education. Additional coursework is necessary for licensure.

These students should consult their advisors and the Education Department for required education courses and additional requirements.
