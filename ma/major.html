Mount Union College

Major in Mathematics - Prior to Fall, 1997

The completion of nine courses in mathematics is required of all majors. For students not preparing for secondary teaching, these nine courses must include MA 141, 142, 241, 322, 411, 441, and three electives chosen from mathematics courses (other than MA 395) numbered 335 or higher. For students preparing for secondary teaching, these nine courses must include MA 141, 142, 241, 322, 400, 411, 441, and two electives chosen from mathematics courses (other than MA 395) numbered 335 or higher.

Students interested in earning a teaching certificate in mathematics must take MA 395. These students should consult their advisors and the Guide to Teacher Certification Programs of the Education Department for specific course requirements.

A senior culminating experience is required of all students majoring in mathematics. This requirement may be met by satisfactory completion of either MA 406 or 442. For those students preparing for secondary teaching, the senior culminating experience requirement may be met by satisfactory completion of MA 400.
