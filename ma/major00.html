Mount Union College

Major in Mathematics - Effective Fall, 2000

Passing ten courses in mathematics is required of all majors. These ten courses must include

  1. MA 141 Calculus I or MA 151 Calculus for Biology
  2. MA 142 Calculus II
  3. MA 241 Calculus III
  4. MA 301 Introduction to Advanced Mathematics
  5. MA 322 Linear Algebra
  6. MA 411 Abstract Algebra I
  7. MA 441 Advanced Calculus I
  8. one course chosen from
    • MA 335 Differential Equations
    • MA 345 Methods of Mathematical Physics
    • MA 351 Numerical Analysis
    • MA 362 Discrete Mathematics
    • MA 405 Mathematical Statistics I
  9. one course chosen from
    • MA 440 Complex Analysis
    • MA 470 Introduction to Topology
    • MA 399 Special Topics, taken for 3 credit hours
  10. one Senior Culminating Experience course chosen from
    • MA 400 College Geometry - 4 semester hours (required for Secondary Education students)
    • MA 406 Mathematical Statistics II - 4 semester hours
    • MA 412 Abstract Algebra II - 4 semester hours
    • MA 442 Advanced Calculus II - 4 semester hours

Students interested in teaching high school mathematics must pass MA 395 in place of AE 335. These students should consult their advisors and the Guide to Teacher Certification Programs of the Education Department for specific course requirements.
