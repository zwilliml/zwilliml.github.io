Mount Union College

Major in Mathematics - Effective Fall, 1997

The completion of ten courses in mathematics is required of all majors. These ten courses must include

  1. MA 141 Calculus I
  2. MA 142 Calculus II
  3. MA 241 Calculus III
  4. MA 301 Introduction to Advanced Mathematics
  5. MA 322 Linear Algebra
  6. MA 411 Abstract Algebra I
  7. MA 441 Advanced Calculus I
  8. one course chosen from
    • MA 335 Differential Equations
    • MA 351 Numerical Analysis
    • MA 362 Discrete Mathematics
    • MA 405 Mathematical Statistics I
    • MA 408 Methods of Mathematical Physics
  9. one course chosen from
    • MA 399 Special Topics
    • MA 440 Complex Analysis
    • MA 470 Introduction to Topology
  10. one Senior Culminating Experience course chosen from
    • MA 400 College Geometry (required for Secondary Education students)
    • MA 406 Mathematical Statistics II
    • MA 412 Abstract Algebra II
    • MA 442 Advanced Calculus II

Students interested in teaching high school mathematics must take MA 395. These students should consult their advisors and the Guide to Teacher Certification Programs of the Education Department for specific course requirements.
