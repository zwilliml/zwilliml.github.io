University of Mount Union

Mathematics Club
"To Promote an Interest in and an Awareness of Mathematics"

Constitution of Mathematics Club

Revised November 20, 2000
Article I - Name
The name of this organization shall be the Mathematics Club of Mount Union College.
Article II - Purpose
The purpose of this organization is to meet the needs of students who hold an interest in the mathematical sciences, and to promote an interest and an awareness of mathematics throughout the college community.
Article III - Membership
Membership in this organization will be open to Mount Union College students who are currently enrolled in or have previously received credit for a math course.
Article IV - Officers
A. General Information:
Officers will consist of a President, Vice-President, Secretary, and Treasurer. Officers will be elected during spring term and shall hold office for one year or until released from duty by their successors. Specific election dates will be announced one week in advance. Duties will be those normally connected with the respective offices.
B. Procedure:
Only students holding membership in the Mathematics Club will be permitted to vote, nominate officers, or hold office. Officers will be nominated from the floor, and the nominee receiving the greatest number of votes shall accede to that office. Nominations and voting for President will occur first, immediately followed by nominations and voting for Vice-President, Secretary and Treasurer, respectively.
Article V - Advisor
The advisor for this organization shall be appointed by the President of Mount Union College on recommendation of the organization. The chosen faculty sponsor must be a member of the Mathematics Department of Mount Union College, and shall hold the position for the entire year.
Article VI - Meetings
Meetings shall be held at regular intervals as determined by the current membership. The advisor or any officer may call a special meeting as deemed necessary. Notices concerning time and location of meetings will be posted in currently functioning math classes and other appropriate locations.
Article VII - Activities
Activities and services of the Mathematics Club shall include the following:
  1. Sponsoring a math tutoring service and offering qualified students the opportunity to obtain tutoring jobs.
  2. Providing information pertaining to math conferences and organizing student groups to attend such conferences.
  3. Providing information pertaining to competitive mathematics examinations, and helping to prepare students for such exams.
  4. Engaging speakers and organizing lectures for the purpose of presenting mathematical material and sharing mathematical ideas.
  5. Organizing social gatherings for the members of the organization and other interested individuals, for the purpose of fostering spirit throughout the mathematical community.
  6. Any activity pertinent to the betterment of the mathematical community of Mount Union College.
Article VIII - Dues
Annual dues will be decided upon as seen fit to meet the needs of the organization.
Article IX - Amendments
  1. In order to amend the constitution, a written notice of the proposed amendment shall be circulated to the membership at least two weeks prior to the meeting at which the amendment will be voted upon.
  2. The amendment to the constitution shall be adopted by a two-thirds vote of the members present at this meeting.
  3. Amendments will become effective upon approval by the Committee on Campus Life
Article X - Ratification
This constitution shall be in effect upon ratification by two-thirds of the voting members present at the ratification session, and by approval of the Committee on Campus Life and the faculty of Mount Union College.
Article XI - Mount Union College Rules and Regulations
This organization shall adhere to the Mount Union College Student Handbook and all other laws, rules and regulations governing Mount Union College, its organizations and its students.
Article XII - Student Activities Clause
Math Club shall maintain a current registration form on file with the Student Activities Office including a list of officers, their addresses and phone numbers, the name of the Advisor, and the most recently amended constitution.
