Math Club Meeting - Thursday, January 18, 2001

Math Club's first meeting of the semseter was Thursday, January 18, 6:00, in room T-H 100. Dr. Ritchey showed us some fun math games and puzzles, and Dr. Brugh brought chips and drinks. This was a lot of fun and a good chance to relax before the semester really got going. Hope you were there!

Math Club Meeting - Tuesday, November 7, 2000

We worked on some old Putnam Exam problems. So If you are going to take the Putnam this year, would like to know what it is like, or would just like to try some challenging math problems, come to this meeting. (TH 100)

Math Club Movie Night - Tuesday, October 3, 2000

We showed "Good Will Hunting" Tuesday, October 3 at 7pm in TH 100. It was a lot of fun and a chance to relax a little bit. Everyone was welcome!

First Math Club Meeting Held - Thursday, September 14, 2000

Math Club had its first meeting Thursday, (Sept. 14) at 6:15 in the Coffee House. We discussed ideas for Homecoming, including the parade, as well as plans for the coming year.
