"Proof" Playing in Akron

Math Club asked for students interested in going to see the play "Proof", described below:

Weathervane Community Playhouse in Akron, Ohio, will be presenting a play that may be of interest to you and your students. "Proof," a play by David Auburn, won the Pulitzer Prize and Tony Award for Best Play in 2001. Heather Snell, Ph.D., has been cast in the role of Catherine. Heather is an Assistant Professor of Mathematics at Cuyahoga Community College and holds a Ph.D. in Knot Theory. Heather has been seen in several productions at Coach House Theatre in Akron and has won Royal Coach Awards for her performances in "The Constant Wife," "The Lion in Winter" and "Educating Rita."

Playwright, David Auburn, is a grandson of the late Dr. Norman P. Auburn, former president of The University of Akron, and son of Drs. Mark and Sandy Auburn. Mark is Dean of Fine and Applied Arts at the University and Sandy is with the Greater Akron Chamber of Commerce.

Weathervane Playhouse is pleased and privileged to present the Akron premiere of "Proof." The plot: On the eve of her twenty-fifth birthday, Catherine, a troubled woman, has spent years caring for her brilliant but unstable father, a famous mathematician. Now, following his death, she must deal with her own volatile emotions: the arrival of her estranged sister, Claire; and the attentions of Hal, a former student of her father's who hopes to find valuable work in the 103 notebooks that her father left behind. Over the long weekend that follows, a burgeoning romance and the discovery of a mysterious notebook draw Catherine into the most difficult problem of all: How much of her father's illness - or genius - has she inherited?

The play runs October 15 - November 2 and includes two low-cost previews ($14) on Wednesday and Thursday, October 15 & 16 at 7:30 p.m. Our special "Opening Night" performance is Friday, October 17 and includes a reception with light refreshments and an informal meet and greet with the cast following the performance. Regular performances ($18) continue Thursdays at 7:30 p.m., Fridays and Saturdays at 8:00 p.m. and Sundays at 2:30 p.m. Of special interest to students with a valid ID, is our offer of "Student Rush Tickets." These are half-price tickets ($9) that are made available one half hour before a performance that includes any seating available at that time. Student Rush Tickets cannot be ordered in advance. Weathervane also offers a student discount ($2 off) regular-priced tickets on Thursdays and Sundays. Group rates (for 15 or more people) are also available. Tickets (with the exception of Student Rush) can be purchased with Visa, MasterCard or Discover at 330-836-2626. Box Office hours are Tuesday through Friday from 10:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.
