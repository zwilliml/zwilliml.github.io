Advisor: Dr. Michael Zwilling
"Statistics In Germany" was the topic Thursday, September 24, 1992, as Dr. Michael Zwilling reported on his trip this summer to Munich, Germany, to attend the 7th International Workshop on Statistical Modeling. The meeting was held in room T&H 102 at 7:30 p.m. and was followed by a mathematics textbook give-away. Free refreshments were also available.

There was a Math Club meeting Tuesday evening, April 27, 1993, at 7:30 p.m. in room T&H 100. Amy Hahn talked on "Non-Euclidean Geometry". There was also election of officers, and free refreshments.

There was no Math Club picture in the 1993 Unonian.
