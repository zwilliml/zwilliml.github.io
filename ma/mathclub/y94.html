Our most recent meeting was September 6, 1994. Dr. Michael Zwilling spoke on "Predicting the Length of a Final Exam". His talk was followed by a mathematics book give-away.

John Nedel presented a talk on "Numerical Approximation of Eigenvalues using Sturm Sequences" on March 14, 1995. Nedel is a 1987 graduate of MUC in mathematics. He is currently a graduate student in mathematics at Indiana.

Daniel Solow presented a talk on "A Systematic Approach to Developing Algorithms in Operations Research" on March 22, 1995. Dr. Solow is a professor of Operations Research at Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland.
