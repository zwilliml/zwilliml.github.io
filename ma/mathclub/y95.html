Meeting Held April 11

Math Club met Thursday, April 11, 1996, at 8:30 PM in room 100 of Tolerton & Hood Hall. The following activities took place:
  • Dr. Michael Zwilling gave a mathematics talk called "How to Vacuum the House".
  • Free mathematics textbooks were given away (Dr. Zwilling has more if anyone wants them).
  • A photo of Math Club was taken for the Unonian.
  • Free refreshments!
  • Election of officers for next year were held:
    • President: Stacey Thompson
    • Vice-President: Carrie Hennon
    • Secretary: Tom Sulhan
    • Treasurer: Alyssa Dillow
