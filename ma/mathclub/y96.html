Textbook Giveaway

A Math Club meeting was held Monday, March 3, 1997, at 6:00 PM in room 100 of Tolerton & Hood Hall. There was a math textbook give-away, Unonian picture, election of officers, and free refreshments. The 1997-1998 officers-elect are
  • President: Miracle Conrad
  • Vice-President: Carrie Hennon
  • Secretary: Duane Farnsworth
  • Treasurer: Ana Matkovic

Ken Luther Speaks

The first Math Club meeting of the year was Sunday, September 29, 1996, at 6:00 PM in room 100 of Tolerton & Hood Hall. The featured speaker was Ken Luther, a 1988 MUC graduate in mathematics.

Ken is now a Ph.D. student in Environmental Science at Indiana University. His talk was on "Modeling Groundwater Flow Using the Analytic Element Method".
