Mathematics Club Meeting - Tuesday, April 27, 1999

The Mathematics Club met Tuesday, April 27, 1999, at 7:00 p.m. in room 100, Tolerton and Hood Hall. The yearbook picture was taken, and members worked on brain teasers.

Mathematics Club Meeting - Monday, March 11, 1999

The Mathematics Club met Monday, March 1, 1999, at 6:30 p.m. in room 100, Tolerton and Hood Hall. A video called "Making a Fuss Over Fractals" was viewed, followed by a brief discussion.

First Mathematics Club Meeting - November 16, 1998

The Mathematics Club will hold its first meeting Monday, November 16, 1998, at 6:30 p.m. in room 100, Tolerton and Hood Hall. Dr. Michael Zwilling, Chairman of the Mathematics Department at Mount Union, will talk on "Connect the Dots: From Handshakes to Lunar Colonies".
