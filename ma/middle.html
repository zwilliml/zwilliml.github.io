Mathematics Requirement for Middle Childhood Teachers

Students who intend to become Middle Childhood Teachers (grades 4 - 9, ages 9 - 14) with an emphasis in mathematics must pass the following five (5) mathematics courses:

  1. MTH 123 Elementary Statistics
  2. MTH 141 Calculus I
  3. MTH 142 Calculus II
  4. MTH 300 College Geometry
  5. MTH 362 Discrete Mathematics

These students should consult their advisors and the Education Department for additional requirements.
