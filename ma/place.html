Mathematics Placement

If a student has college credit for math courses (such as from AP or CCP or transfer credit), then those courses are used for math placement.

Students who do not have college credit for a math course will be placed by their ACT math or SAT math section scores. Students who have both ACT math and SAT math section scores will be placed by whichever score places the student in higher level math courses.

Students who have neither college credit for math nor ACT math nor SAT math section scores, will take a math placement test. Accuplacer test scores are acceptable but Mount Union will administer only its own math placement tests.

There are two math placement tests:
a) Test 1: Calculus Readiness Test;
b) Test 2: Precalculus Readiness Test.

Students may choose to take either one but not both. Students who might take calculus, or who want to attempt to satisfy the Mount Union math proficiency requirement, should take the Calculus Readiness Test. Otherwise, they should take the Precalculus Readiness Test. For both math placement tests, there is a 90-minute time limit, and no calculators are allowed.

Students who are not satisfied with their math placement level by ACT/SAT may take an exam one time, keeping whichever result places the student in higher level math courses. The test must be taken before the end of their first semester at Mount Union, and may not be taken after the student has attended one day of any math class.

Students with no ACT/SAT who are not satisfied with their math placement level from their first attempt can re-take the same test once (not a different test), with a 14-day waiting period, keeping whichever result places the student in higher level math courses. The test must be taken before the end of their first semester at Mount Union.

The following course sequences will be required to meet the mathematics proficiency component of general education for levels A, B, C, and D. Students placed into Level E will already have met the mathematics proficiency requirement.

  1. Level A Students: Must complete MTH 119 (Quantitative Literacy); OR MTH 100 (Intermediate Algebra) and then one of the following options:
    1. MTH 105 (College Algebra) and then MTH 140 (Precalculus), or
    2. MTH 105 (College Algebra) and then MTH 123 (Elementary Statistics), or
    3. MTH 105 (College Algebra) and then MTH 125 (Elementary Discrete Math).

  2. Level B Students: Must complete one of the following options:
    1. MTH 119 (Quantitative Literacy), or
    2. MTH 105 (College Algebra) and then MTH 140 (Precalculus), or
    3. MTH 105 (College Algebra) and then MTH 123 (Elementary Statistics), or
    4. MTH 105 (College Algebra) and then MTH 125 (Elementary Discrete Math).

  3. Level C Students: Must complete one of the following options:
    1. MTH 119 (Quantitative Literacy), or
    2. MTH 123 (Elementary Statistics)
    3. MTH 125 (Elementary Discrete Math)
    4. MTH 140 (Precalculus)

  4. Level D Students: Must complete one of the following options:
    1. MTH 119 (Quantitative Literacy), or
    2. MTH 123 (Elementary Statistics)
    3. MTH 125 (Elementary Discrete Math)
    4. MTH 140 (Precalculus)
    5. MTH 130 (Calculus Lab) and MTH 141 (Calculus I), taken concurrently.

  5. Level E Students: Mathematics Proficiency Requirement completed. If further mathematics courses are required or desired, then any of the following courses may be taken:
    • MTH 123 (Elementary Statistics)
    • MTH 125 (Elementary Discrete Math)
    • MTH 140 (Precalculus)
    • MTH 141 (Calculus I)
    • MTH 142* (Calculus II)
    • MTH 241* (Calculus III)
*Students who already have earned credit for Calculus I and/or Calculus II only, or permission of the Department of Mathematics.
