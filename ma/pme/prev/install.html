Mount Union College

Pi Mu Epsilon Installation 1983

Advisor: Dr. James Dillon
The installation of the Ohio Omicron Chapter of Pi Mu Epsilon at Mount Union College took place November 18, 1983 in the Presidential Dining Room.

Dr. William A. Markley, Jr., Professor and Chairman of the Mathematics Department at Mount Union College, gave the welcoming remarks.

Dr. Milton D. Cox, Professor of Mathematics at Miami University of Ohio and the President-Elect of national Pi Mu Epsilon, gave the address, conducted the installation and initiation, and presented the charter.

Dr. John F. Fleischauer, Dean of Mount Union College, accepted the charter.
