Initiation Ceremony Held

The 1996 Pi Mu Epsilon initiation ceremony was held on Monday, April 22, at 6:30 PM in the Private Dining Room.

Dr. Don Ray, Professor of Psychology at Mount Union College, gave the address. Entitled "Student Likes and Dislikes", Dr. Ray explained the use of factor analysis in the 1995 student survey.
