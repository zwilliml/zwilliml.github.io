Initiation Ceremony Held

The 1997 Pi Mu Epsilon initiation ceremony was held on Monday, April 21, at 7:00 PM in the Private Dining Room.

Dr. Martin Horning, Professor of Economics at Mount Union College, gave the address. Entitled "Mathematics and Economics", Dr. Horning explained how mathematics is used in economics.
