Initiation Ceremony Held

The 1998 Pi Mu Epsilon initiation ceremony was held on Tuesday, April 21, at 7:00 PM in the Private Dining Room of the HPCC. Dr. John Kirchmeyer, Director of Computer Information Services and Professor in Computer Science, Information Systems, and Mathematics, gave the address on ISETLW, the Interactive Set Language for Windows.
