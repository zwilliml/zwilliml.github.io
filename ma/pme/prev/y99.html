Initiation Ceremony Held

The 2000 Pi Mu Epsilon initiation ceremony was held on Wednesday, April 19, at 7:00 PM in the Private Dining Room of the HPCC. Dr. Tom Botzman, Associate Professor in the Department of Economics, Accounting and Business Administration, gave the address on "Mathematical Models and the Stock Market". Also, the yearbook picture was taken.
