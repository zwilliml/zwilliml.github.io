Mount Union College

October, 1999

Position Available in Mathematics Education

Responsibilities: The successful candidate will have a strong commitment to quality teaching at the undergraduate level and will teach mathematics education courses as well as other mathematics courses.

Qualifications: Doctorate required with at least one graduate degree in mathematics. Experience teaching at the pre-college level is preferred. Candidates must have a commitment to teaching in a liberal arts setting.

Available: August, 2000

Compensation: Competitive, based upon qualifications. Fringe benefits include a 10% contribution by the College to TIAA/CREF after one year service; contribution by the College toward hospitalization, major medical, disability, and group life insurance; tuition discount for spouse or children attending Mount Union.

Equal Opportunity: Mount Union has a strong commitment to nurturing a culturally diverse faculty and student body. Women and minorities are encouraged to apply. Mount Union College is an equal opportunity employer.

Academic Rank: Tenure-Track Position. Rank dependent upon qualifications.

Descriptive Information: Mount Union College, founded in 1846, is an accredited, four year, residential coeducational liberal arts college with 110 full-time faculty members and more than 2150 students. Eighty-three percent of the faculty hold terminal degrees. Relationships between the faculty and students are close and constructive. The College is affiliated with the United Methodist Church and emphasizes the total educational growth and development of students: intellectual, moral, spiritual, and social. Facilities are modern, the campus is pleasant, and the College has well-rounded cultural and social programs. The College's endowment is valued in excess of $120 million. Grants and gifts for all purposes approach $9.5 million annually. Relations with the City of Alliance (population 25,000) in which Mount Union is located are excellent. Alliance is located near Canton, Akron, and Youngstown and is approximately 60 miles southeast of Cleveland and 75 miles west of Pittsburgh.

Send a letter of interest, resume, graduate transcript, and three current letters of reference with addresses and phone numbers to: Dr. Michael Zwilling, Chair, Department of Mathematics, Mount Union College, Alliance, OH 44601. E-mail: ZWILLIML@MUC.EDU. Please specify that the position is the mathematics education position, not the position for which a demonstrated interest in applied mathematics is preferred. See http://www.muc.edu/ma/ for more information.

Consideration of applications will begin immediately.
