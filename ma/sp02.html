MA 399 Special Topics course description for Spring semester 2002:

MA 399 Special Topics - Number Theory. A study of the fundamental concepts of number theory. The concept of number will be explored using an axiomatic, an experimental, and an historical approach. Topics include divisibility, prime numbers, numerical functions, and congruences. Prerequisite: MA 301 or permission of the instructor. 3 Sem. Hrs.
