Mathematics Requirement for Mathematics Teachers

Students who intend to teach must satisfy the mathematics requirement in the appropriate category below:

  • Middle Childhood Teachers (grades 4 - 9, ages 9 - 14) with a mathematics emphasis;
  • High School Mathematics Teachers (grades 7 - 12, ages 9 - 21);
  • College Mathematics Teachers.
