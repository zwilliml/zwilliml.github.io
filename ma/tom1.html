University of Mount Union

The Tomsich Outstanding Junior or Senior Prize

Five thousand dollars will be awarded in a single prize to the outstanding Mount Union College junior or senior student in natural science, mathematics, and computer science. The award will be presented at the annual honors awards convocation held at the end of each academic year. The awardee will be chosen by the heads of the departments of Biology, Chemistry, Computer Science, Geology, Mathematics, and Physics.

This program is for Mount Union College students who are juniors or seniors as defined in the Mount Union College catalog. Nominees must also have completed two consecutive semesters at Mount Union College.
