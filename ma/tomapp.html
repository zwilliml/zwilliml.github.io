Letter of Support

In evaluating candidates for the summer research program it will be especially helpful if the second letter, which is from a person other than the research supervisor, describes the degree to which the candidate is self-motivated, exhibits good laboratory skills, if appropriate, and has given evidence of being able to complete a project. In addition, the letter should discuss the ability of the candidate to write effectively. Letters of support should be addressed to the selection committee.
