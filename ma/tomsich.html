University of Mount Union

Tomsich Awards for Excellence in Natural Science, Mathematics, and Computer Science

Note: The grant for the Tomsich Awards has been depleted, and the Tomsich Awards are no longer being presented.

The Tomsich Awards are four annual awards of $5,000 each to students of Mount Union College in natural science, mathematics, and computer science.

One of the awards is for the outstanding junior or senior student. Three of the awards are for summer research.

  • The Tomsich Outstanding Junior or Senior Prize

  • The Tomsich Summer Research Program
