The following announcement is from the March 17, 2000, edition of The Catholic Exponent:

Two priests retire, two accept 'Senior Active' role; four named to new pastorates

Bishop Thomas J. Tobin has announced assignment changes for eight priests that will affect numerous parishes in the Diocese of Youngstown. The changes are effective July 1. A ninth priest will resign one pastorate but retain a second.

...

Two priests will assume the role of Senior Priest Active:

- Father Richard Brobst, pastor of Youngstown St. Brendan Parish, will move to Boardman St. Luke as of July 1 to minister there.

- Father John MulQueen, pastor of East Liverpool St. Aloysius Parish, will move to a new assignment that was not determined at press time.

Senior Priests Active continue to serve in active priestly sacramental ministry in parishes, but on reduced schedules without administrative responsibilities.

...

FATHER BROBST, a native of Youngstown, attended St. Brendan School and Ursuline High School. Following graduation from Holy Cross College in Worcester, Mass., he served four years in the Navy and then entered St. Philip Neri School (for delayed vocations) in Boston, Mass. He attend Mount St. Mary's of the West Seminary in Cincinnati and Xavier University in Cincinnati. He was ordained Aug. 14, 1965, by Bishop James W. Malone in St. Brendan Church.

Following ordination, he was assigned as assistant pastor at St. Edward, Youngstown (1966-67), and became a faculty member at Cardinal Mooney High School in 1967-69. He was named to the faculty of Louisville St. Thomas Aquinas High School (1969-71) and served as assistant pastor at Boardman St. Charles (1971-81).

Father Brobst was named pastor of New Middletown St. Paul the Apostle Parish in 1981, where he served until becoming pastor of Newton Falls St. Joseph Parish in 1987. He was named pastor of St. Brendan in 1989.

While pastor of St. Paul, Father Brobst was named a team member to the continuing education for priests program, and was named to the newly-formed Priests Council in 1984. In 1985, he assumed additional part-time duties as priest personnel director, a position he held until 1987.
