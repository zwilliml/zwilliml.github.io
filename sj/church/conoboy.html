Saint Joseph Church

Father Shawn Conoboy

June 29, 2018

Beginning July 14 (2018), Father Shawn Conoboy, sacramental minister of Holy Family Parish, Navarre, has been appointed administrator of SS. Mary and Joseph Parish, Newton Falls, for one year.

FATHER CONOBOY was ordained July 29, 2006, in St. Columba Cathedral by Bishop Carl F. Mengeling, retired bishop of Lansing, Mich. Since ordination, he has served at St. Charles Parish, Boardman, as associate pastor; and Christ the Good Shepherd Parish, Campbell, as associate pastor and later as pastor.

During a sabbatical over the past year, he was in residence at Walsh University, North Canton, while studying theology at Duquesne University, Pittsburgh, and served as sacramental minister at Holy Family Parish.
