The following announcement is from the May 11, 2001, edition of The Catholic Exponent:

Diocese to honor 16 priest jubilarians May 21 at Boardman St. Charles

A celebration for diocesan priests celebrating their 65th, 50th, 40th, and 25th anniversaries of ordination will be held at 4 p.m. May 21in Boardman St. Charles Church. The priests will concelebrate a Mass with Bishop Thomas J. Tobin.

Marking their 65th anniversary of ordination this year:

- Father Stephen Bakos, pastor emeritus of Youngstown, St. Francis of Assisi;

- Father Albert Klein, pastor emeritus of Garrettsville St. Ambrose;

- Father John C. Rath, pastor emeritus of Canton Our Lady of Peace.

FATHER KLEIN was born Feb. 23, 1910, in Yukon, Pa, and attended Youngstown SS. Cyril and Methodius School, St. Procopius College in Lisle, Ill., and St. Mary Seminary, Cleveland. He also graduated from John Carroll University, Cleveland. He was ordained March 22, 1936, by Bishop Joseph Schrembs in St. John Cathedral, Cleveland.

Father Klein, pastor emeritus of Garrettsville St. Ambrose Parish, served as an associate pastor at Campbell St. John from 1936-40. He entered the Army in 1940 as a chaplain, serving throughout World War II in the Pacific and European theaters. He retired with the rank of lieutenant colonel in the Army Reserves. In 1946 he was named pastor of Newton Falls St. Joseph Parish, where he served 20 years. He returned to Campbell St. John as pastor in 1966, and in 1971 became pastor of St. Ambrose Parish, from which he retired in 1975. He also served on the Priests Senate, served as chaplain of the Knights of Columbus in Newton Falls, and was a chaplain in the Civil Air Patrol and a first lieutenant in the Chaplain's Corps. He received permission to celebrate Mass in the Byzantine Rite in 1975.

Father Klein lives in Margate, Fla.
