The following announcement is from the May 16, 1997, edition of The Catholic Exponent:

Fr. John Madden named pastor of two Newton Falls parishes

Father John Madden, formerly associate pastor at Struthers St. Nicholas Parish, has been appointed pastor of St. Joseph and St. Mary Parish, Newton Falls. The appointment is effective June 9.

He succeeds Father Kenneth Strader, who was granted a leave of absence in April.

Father Madden was born August 6, 1950, in Youngstown, and attended the parish school and East Palestine High School. He earned a bachelor's from Walsh College in 1973 and another in business administration from Youngstown State University in 1979. He earned a masters of divinity degree at Mount St. Mary of the West Seminary, Cincinnati. He was ordained a deacon May 5, 1985, by Bishop Benedict C. Franzetta and served his diaconate internship at St. Brendan, Youngstown.

Father Madden was ordained June 21, 1986, at Our Lady of Lourdes, East Palestine, his home parish, by Bishop James W. Malone.

Following ordination, he was named associate pastor at Blessed Sacrament Parish, Warren, where he served until 1991. He was named associate pastor at St. Nicholas in 1991.

The following announcement is from the May 1, 2006, edition of The Catholic Exponent:

Four Priests Receive New Assignments

Four priests of the Diocese of Youngstown have received new assignments from Reverend Monsignor Robert J. Siffrin, Diocesan Administrator. Reverend Edward Beneleit is appointed Administrator of St. Clement Parish in Navarre, effective July 1, 2006; Reverend John Madden is appointed Administrator of Our Lady of Perpetual Help Parish in McDonald, effective July 1, 2006; Reverend Richard Pentello is appointed Administrator of St. Patrick Parish in Kent, effective July 10, 2006; and Reverend Frank Zanni is appointed Administrator at St. Bernadette Parish in Masury, effective July 1, 2006.

Father Beneleit was ordained November 10, 1974, after completing seminary studies at Mt. St. Mary Seminary in Cincinnati. His previous assignments include associate pastor at St. Joseph Parish in Alliance, Holy Family Parish in Poland, Blessed Sacrament Parish in Warren, St. Edward Parish in Youngstown, St. James Parish in Waynesburg, St. Joseph Parish in Canton, and his current assignment as associate pastor at St. Joan of Arc Parish in Canton. Father Beneleit was chaplain for the Boy Scouts of Mahoning County, and is Faithful Friar for the Knights of Columbus.

Father Madden was ordained June 21, 1986 following seminary studies at Mt. St. Mary Seminary in Cincinnati. He served as associate pastor at Blessed Sacrament Parish in Warren and St. Nicholas Parish in Struthers. He currently serves as pastor of both St. Joseph Parish and St. Mary Parish in Newton Falls, a position he has held since 1997. Fr. Madden also served as Chaplain for the Serra Club of Youngstown, the Boy Scouts of Trumbull County, and the Knights of Columbus.

Father Pentello was ordained June 23, 1979. He completed seminary studies at Mt. St. Mary Seminary, Cincinnati. Fr. Pentello has served as associate pastor Holy Family Parish in Poland, St. Brendan Parish in Youngstown and St. Mary Parish in Massillon. He currently serves as pastor of St. Ambrose Parish in Garrettsville, a position he has held since 1990. Fr. Pentello also provides campus ministry at Hiram College. He has served as Dean of Portage County Deanery, and as a member of the Diocesan Liturgical Committee. He is Faithful Friar for the Knights of Columbus.

Father Zanni was ordained June 9, 1990 after completing seminary studies at Mt. St. Mary Seminary in Cincinnati. He served as associate pastor at St. Patrick Parish in Hubbard, St. Luke Parish in Boardman and St. Rose Parish in Girard. He has served as pastor at St. Vincent DePaul Parish in Vienna since 1998, and will continue in that assignment in conjunction with his new assignment at St. Bernadette Parish. Father Zanni has served on the Priests’ Personnel Board, the Priests’ Council, and was spiritual advisor to the diocesan AIDS ministry.

The following announcement is from Kay Suzelis, principal of of Saints Mary and Joseph School:

Fond Farewell

If you are not a member of St. Joseph or St. Mary Parish, you may have missed the announcement this past weekend at mass. Fr. John Madden, who is the pastor of St. Joseph & St. Mary Churches and our school canonical administrator has been asked to accept another position. He will be leaving on July 1, 2006 to pastor Our Lady of Perpetual Help in McDonald. He has been the pastor here for 9 years. For all of our students, he is their pastor; the only one they've ever known. We certainly honor his decision and wish him only great success and happiness in his new pastorate. We are still saddened by having to say farewell to a very good friend.

Fr. Madden has always shown complete support for our school. He has allowed us to pilot our own future and respected the leadership in place at our school. He's worked closely with Consultative Council and me to plan for the future and oversee current school operations. He has always been available and present when we've asked him to be.

Personally, I have appreciated his trust in me, his support of my leadership capabilities and his willingness to "be there whenever we needed him." His presence in our school is evident in the relationship he has developed with our students. He has made it a priority to share retreat time with our students and to be with them on their class trip. He's our celebrant at every mass and reconciliation service. Our children know him and they love him. I think that's an extremely valuable gift to our children.

When I look back over the last nine years, I see a great many accomplishments for our school. We would not have been able to accomplish many things without Father's support, his assistance and ultimately his permission. We have been able to see our school family stabilize and grow when so many other schools are experiencing downsizing and even closing. When I look at the state of many schools around the Diocese, I am saddened by what's happening. At the same time, I am so grateful and feel extremely blest that we have been able to stand firm in the face of many challenges. When it comes right down to it-this school belongs to God. You've heard me say that often. Please know that Father has been instrumental in allowing the Spirit to stand at the helm of our school. That's why we survive and flourish.

Please take the opportunity to thank Fr. Madden for all he has done for us. Make sure that your children thank him as well. As a school, we will do some things at the end of the year to express to Father our gratitude.

I also ask that you pray for the appointment of our next pastor. He will be the canonical administrator of the school. Let's pray for someone that is dedicated to Catholic Education, has a deep love for our children, is committed to building the future through the ministry of Catholic Education and is a good boss! (That last one is for me!)
