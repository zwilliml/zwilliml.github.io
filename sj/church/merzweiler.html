Father David Merzweiler

Bishop Murry has made the following appointment:

Father David Merzweiler, Pastor of Saints Mary & Joseph, Newton Falls, effective September 16, 2019. He will remain pastor of Our Lady of the Lakes in North Jackson/Lake Milton.
