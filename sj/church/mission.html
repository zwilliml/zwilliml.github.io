Mission Statement of Saint Mary and Saint Joseph Church, starting in 2008

As the faith community of St. Mary and St. Joseph Parishes reunited in the body of Christ, we see our mission as one of both visual and spiritual unity. Together we shall work and witness to advance this mission of Christ in our families, our community, our school, and in the broader world. We pledge obedience to the truth of God as revealed in his Word, the Holy Scriptures.

Mission Statement of Saint Joseph Church prior to 2008, before Combining with Saint Mary Church

As a faith community, united in sacrament, service, education and worship, we dedicate ourselves to living the Gospel in witness to our Catholic faith and commit ourselves to the following mission:
  • to be a welcoming parish that fosters a spirit of hospitality and unity within our parish families;

  • to provide for the spiritual growth and worship needs of the Catholic community through prayer, liturgy, celebration of the sacraments and religious formation;

  • to successfully educate our children in their Catholic faith through strong religious ministries in our parish school, CCD programs and youth ministry;

  • to promote peace, justice and charity in our community and in the world through our Christian witness, stewardship, service and social ministries;

  • to foster for all ages, an active and life-long participation in the Christian community by providing opportunities for individuals to use their time and talents in service to the parish.
