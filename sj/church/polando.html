The Life of Monsignor Peter M. Polando

Monsignor Polando was born on February 17, 1954 in Youngstown, a son of Paul H. and Mary T. (Horney) Polando. He attended Holy Name of Jesus School, West Junior High School and Chaney High School. Following his graduation from high school, he entered Saint Gregory Seminary in Cincinnati, Ohio, where in 1976 he was awarded a Bachelor of Arts degree, majoring in ecclesiastical and United States histories. He continued his graduate studies at Mount Saint Mary Seminary of the West, also in Cincinnati, and earned a Master of Arts degree in sacred theology.

Monsignor Polando completed his deacon internship at Saint Aloysius Church in East Liverpool. He was ordained to the priesthood on June 21,1980 by the Most Reverend James W. Malone at Saint Columba Cathedral. He was assigned to the Church of Saint Mary in Massillon, Ohio, as parochial vicar and was the Catholic chaplain at Indian River Correctional Institute for Boys. He was also on the teaching staff at Walsh University in Canton, lecturing in the department of theology.

In July 1982, Monsignor Polando was transferred to Cardinal Mooney High School in Youngstown where he served as an instructor in religion and western civilization. In August 1984, he was appointed associate principal. During his tenure at Cardinal Mooney, he was head coach of boys and girls cross country and of boys track. In August 1986, he was awarded a Master of Arts degree in liturgical studies from the University of Notre Dame in South Bend, Indiana, and in May 1989, he was awarded a Master of Arts degree in non-public school administration from Ursuline College in Cleveland, Ohio. During that time he did additional studies at the University of San Francisco and Kent State University.

In August 1988, Monsignor Polando was appointed pastor of Saint Mary Church in Newton Falls, Ohio and the following year he was appointed pastor of Saint Joseph Church in Newton Falls. He was elected Supreme Chaplain of the First Catholic Slovak Union of the United States and Canada at its forty-fourth convention in Anaheim, California, in 1991. At this time he was on the teaching staff of Ursuline College and pursued doctoral studies at Duquesne University in Pittsburgh. Monsignor Polando continued studies in canon law at the Catholic University of America in Washington, DC, in 1992. At the Slovak Catholic Federation's fifty-third convention held in Youngstown, he was elected First Vice President in 1993. After receiving his degree in canon law, Monsignor Polando was assigned pastor of Saint Matthias Church and was appointed judge on the tribunal of the Diocese of Youngstown in 1994. In that same year he was elected to a second term as Supreme Chaplain for the First Catholic Slovak Union at its forty-fifth convention held in Chicago. Monsignor presently is the President of the Slovak Catholic Federation, a position he was elected to at its fifty-fourth national convention in Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania, in 1996.

In addition to his parochial and tribunal duties, Monsignor Polando is a member and chaplain of Branch 731 of the First Catholic Slovak Union, chaplain for the Reverend Joseph L. Kostik District of the Jednota, chaplain of Slovak Catholic Sokol Wreath 54 and member of Wreath 108, member of the First Catholic Slovak Ladies Association Branch 169, member of the Youngstown Diocese Liturgical Commission, moderator of the Youngstown Council of Catholic Nurses, member of Phi Beta Kappa of the University of San Francisco, member of the Notre Dame Club of the Mahoning and Shenango Valleys, member of the Alumni Association of the University of Notre Dame, a Fourth Degree member of the Knights of Columbus (Massillon Assembly), member of the Newton Falls Council of the Knights of Columbus, and serves as master of ceremonies at episcopal celebrations.
