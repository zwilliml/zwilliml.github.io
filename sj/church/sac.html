Saint Joseph Church

The Sacraments

INFANT BAPTISM: Baptisms are scheduled on an individual basis; please contact the Parish Office to set a date. All parents are required to participate in our Baptism Preparation Program prior to their child's Baptism. Please register for the class by calling the Parish Office.

MARRIAGE: Please schedule an appointment with the pastor at least 6 months prior to the date you would like. All engaged couples participate in a Preparation Program.

RECONCILIATION: The Sacrament of Reconciliation will be offered every Saturday 1/2 hour before Mass by appointment. Father is also available for individual confessions upon request - please call the Parish Office to make an appointment. Communal Celebrations of the Sacrament are held during Advent and Lent.

SICK CALLS: If your loved one is in the hospital or Nursing Home and you would like a visit from our pastor, please call the Parish Office. The same policy applies to those who are homebound.

NEW INQUIRERS TO THE CATHOLIC FAITH: The Parishes welcome those who have questions about the faith. Please contact the Parish Office. Persons or families, new to the parish, may register by calling the Parish Office.
