1995 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

St. Joe Edges Keiffer's DDS 9-8

The St. Joe Owls got their first victory of the season with a narrow 9-8 victory over Keiffer's DDS. The Keiffer team featured former St. Joe players Todd Hawkins, Terry Smith, Joe Thiry, and Jim Wolfe.

The Owls forged a 9-5 lead going into the 6th when the Dentists loaded the bases with none out. Terry Smith followed with a grounder that was fielded by St. Joe third sacker Mike Zwilling, who converted it into a 5-2-1-5-1 double play, erasing the two lead runners. But Keiffer's came back to push across 3 runs that inning, narrowing the gap to the final 9-8.

Sean Young led the Owls with a double and a homer, garnering 6 rbi's. Mike Slabaugh and Mike Sandor were 3-for-3 for the Holy Ones. Zwilling added a 2-out rbi single in the 4th.
