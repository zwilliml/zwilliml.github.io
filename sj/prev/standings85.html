St. Joe Softball 1985 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1985 Season Summary

St. Joe had a 9-7 regular season record and featured some punch in the line-up with Don McGregor's 5 home runs and .551 batting average. Bob Jadick batted .607, while manager Al Ratini slugged at an .824 pace. The team average was a respectable .442.
