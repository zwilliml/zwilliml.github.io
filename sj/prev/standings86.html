St. Joe Softball 1986 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1986 Season Summary

St. Joe's 7-9 regular season record was pretty good, considering the offense could muster only 11 doubles, 1 triple, and no homers--for the entire season!

One bright spot in the 1-3 record of the mid-season tournament was St. Joe's only triple play in history, on August 9 in a 4-2 loss to Latenight: with men on first and second, Mike Zwilling fielded a sharp one-hopper, stepped on third, threw to first to retire the batter, and the baserunner was tagged off first, confused about whether Zwilling had trapped the ball.
