St. Joe Softball 1987 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1987 Season Summary

St. Joe's punchless offense could muster only a meager .309 team batting average, the worst ever. We managed one win in the Umpire's Tournament, but followed with a 1-hit shutout loss--almost a 7-inning no-hitter!
