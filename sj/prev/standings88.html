St. Joe Softball 1988 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1988 Season Summary

St. Joe and Fiesta faced off in the season finale with identical 8-7 records--the winner going to the playoffs and the loser going home. St. Joe won it 7-2! Al Ratini's 2-run homer was the big blow.

Mike Slabaugh finished 8th in the league in batting average at .500 (really .468), while Ron Ardlen was 6th in homers with 3 (really 2).
