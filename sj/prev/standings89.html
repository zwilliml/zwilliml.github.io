St. Joe Softball 1989 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1989 Season Summary

The St. Joe team went 6-11 in the regular season and 0-2 in the Umpire's Tournament. The season featured a 10-inning 4-3 victory over Fiesta.

Jeff Smith finished 15th in the league in batting average at .549 (really .483) and first in triples with 6 (really 8).
