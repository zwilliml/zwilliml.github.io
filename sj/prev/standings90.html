1990 Season Summary

This season was really not as bad as the 4-16 record looks. We won two of our first three games, had many close games, and lost only one game by the 12-run mercy rule.

Ed Friesen finished 8th in the league in batting average at .516 (really .569) and first in triples with 12 (really 13). Terry Smith was 24th in BA with .444 (really .420).

Oh yes, this was the year of the famous loss by an unintentional intentional walk. The second game of the year, we led Amvets 4-0 going into the bottom of the 7th. Amvets rallied and tied the score with a one-out triple, so St. Joe pitcher Bob Jadick waved the next two batters to first to load the bases. But he lost track and waved the next batter to first also, forcing in the winning run. He immediately realized his mistake and we all protested that of course we did not intentionally give that batter an intentional walk, but to no avail.
