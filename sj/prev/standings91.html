St. Joe Softball 1991 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1991 Season Summary

Well, it doesn't get much worse than this (but see next year). The team batting average was a miniscule .337 (only 1987's .309 was worse), with the slugging average not much higher since only 19 hits in 605 at bats went for extra bases. Demetra Hartman batted .364, seventh on the team, earning her a better average than the team and nine men.
