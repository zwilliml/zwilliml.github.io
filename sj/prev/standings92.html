St. Joe Softball 1992 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1992 Season Summary

Ouch! And we thought it couldn't get any worse after last year's 1-19. Yes, a rare perfecto: no wins and twenty (count 'em) losses. Six were by the 12-run mercy rule; three by a single run, and the last one by forfeit. 'Nuff said.
