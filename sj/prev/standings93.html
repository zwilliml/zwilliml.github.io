St. Joe Softball 1993 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1993 Season Summary

Well, I guess anything is an improvement over last season's 0-20 mark, but we were expecting a little better than our eventual 4-16 record after we won two of our first three games. Still, we climbed out of last place after two straight years there. A win over cross-town rivals NF Amvets (third place, 13-7) was a season highlight.
