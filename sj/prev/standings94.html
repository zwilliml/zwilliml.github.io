St. Joe Softball 1994 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1994 Season Summary

St. Joe posted their second straight 4-16 season and their sixth consecutive year of failing to win a third of their games.

The Owls started the season slowly, but started playing better by mid-June. Then a forfeit victory over West Minerals seemed to inspire the Holy Ones, as they came within one run of MB Builders, then defeated Fiesta and Amerital Angels in consecutive games (two teams that ended up 13-7). The season ended on a high note with a 13-11 win over Bob's Carpet Cleaning.
