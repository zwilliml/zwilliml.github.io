St. Joe Softball 1995 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1995 Season Summary

St. Joe decided to drop down into D-League after six straight years of winning fewer than one-third of their games in C-League, but so many other C-League teams decided the same thing that C-League was eliminated and all the teams were put into three D-League divisions. The drop down didn't help, as St. Joe could muster only two wins against 18 losses, their seventh straight year of futility.

The 1995 edition of the St. Joe team featured a more potent offense, with the highest team slugging percentage in ten years. Not since the 1985 St. Joe team accomplished the last over .500 on-the-field record have the Holy Ones slugged like this. They even featured the league's home run champion: Mike Slabaugh (officially credited with 5 HR's by the league, he actually walloped 6 but was denied one by the usual inaccurate scorekeeping).

Defense was another matter, however. While fielding statistics are not kept, it was this observer's solid opinion that costly errors were at an all-time high, especially in the outfield. It was not unusual for the Saints to grant 5 or more outs per inning, and separate streaks of 19 and 16 consecutive innings of failing to hold our opponents scoreless, and single inning scoring outbursts of 14 runs, 10 runs, 8 (three times), 7 (twice), etc., etc., illustrate the lack of fielding support.

In the end, shoddy defense overcame a solid offensive effort and doomed the Owls to yet another dismal record, this time 2-18, and against several perennial D-League teams no less.
