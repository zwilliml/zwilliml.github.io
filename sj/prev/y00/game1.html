2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

St. Joe Drops Opener 24-7 To Auto Workers

Hippos Falter After 7-Run First

UAW scored every inning, including an 11-run explosion in the 4th, to subdue the Hippos 24-7 in a game played Wednesday night at POW-MIA's Field (Packard #4).

St. Joe scored all 7 of their runs in the first inning on 6 hits and 3 walks, but then their bats fell silent while the Auto Workers poured it on.

Tim Beckner was a perfect 2-for-2 with 2 rbi's for the Shepherds, while Mike Zwilling was also 2-for-2 including a double. Terry Smith added 2 hits for the Holy Ones.
