Saint Joseph Church

2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Crush Gillette 22-14

Slabaugh Shines For Shepherds

St. Joe pummeled hapless Gillette 22-14 as the Hippos scored every inning. Mike Slabaugh led the way for the Hippos with 5 hits, 5 runs scored, 4 rbi's, and 2 homers, including one to lead off the game.

The Nursing Home Boys battled gamely for a few innings, but consecutive 6-run Hippo innings in the 4th and 5th effectively crushed any resistance.

Dean Longwell and Jerry Fratarcangelo each had 4 hits, including a triple. Bryan Channell garnered 2 singles and a triple, while Dennis Roach homered and singled. Also collecting 2 hits each were Clyde Hatch and Troy Heckathorn.
