2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Lose Slugfest 22-19 to Quality Machine Jr.

Play Even Until 7th Inning

This game was tied 3 times, including 18-18 going into the 7th inning. But the Junior Machinists scored 4 times in the final frame, to only one for the Saints, clinching the contest.

Terry Smith had 3 hits for the Holy Ones, including 2 triples. Frank Diehl and Troy Heckathorn each had 3 safeties, while seven Hippos had 2 hits each.
