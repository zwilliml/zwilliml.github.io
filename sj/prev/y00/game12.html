2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Handle Leon's 11-1

Roach Leads Shepherds

Dennis Roach was 3-for-3 with a double and 3 rbi's as he led the Saints to an easy 11-1 win over Leon's in a game halted during the 5th inning by the 10-run mercy rule.

Bob Jadick was 2-for-2 for the Holy Ones, while Jim Wolfe and Mike Zwilling were 1-for-1. Wolfe scored 3 times, while Zwilling turned a nifty 4U-3 double-play to help stifle the meager Leon's defense. Mike Slabaugh had 2 hits, including a triple.
