2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Triumph Over United Refractories 18-9

Dean and Diehl Deal Defeat to Refractory Boys

St. Joe trailed in this game till the 6th inning, when a 5-run rally gave them a 10-8 lead. Then an 8-run 7th cemented the win for the Saints 18-9 over United Refractories, elevating the Hippo record to 7-6, their first time over .500 this year.

Frank Diehl led the Shepherds with 3 hits, including 2 triples and 3 rbi's. Dean Longwell clubbed 2 doubles and a single, driving home 4 runners. Bryan Channell had 3 hits and 3 ribbies. Bill Channell, Jim Wolfe, and Mike Zwilling chipped in 2 hits each.
