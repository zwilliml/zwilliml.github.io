2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Handle Gillette 12-2

Win Easily in 5 Innings

St. Joe claimed an easy 12-2 win over Gillette, raising their season record to 8-6. The Saints need only one win in their last 4 games to make the Championship Playoffs, but those 4 games are against the top 4 teams, each of which has handily defeated the Hippos once already this year.

Jerry Fratarcangelo led the Saints with a perfect 3-for-3 night, plus 2 rbi's. Jim Wolfe was 2-for-2, while Mike Slabaugh had 2 hits, including a 2-run homer. Bryan Channell tripled, singled, and drove home 2 runs. Clyde Hatch had 2 hits, and Bob Jadick added a double.
