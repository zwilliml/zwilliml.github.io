2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

IHS Eastwood Orthotics Slaughters Hippos 21-7

Eastwood Features Solid Hitting

IHS Eastwood Orthotics is in the business of making artificial limbs, and they created more business for themselves Friday night by tearing the Shepherds limb-from-limb in a 21-7 laugher at POW-MIA's field.

The Orthotics Boys scored at will on solid hitting, while the Saints managed 7 runs on 8 hits and 3 walks. The game was halted after 4.5 innings by the mercy rule.

Dave McElrath, Troy Heckathorn, and Mike Zwilling were each a perfect 1-for-1 for the Hippos. Bill and Bryan Channell picked up 2 rbi's each.
