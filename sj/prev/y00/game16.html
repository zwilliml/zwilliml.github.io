2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Front Street Trucking Edges Hippos 17-14

Saints Lose Slugfest

St. Joe led much of this game, but faltered at the end to drop a 17-14 decision to undefeated Front Street Trucking. The loss evened the Saints' record at 8-8.

The Holy Ones mounted a 22-hit attack, but all 22 were singles, plus they stranded 11 runners during the game. Meanwhile, the Truckers showed why they are undefeated as they rallied from deficits of 9-2, 10-8, and 14-13 to win.

Clyde Hatch led the Hippos with a perfect 4-for-4 night. Mike Slabaugh, Jim Wolfe, and Dennis Roach each had 3 hits for the Shepherds. Mike Zwilling and Al Ratini had 2 hits each, while Dave McElrath had a single and 4 rbi's.
