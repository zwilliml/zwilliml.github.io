2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Quality Machine Dumps Hippos 15-4

Little Offense in Blowout

St. Joe dropped their third straight contest, losing 15-4 against Quality Machine. The game was halted after 5 innings by the mercy rule. The Hippos now must win their last game to make the Championship Play-offs, since the loss dropped them below .500 at 8-9.

The Saints played decent defense, turning 2 nifty double plays (5-4-3 in the 3rd, and 6-4-3 in the 5th), but the Holy Ones' bats were uncharacteristically quiet in the loss.

Karl Lyngstad celebrated his birthday by blasting an rbi triple. Mike Slabaugh lofted a double up against the fence. Bob Jadick was a perfect 1-for-1.
