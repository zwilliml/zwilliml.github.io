2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

St. Joe Upsets Quality Machine JR 19-15

Hippos Reach Playoffs in Last Game

St. Joe took their 8-9 record up against the 2nd place Junior Machinists and their 14-3 record, with the Hippos needing a win in this last regular season game in order to reach the Championship Playoffs. And the Hippos scrambled to victory and headed for the playoffs with a 19-15 triumph.

Early on, it looked like the Saints would run away with a laugher, as they built a 15-0 lead going into the bottom of the 5th. But instead of a 10-run mercy rule triumph, the Junior Machinists scared the Holy Ones with a 9-run explosion, as their bats suddenly came alive.

But the Hippos kept up the offensive pressure, scoring every inning against the highly touted Junior Machinist defense, refusing to wilt under pressure with the playoffs on the line.

Dean Longwell and Jerry Fratarcangelo led the Hippos with perfect 4-for-4 nights, each with a double and 3 runs scored. Dave McElrath had 3 hits and 5 rbi's, while Troy Heckathorn was 2-for-3 with 4 ribbies.
