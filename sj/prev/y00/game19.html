2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

St. Joe Starts Playoffs with Solid 13-5 Win Over IHS/Eastwood Orthotics

7th Place Hippos Upset 3rd Place Orthotics

St. Joe opened the Championship Playoffs with a startling 13-5 win over IHS/Eastwood Orthotics, who had defeated the Shepherds easily in 2 previous meetings this season.

But it was a different story in the playoffs, as the Hippos played well, especially on offense with key hits. A 5-run first inning got the Saints off to a good start and seemed to dampen the Orthotics Boys' booming bats.

Among the key hits were Frank Diehls' 2-out, 2-run single in the first, Dean Longwell's 2-out rbi single in the third, and Dennis Roach's 2-out, 2-run single in the 6th. The Holy Ones maximized efficiency by using 3 outs for run-producing sacrifice flies.

The Saints were aided when the Orthotics' big #4 hitter smashed 2 balls over the center field fence for outs, including one to end the game. Both came with men on base.

Five Hippos had perfect 2-for-2 nights: Bryan Channell, Dean Longwell, Dave McElrath, Jerry Fratarcangelo, and Bob Jadick. Four more players were 2-for-3.
