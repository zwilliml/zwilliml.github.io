2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Kraft Maid Eases To 10-3 Win Over St. Joe

Hippos Play Short-Handed

St. Joe could not mount a consistent offense and fell to the Cabinet Makers 10-3. The Hippos had only 8 players at game time, so to avoid a forfeit they drafted a stranger from the stands, Ryan Blair, who performed well. But with only 9 players, the Saints had to take an automatic out in the 10th spot in the batting order, effectively squelching any rallies.

Meanwhile, Kraft Maid scored 3 in the first and 6 more in the second, then coasted the rest of the way.

Bill Channell and Blair each had 2 hits and an rbi for the Hippos. Mike Slabaugh whacked a triple for the only Shepherd extra base hit.
