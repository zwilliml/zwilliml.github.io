2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

ITAM Hammers Hippos 13-3

Saints Shoved Into Loser's Bracket

ITAM beat St. Joe like a drum, scoring early and often, in this second round playoff game that put the Shepherds into the loser's bracket.

The Italian Americans hit at least 3 or 4 balls that would have been over the fence for outs in a normal softball park, but this game was played on McGregor Field, the baseball park with a 390 foot fence in center field, so the Italians scampered around the bases on their blasts before the Hippo outfielders could even touch the ball. Not that it would have made much difference.

Dean Longwell and Clyde Hatch led the Holy Ones with perfect 2-for-2 games. Bob Jadick was 1-for-1 plus a walk, while Troy Heckathorn garnered 2 walks in 2 appearances.
