2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

St. Joe Ousted From Playoffs By Pogo's Pub 15-11

Hippos Fought Valiantly, But Softball Year Is Over

St. Joe bowed out of the playoffs and ended the year with a 15-11 loss to Pogo's Pub. The Hippos ended the year with an overall record of 10-11.

The Pubsters threatened to make a laugher out of this one, building a 14-2 lead going into the 5th inning. The Saints would need 3 runs to make the game last another inning, and they exploded with 7 runs to put themselves right back in the game and scare the bejeebers out of Pogo's, who appeared to start crumbling.

But the Publings pulled themselves together enough to stave off the charging Hippos and secure the victory. Pogo's featured several former Saints, such as Roy Shafer, Sean Young, Pete Bartlett, and Mike Sandor.

Bill Channell and Jerry Fratarcangelo were perfect, each going 3-for-3. Bryan Channell was 2-for-2 plus a walk, while Dave McElrath hammered a double and a single plus 2 rbi's.
