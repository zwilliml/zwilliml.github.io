2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Shade Outback 7-5 for First Victory

Six Runs in 2nd Inning

St. Joe built an early 7-0 lead, then held on to defeat Outback 7-5 for the first Hippo victory of the year.

In the big second inning, the Shepherds garnered 6 runs on 5 hits, supplemented by 4 walks and a key Outback dropped fly for an error. The Steak House Boys battled back, but a stubborn Hippo defense held them in check.

Mike Slabaugh stroked 3 singles for the Holy Ones, while Bill Channell had 2 hits and 3 rbi's. Dean Longwell added 2 hits and a ribbie, while Troy Heckathorn drew 3 walks. Al Ratini pitched 5 scoreless innings, striking out two.
