2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Rally for Wild 14-13 Victory Over R & R

Blow Big Lead, Then Win

St. Joe strolled into the final inning leading R & R 11-5, but defensive lapses helped R & R score 8 runs to give them a 13-11 lead. The Hippos were not to be denied, however, as they mounted a 3-run 2-out rally to win the game.

Terry Smith's 2-out bases-loaded single drove home 2 runs to tie the game 13-13. Then R & R issued an intentional walk to Dave McElrath, who had boomed a double off the right field fence in his previous at bat. But the strategy backfired as Dennis Roach walked on a pitch in the dirt with a 3-2 count, forcing home Bill Channell with the winning run.

The Hippos once again had only 9 players, forcing them to take an automatic out in the 10th spot in the batting order. But those 9 players mounted an impressive 23-hit offensive attack.

Smith led the Holy Ones with 4 hits and 3 rbi's. Mike Slabaugh added 3 hits, including a solo homer, and McElrath added 3 hits and 3 ribbies. Mike Zwilling and Bill Channell had 3 hits each, while Bryan Channell drove home 3 runs. Bob Jadick stroked 2 singles, and Jerry Fratarcangelo had a single and tagged out a runner trying to score in a key play.
