2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Fall To Truckers 12-2

Bats Silenced By Double-Plays

St. Joe had only 7 hits in this game, and 3 of those were erased on 3 double plays, as Front Street Trucking easily defeated the Hippos 12-2 in a game called after 6 innings by the mercy rule.

The Shepherds scored both their runs in the first, as Mike Slabaugh led off with a single and Bill Channell tripled him home. Channel scored on Dave McElrath's sac fly, but that was it offensively for the game.

Slabaugh was a perfect 2-for-2 for the Hippos, while Troy Heckathorn, Bob Jadick, and Al Ratini were 1-for-1. Bill Channell's triple and Bryan Channell's single rounded out the meager Shepherd offense.
