Saint Joseph Church

2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Win 20-18 Slugfest Over United Refractories

Shepherds Unleash Furious 26-Hit Attack

St. Joe and United Refractories unfurled relentless offensive attacks against each other, but the Hippos emerged from the bloodbath with a 20-18 victory in hand over the Firebrick Makers.

The final outcome was in doubt to the very end, as United Refractories put 2 men on in the 7th with only one out. But Bryan Channel took matters into his own hands, fielding a grounder at second base, stepping on the bag to force the runner, and throwing out the batter at first on a bang-bang play to end the game.

The Firebrick Makers attacked first, scoring 5 runs to start the game, but St. Joe came right back with 4 tallies in their half of the first, followed by a 9-run eruption in the second, in which 10 different Hippos had hits and 2 more had sacrifice flies.

But the Refractory Boys stormed back to take a 15-14 lead in the 5th, only to see the Shepherds score 6 to retake the lead and set up the dramatic ending.

All 15 Hippos contributed to the offense. Dean Longwell and Mike Zwilling each went 3-for-3 with 2 rbi's. Terry Smith and Dave McElrath also had 3 hits each.

Six Hippos had 2 hits each: Bryan Channell (including a triple), Dennis Roach, Jerry Fratarcangelo, Karl Lyngstad, Clyde Hatch, and Al Ratini.

Bob Jadick had a hit, a sac fly, and 2 ribbies, while Troy Heckathorn singled and walked. Mike Slabaugh and Bill Channell each had a sac fly and an rbi.
