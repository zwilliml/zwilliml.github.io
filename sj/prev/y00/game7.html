2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Quality Machine Stymies Hippos 12-2

Lack of Clutch Hits Dooms Shepherds

St. Joe had 13 hits but stranded 11 runners as they couldn't get the key hits they needed in falling to Quality Machine 12-2 Friday night. The game was halted during the 6th inning by the 10-run rule.

Dennis Roach was perfect for the Hippos, going 2-for-2 with a double and an rbi. Troy Heckathorn was also 2-for-2.

Also garnering 2 hits each for the Holy Ones were Bill Channell, Dave McElrath, and Bryan Channell. Dean Longwell singled home the other Hippo run.
