2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Lose Slugfest to IHS Eastwood Orthotics 26-18

Defensive Woes Hurt Shepherds

St. Joe had 26 hits and scored 18 runs, but it wasn't nearly enough as mistakes on defense cost the Hippos dearly in the 26-18 defeat to the Orthotics Boys.

Bob Jadick led the Hippos with a perfect 4-for-4 and 2 rbi's. Garnering 3 hits each were Dennis Roach, Bill Channell, Dean Longwell, and Clyde Hatch.
