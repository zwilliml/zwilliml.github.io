2000 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Thrash Leon's 22-11

Offense in High Gear

St. Joe continued their offensive fireworks in handily disposing of Leon's 22-11 in 5 innings on the 10-run rule.

A huge 14-run 2nd inning effectively won the game for the Shepherds, although Leon's did come back with 7 runs that inning.

Mike Slabaugh, Steve Stiles, Dennis Roach, and Gerry Fratarcangelo each had 3 hits for the Hippos. Karl Lyngstad pounded a ball over the fence for the longest smash of the night, but in the Continental League that's an out.
