2000 Season Summary

The Hippos had a good offensive season, finishing 10-11 overall, making the Championship Playoffs and winning the first playoff game before being eliminated.

The Saints needed to win 9 games in the regular season to make the playoffs. They were 8-6 with 4 games left, but those 4 were against the top teams. The Shepherds lost the first 3, but won a 19-15 thriller against Quality Machine JR on the last day of the regular season to recah the playoffs.

Mike Slabaugh hit at a .561 clip, was second in the league in homers and runs scored, and led the team in slugging average, runs scored, home runs, and hits. Dean Longwell led the Hippos with a .608 batting average on the year, good for 9th in the league. Mike Zwilling batted .564 and led the team with a .705 on-base percentage.

Bryan Channel led the Saints in rbi's with 27. Troy Heckathorn's 11 walks more than doubled anybody else's total. Dennis Roach was among the team leaders in most offensive categories, while Jerry Fratarcangelo led the team in singles.
