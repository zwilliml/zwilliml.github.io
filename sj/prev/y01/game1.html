2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Slaughtered in Season Opener

Double-header 10-Run Rule Losses to Sticks-N-Stones

With the parks levy defeat in Warren, the Hippos were forced to move to the Alliance Men's League, where they were rudely greeted by Sticks-N-Stones, who welcomed the Saints by blistering them in back-to-back mercy rule massacres. The first game score was 15-5, in 5 innings.()

The Stonemen started the lidlifter by scoring 8 runs in the first inning on the shaky Saints, hitting several over-the-fence homers (7 are allowed per game in this league - not a consideration for the punchless Shepherds).

At least the Hippos were not totally humiliated, as every St. Joe batter got at least one hit, with Mike Eckart getting 2. Scott Cain and Troy Heckathorn each had a double.
