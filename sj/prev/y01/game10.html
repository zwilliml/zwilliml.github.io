2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Gionino's Pizza Edges Hippos in Nightcap

10-7 Loss Drops Hippo Record to 2-8

St. Joe was in this game all the way, struggling to a 7-7 standstill through 6 innings, when the Pizza Boys struck for 3 runs and the Hippos couldn't recover.

Gionino's staged a 6-run explosion in the first, but the Saints battled back with 10 hits in the first 3 innings to take a brief 7-6 lead. After that, the Holy offense went back to sleep and didn't threaten again.

Mike Zwilling and Tom Pleat each had 2 hits for the Hippos, while Roy Shafer smacked a triple. Troy Heckathorn had 2 rbi's on a bases-loaded single in the 2nd. Bob Jadick drew 3 free passes.
