2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Legionnaires Humiliate Hippos 29-7

Defensive Woes Doom Saints

St. Joe had a tough time defensively and were handily defeated by American Legion Post 166, as the mercy rule shortened the game.

The Shepherds won the first game of the night by forfeit, as the 9th Legionnaire showed up 5 minutes past the deadline. The Hippos might have been fortunate to receive that gift, as they could do little right in the field.

Offensively, Roy Shafer led the Holy Ones with a perfect 2-for-2, including a triple and 3 runs scored. Steve Cleary added 2 hits and 3 rbi's.
