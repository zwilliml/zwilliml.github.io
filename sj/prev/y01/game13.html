2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Marathon Sweeps Hippos

10-Run Rule Shortens Lidlifter

Roy Shafer slugged a towering 3-run homer over the centerfield fence to give the Saints a first inning lead, but that was it for the Hippo offense as the Marathon Men took control to win 14-4.

The early ending came as a surprise to the Holy Ones as the Marathoners slowly pulled away, inning by inning, to take control.

Jeff Ratini doubled and singled for the Shepherds, and Steve Cleary had 2 hits. Aside from Shafer's blast, not much else happened.
