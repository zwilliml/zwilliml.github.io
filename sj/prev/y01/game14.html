2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Marathon Edges Hippos 11-8

Record Drops to 3-11

The Saints generated a little more consistent offense this game, but fell nevertheless to the Marathon Men in the nightcap.

A 5-run 5th inning put the Marathoners in charge of affair, and while the Holy Ones remained close, they couldn't pull it out.

Head Hippo Big Al Ratini led the way with a perfect 3-for-3, while cousin Jeff tripled and doubled, providing 3 rbi's.

Jim Wolfe doubled and singled, and veteran Bob Jadick chipped in with 2 singles. Mike Slabaugh slashed a 2-run inside-the-park homer in the 7th, but it wasn't enough.
