2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Heritage Church Sweeps St. Joe

16-3 Laugher in Lidlifter

The Hippos could generate little offense in the opener, with only 8 hits and 3 runs, while Heritage Church had their offense in high gear.

Steve Cleary was 2-for-2 for the Hippos, including a triple. Jim Wolfe was also perfect with 2 hits, while Al Ratini had a double and a walk. Mike Slabaugh added a double.
