2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Saints Lose to Heritage Church in Extra Innings

Brilliant Come-From-Behind Effort Falls Just Short

St. Joe trailed by 5 runs with 2 out in the bottom of the 7th inning, then shocked Heritage Church by scoring 5 runs to tie, only to lose in extra innings 24-20.

Heritage Church appeared to have taken control of a 14-14 tie game by scoring 5 runs in the top of the 7th to take a 19-14 lead. But with 2 outs in the 7th, Mike Zwilling doubled home 2 runs, Jim Wolfe drove in another, and Tom Pleat tied the game with a booming 2-run triple to place himself on third as the winning run. With a chance to win the game, Jared Svette smashed a vicious drive to left center, but it was right at the outfielder and the game went into extra innings, where the Hippos succumbed to another 5-run rally by Heritage.

The Shepherds had an 8-run 4th inning explosion to take the lead, on the strength of 7 consecutive hits, but the Heritage offense was in high gear, scoring 5 runs in each of the last 3 innings.

The Hippo offense was also potent, scoring 20 runs on 24 hits, 11 of them for extra bases, and adding 7 walks. Mike Slabaugh led the Holy Ones with 4 hits in 5 at bats. George Zaczyk had 2 triples, a single, and a sac fly.

Pleat had 2 triples and 5 rbi's, while Zwilling had 3 hits and 5 ribbies. Wolfe slashed 3 hits and added 4 rbi's, while Steve Cleary had a single, double, and a triple. Scott Cain had 2 doubles and 2 walks.
