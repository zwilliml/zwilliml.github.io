2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Roadhouse Dismantles Saints

Hippos Offense Dismal

St. Joe could muster only 2 runs on 8 hits in falling to the powerful Barley's. The Holy Ones played some decent defense however. The game was halted after 5 innings by the mercy rule.

Troy Heckathorn led the Hippos with a perfect 2-for-2, including a double. The Ratini cousins provided the Hippo runs, as Jeff's 4th inning triple drove in one runner, and Big Al singled Jeff home.
