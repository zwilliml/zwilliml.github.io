2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Stonemen Wreak Havok on Hippos, 21-4

More Carnage in Nightcap

Sticks-N-Stones kept up their onslaught, plating 9 runs in the top of the first and scoring every inning to stomp the Saints 21-4 in the nightcap of the season opener.

The Stonemen again hit several homers, having no problem subduing the hapless Hippos.

Brian Zimmerman was a bright spot for the Saints, going 3-for-3. Jim Wolfe had 2 hits, each driving in 2 men to account for all the Shepherd scoring. Mike Slabaugh also had 2 hits.
