2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Roadhouse Sweeps Hapless Saints

Hippos Limp Into Playoffs

Barley's swept a doubleheader by shutting out the Shepherds 10-0 in a mercy rule shortened contest. The Hippos end the regular season with a record of 5-15, with 3 of the 5 wins coming by forfeit.

The Holy Ones had 11 hits in the 5 innings, but amazingly were unable to score a single run, stranding 9, hitting into a double play, and having a runner thrown out at home. Hippos were constantly on the bases, but nobody could come up with the big hit.

Mike Zwilling and Al Ratini were each 2-for-2 for St. Joe. Ryan James had a double, and Troy Heckathorn went 1-for-1 to end a perfect 3-for-3 night in the doubleheader.
