2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Lose Playoffs Opener

Roadhouse Sends Saints to Loser's Bracket

The Roadhouse powerhouse, seeded first after their big season ending upsets of Rhoads Construction, easily dispatched the pesky Hippos 19-9 in first round playoff action. The loss sent the 8th-seeded Saints to the loser's bracket in the double elimination tournament.

Barley's struck early and often in building a commanding lead, but the Hippos battled back gamely, almost extending the game beyond the mercy rule minimum as they scored 3 runs and left the bases loaded in the 5th.

Steve Cleary was 2-for-2 for the Shepherds, adding a sac fly. Roy Shafer was also 2-for-2, including a triple. Jeff Ratini and Ryan James slammed triples of their own, while Troy Heckathorn added a double.
