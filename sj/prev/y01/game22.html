2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Saints Stun Steemers 17-13

Hippos Oust Stanley Steemer from Playoffs

St. Joe unleashed a powerful 27 hit attack to swamp the #4 seeded Steemers 17-13 in playoff action Tuesday night.

The Shepherds struck for 5 runs in the top of the first on 6 singles, a walk, and a sac fly. The Saints never trailed, although the Steemers kept it fairly close with consistent offense of their own.

Roy Shafer led the Hippos with a perfect 5-for-5 night, including 3 doubles, 2 rbi's, and 3 runs scored. Sean Young had 4 hits and 5 rbi's, while Steve Cleary had 4 hits including a double.

Mike Slabaugh smashed a triple and added 2 singles. The Ratini cousins had 2 hits each, as did Mike Zwilling. Every Hippo had at least 1 hit and 1 run scored.
