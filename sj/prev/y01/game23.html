2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Saints Bow Out of Playoffs

Sticks-N-Stones End St. Joe's Year

The Hippos ended the 2001 year with a 15-9 loss to Sticks-N-Stones, their second loss in the double elimination playoffs. The Holy Ones' overall record was 6-17.

The Shepherds played a decent game against the #5 seeded Sticks, but 3 Stick homers, including a back-breaking grand salami in the 2nd inning, was too much for the Saints to overcome.

The Hippos stroked an impressive 20 hits, 4 of them for extra bases, but stranded 11, lost 2 runners, and hit into a double play.

Steve Cleary led St. Joe with a perfect 4-for-4 night, scoring twice and adding 2 rbi's. Roy Shafer stroked 2 doubles and a single, also garnering 2 ribbies.

Mike Slabaugh had 3 hits, including a triple, while Troy Heckathorn went 3-for-4 to continue his hot streak.
