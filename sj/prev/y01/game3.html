2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Pleat Leads Hippos to First Victory

First-Inning Homer Ignites Saints

Tom Pleat blasted a solo homer in the top of the first inning to spark a St. Joe 7-4 win over Stanley Steemer in the lid-lifter of a double-header split. The win was the first of the season for the Hippos, raising their record to 1-2.

Pleat also doubled home 2 runs in the decisive 4-run 3rd inning, and he singled in his final at bat for a perfect 3-for-3 game.

Jim Wolfe doubled and singled for the Shepherds, while Mike Slabaugh added 2 safeties, as did Bryan Channell.

The Hippo rally in the 3rd inning began with walks to Troy Heckathorn and Ryan James, followed by a Rob Bauman single to load the bases. After Pleat's 2-run double, Mike Zwilling drove home Bauman with a sac fly, and Slabaugh singled in Pleat. The Steemers never recovered.
