2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Nightcap Loss Gives Hippos Split

Can't Hold Lead Over Steemers

The Saints took an 11-8 lead into the 7th and final inning against Stanley Steemer, but couldn't hold it as the Steemers exploded for 6 runs to win the nightcap 14-11 and give the Hippos a double-header split.

The Shepherds built the lead with a 5-run 3rd inning, with 5 hits and 5 different Hippos driving in runs. But the Steemers caught fire in the 7th as the Holy Ones sputtered.

Bill Channell had 3 hits for the Saints, including a double. Jim Wolfe contributed 3 safeties.

Bob Jadick was a perfect 2-for-2. Also garnering 2 hits apiece for the Hippos were Rob Bauman, Tom Pleat, Mike Zwilling, Mike Slabaugh, and Mike Eckart.
