2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhoads Construction Destroys Hippos

Top Team Swamps Saints in Doubleheader Laughers

Rhoads Construction, one of the top teams in the state, used their tremendous power hitting to completely demolish the Holy Ones 25-5, implementing the super-mercy 20-run rule in only 4 innings.

The Construction Men used up their 7-homer per game allotment before the second inning was through, after which one of the few ways the Shepherds could get them out was by coaxing them to hit it over the fence. It made no significant difference that the Saints could muster only 9 players.

The Hippos did take a (very) brief 2-run lead in the top of the first, on consecutive rbi doubles by Jim Wolfe and Ryan James, both 2-for-2, but it was all Rhoads after that.
