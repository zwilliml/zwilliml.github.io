2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhoads Construction Hammers Hippos Again

New Game, Same Result

Rhoads Construction again tore apart the Saints 25-4, another 20-run rule in 4 innings. The Construction Boys again quickly used up their 7-homer limit.

Tom Pleat was 2-for-2 for the Shepherds, igniting the Hippos' mini-rally in the 4th with a single, the first of 6 consecutive Saint safeties.
