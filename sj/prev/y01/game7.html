2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Split Doubleheader with Bier Haus

Saints Dominate Lidlifter 17-7

St. Joe lowered the offensive boom on Bier Haus with a potent 28-hit attack, as every Shepherd contributed at least one hit.

Trailing 5-4 after 3 innings, the Holy Ones took the lead with 2 in the 4th, then blew it open with 6 more in the 5th, then were never headed. The big inning featured 7 singles, 5 of them consecutive.

Bob Jadick was the big Saint, going 4-for-4 with 4 rbi's. Tom Pleat had 3 hits, including 2 triples. Adding 3 singles each were Jim Wolfe, Jared Svette, and Scott Cain.

John McQuiston tripled and singles. Mike Zwilling, Jeff and Al Ratini, and Mike Slabaugh each lined two hits.
