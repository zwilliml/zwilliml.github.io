2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Wither in Nightcap Loss to Bier Haus

Sun Bakes Saints in 13-6 Defeat

You can't really call it a nightcap, as the 2:00 game was played under a blazing sun and the Hippos, who are accustomed to cool dark 9:00 PM games, visibly weakened while mounting few offensive threats.

The Bier Boys used a 6-run 8-hit 5th inning attack to crack open the game, then followed with 2 more 2-run innings, collecting a total of 18 hits for the game.

Roy Shafer led the Shepherds with a perfect 1-for-1 that featured a 3-run homer, a walk, a sac fly, and 4 rbi's. Jared Svette and Bob Jadick each swatted 2 hits in 3 tries. Mike Zwilling was 1-for-1 with 2 walks and a ribbie.
