2001 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Drop Doubleheader to Gionino's Pizza

Offense Sputters in Lidlifter, 9-1

St. Joe lost the opener 9-1 as they could manage only 8 hits against the Pizza Boys.

It was close for the first 4 innings, tied 1-1 going into the 5th. But then Gionino's exploded for 5 runs and the Hippos never responded.

Mike Zwilling had 3 of the 8 Hippo hits, while Jeff Ratini's solo homer provided the only Shepherd run.
