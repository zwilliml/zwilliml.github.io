2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Trounce Firemen 16-6

Sparkling Defense Turns 3 DP's

St. Joe looked good as they easily handled Fire Foe Corp 16-6 in the season opener for both teams. The game was called after 4.5 innings by the mercy rule.()

The Hippo defense played error-free ball and turned 3 double-plays, including the 5-4-3 game-ender. A big 9-run 4th inning sealed the win for the Holy Ones.

Andy Bone had 2 hits, including a double, plus 4 rbi's for the Saints. Mike Slabaugh and Eric Noebe also had a double and single each, while Ed Freisen tripled home 2 runs. Dean Longwell had 2 hits and 2 ribbies, while Mark Totta and Mike Welsh each added 2 singles to the 17-hit Shepherd attack.
