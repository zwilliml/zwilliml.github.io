2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Machinists Edge Hippos 17-14

Saints Can't Hold Leads

St. Joe held leads of 2-1 in the 2nd, 12-8 in the 5th, and 13-12 in the 6th, but Quality Machine overcame them all with consistent hitting, scoring every inning but one in notching the victory.

The Holy Ones staged an 8-run uprising in the 5th on 8 hits helped along by some costly Machinist errors. The Hippos played a savvy defense against QM, playing shallow and robbing them of several hits. But the Quality Machine offense was too consistent, as their 5-run 6th inning rally finally nailed down the win.

Mark Totta led the Shepherds with 3 hits. Garnering 2 hits each were Dean Longwell, Rob Medallis, Bob Jadick, and Al Ratini. Eric Noebe burned the Machinists with a ringing bases-loaded triple.
