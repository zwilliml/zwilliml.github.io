2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Upset #1 Quality Machine

Saints Control 10-5 Win

St. Joe notched a huge victory as they easily defeated the #1 ranked Quality Machine 10-5.

The Saints systematically fashioned a good lead, then cemented it with a 5-run outburst in the 7th. The Shepherds were in control the entire game, pulling off 2 double plays as the Machinist's usually potent offense could never get on track.

Dean Longwell kept up his torrid hitting for the Holy Ones, with a perfect 3-for-3 including a 2-run homer and 3 rbi's. Longwell increased his batting average to a sizzling .769 with the perfecto.

Brian Channell also had a perfect night with 2-for-2. Also contributing 2 hits each were Mark Totta, Mike Slabaugh, and Andy Bone.

This was the third and final meeting between the 2 teams during the regular season, with Quality Machine winning the first 2 but St. Joe improving each game. The schedule-maker was only supposed to assign 2 games with each team, but the mistake may help the Hippos as they learn how to play against these likely playoff opponents.
