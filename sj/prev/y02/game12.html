Saint Joseph Church

2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Edge Cortland Trinity 15-14

Survive 7th Inning Scare To Clinch Playoff Spot

St. Joe staved off a furious Cortland rally and held on to defeat the Baptists 15-14, garnering their 9th victory of the season, the magic number needed to clinch a playoff spot.

Both teams started the game with offense in high gear as the Saints led 9-8 after just 2 innings. Then after a quiet mid-game, both teams erupted for late inning rallies.

First, the Hippos used a 4-run 6th to build a seemingly comfortable 15-8 lead. But then it was the Baptists' turn in the bottom of the 7th: trailing 15-9, their offense erupted as their first 5 batters scored to narrow the gap to 15-14. They had the tying run on first with nobody out.

But then came the most dramatic and important play of the game. On a medium-depth fly to Hippo Eric Noebe in right center, the runner on first tagged and headed for second, knowing it would take a perfect throw to get him. Noebe delivered, and the runner was nailed in a double-play that suddenly turned the situation into 2-outs, nobody on. The next batter flied out meekly and the game was suddenly over.

Dean Longwell continued his torrid hitting for the Holy Ones, going a perfect 4-for-4, including a triple, a double, and 3 rbi's. Mike Slabaugh also had a perfect night, 3-for-3, with a double and a sac fly.

Also garnering 3 hits each were Mark Totta, Andy Bone, and Jim Wolfe. Al Ratini slapped 2 hits and picked up 3 ribbies. Bob Jadick also had 2 hits for the Hippos.
