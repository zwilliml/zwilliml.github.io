2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Shade Leon's 9-7

Saints Sweep Doubleheader

St. Joe staged a 5-run 6th-inning rally to erase a 6-3 deficit and post an impressive 9-7 victory over a highly rated Leon's team that had entered the game with a 13-4 record and second place in the other division. The Saints improved their record to 10-3 with this win in the lidlifter of a doubleheader sweep, defeating Chassis in the nightcap.

The Holy Ones scored 3 in the second, but Leon's answered with 4, then retired 8 consecutive Hippos as they stretched their lead to 6-3. But the Shepherds rallied in the 6th with 5 runs driven in by 5 different players on 7 hits, all singles, then held on with solid defense for the win.

Bryan Channell and Mike Welsh each had perfect 2-for-2 games plus an rbi. Also garnering 2 hits each were Dean Longwell, Mike Zwilling, and Ed Freisen.
