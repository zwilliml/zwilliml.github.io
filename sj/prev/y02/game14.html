2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Swamp Chassis 14-4

Saints Sweep Doubleheader

St. Joe completed a doubleheader sweep by overwhelming Chassis Lounge 14-4 in a game halted early by the 10-run rule. Chassis featured several former Hippos, including Roy Shafer, Sean Young, and Mike Sandor.

The game did not start well for the Saints, as Chassis scored 4 runs in the top of the first before anybody was out, and were in danger of scoring more when the Shepherds' Dean Longwell made a sensational diving grab of a Chassis bloop fly then threw out a baserunner for a double play.

This play, along with Mike Slabaugh's leadoff hustling triple in the bottom of the first, changed the complexion of the game and shifted the momentum to the Holy Ones, who went on to score every inning and not allow another run. When Hippo second baseman Mike Zwilling fielded a grounder, tagged the runner from first, and threw out the batter for another double play in the 5th, it was the final nail in the coffin.

Five Saints had perfect 2-for-2 games, including Slabaugh who added a second triple. Andy Bone doubled and tripled, while Dean Longwell, Bryan Channell, and Rob Medallis posted the other perfectos.

George Zaczyk slammed a 3-run homer in his only appearance at the plate, while Al Ratini was perfect with 1-for-1 and a run scored.
