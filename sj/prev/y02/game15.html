2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Swallow Bitter Pill

Controversial Loss to Cox 10-8

St. Joe suffered a crippling blow to their first-place dreams as they dropped a 10-8 decision to Cox Contracting. The loss gave the Hippos 4 losses to first place Quality Machine's 3. Cox has lost 5.

Bad calls hurt the Hippos. In the top of the first, Mark Totta slid home with what appeared to be the first run of the game as the catcher dropped the ball. But the umpire was positioned behind the catcher and didn't see the ball rolling around. Later in the game the ump missed a jugglling act by the Cox second baseman as he crossed the bag for a force out.

But Cox displayed a stingy defense that did not donate runs to the Saints like many other Continental League teams. And their offense stitched together some of their runs on bloop hits and Hippo miscues.

Dean Longwell continued his torrid hitting for the Shepherds, going 3-for-3. The man has an astonishing 31 hits in 38 at bats this season for a league-leading .816 batting average! He has his patented inside-out swing working to perfection. No Hippo has ever hit this well.

Mark Totta was 2-for-3 with a double. Troy Heckathorn also stroked 2 hits. Mike Zwilling tripled home 2 runs.
