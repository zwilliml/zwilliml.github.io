2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Contractors Thrash Hippos 20-9

Too Much Offense

Cox Contractors walloped St. Joe with a potent offense and smashed the Hippos 20-9 in a game shortened by the 10-run rule. The loss ended the regular season with the Saints in a tie with Cox and other teams with a record of 13-5.

There were few bright spots for the Holy Ones, but Dean Longwell was perfect again with 3-for-3 including 2 doubles, as was Andy Bone. Mike Slabaugh added 2 hits.

Weather note: This game started on Monday, then the rains came in the 4th inning, and the game had to be finished on Wednesday. The Hippos have had unbelievably bad luck with rain, as 10 games were postponed by rain, and this is one of the driest summers on record! This doubleheader alone was rained out 3 times, and it deprived the Hippos of 2 games against Horseshoe Bar, who chose to forfeit rather than conclude their dismal season against the strong Saints. Their games had originally been scheduled for early in the season when they would have played rather than forfeit. But because of the rain, the Hippos play 2 fewer games, they don't get to fatten their batting averages against Horseshoe like every other team did, and several Hippo players don't get enough official at bats to qualify for the league leaders.
