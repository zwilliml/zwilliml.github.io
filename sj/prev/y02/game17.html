2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Cox Sends Hippos To Loser's Bracket 19-12

Contractors Have Too Much Offense

St. Joe lost their third consecutive game to Cox Contracting, who once again swamped the Hippos with hits and runs. The loss in the first round of the playoffs sent the Holy Ones careening to the loser's bracket.

The Saints thought they had a good 5th inning going, scoring 8 runs to take a brief 12-8 lead. But Cox unleashed a barrage of hits in their half of the 5th and scored a crushing 11 runs to sink the Holy Ones.

Ed Freisen led the Shepherds with a perfect night, going 3-for-3 and crushing 2 triples, garnering 4 rbi's in the process. Dean Longwell went 3-for-4, a great night for most people, but it lowered his batting average since he was hitting over .800 going into the game.

Mike Zwilling was perfect at the plate, with a double and 2 sac flies. Troy Heckathorn was also a perfect 1-for-1, with an rbi single. Mike Slabaugh smashed a 2-run homer.
