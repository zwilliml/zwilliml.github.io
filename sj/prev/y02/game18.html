2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

7th Inning Rally Lifts Hippos To Playoff Victory

Bone Ties It, Ratini Wins It, 11-10 Over JH Construction

St. Joe rallied from a 10-8 deficit to score 3 runs in the bottom of the 7th and eliminate JH Construction from the playoffs in Friday's game at POW-MIA Field (Packard #4).

The Saints never led in this game until Al Ratini's dramatic game-winning, 2-out, rbi single in the bottom of the 7th drove home Andy Bone with the triumphant tally that touched off a St. Joe celebration.

With 2 outs and a man on first, trailing 10-8, the outlook wasn't brilliant for the Hippos, but then Dean Longwell singled, and Andy Bone got the big blow as he scorched one to the fence that burned the JH outfield for a booming 2-run game-tying double. Then Ratini whacked the first pitch up the middle and Bone carried home the winning run.

Longwell and Mike Slabaugh were each 3-for-4 for the Holy Ones, while Mike Zwilling was a perfect 2-for-2.
