Saint Joseph Church

2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Manhandle Quality Machine Jr. 15-2

10-Run Third Seals Victory

St. Joe had no trouble defeating Quality Machine Jr., thanks to a 10-run 3rd inning that began with 8 consective singles by the hot-hitting Saints. The Holy Ones had 19 hits in only 29 at-bats for the game.

Dean Longwell and Andy Bone led the Shepherds, each going a perfect 3-for-3. Longwell had a double and a triple, while Bone also clouted a triple.

Ed Freisen was also perfect with 2-for-2 and 2 rbi's. Al Ratini and Mike Zwilling added 2 hits each.
