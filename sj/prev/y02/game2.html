2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Edge Montville Plastics 13-10

Big Inning Lifts Saints

St. Joe used a 9-run 12-hit 5th inning to erase a 6-2 deficit, then held on to defeat Montville Plastics 13-10, raising the Holy Ones' season record to 2-0.

The Plastic Men mounted a 4-run comeback in the 6th, but a 7th inning rally was snuffed by a game-ending double-play as Mike Zwilling snagged a line drive at third and doubled off the runner at first to seal the victory.

Dean Longwell led the Shepherds with a perfect 4-for-4, including 2 doubles. Jim Wolfe was also perfect with 3-for-3. Head Hippo Al Ratini had 3 rbi's on 2 hits, while Zwilling garnered 2 ribbies on 2 hits.

Also with 2 hits each were Mike Slabaugh, Mike Welsh, and Troy Heckathorn. Eric Noebe and Mark Totta each had 2 rbi's for the Hippos, while Ed Freisen blasted a triple.
