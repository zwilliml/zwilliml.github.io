2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Rally in 7th To Beat Leon's 23-22

Come From Behind Again

St. Joe staged a dramatic 3-run rally in the bottom of the 7th to come from behind and defeat Leon's 23-22 in a win that propelled the Saints to the semifinals in the playoffs.

This was a well-played game between two evenly matched clubs. Offense dominated in this see-saw affair that saw both teams score every inning but one to make the lead exchange hands several times. As the game went on, it began to look like whoever batted last would have the advantage, and luckily that was the Hippos.

Head Hippo Al Ratini several times expressed his displeasure with the umpire's calls of illegally high pitches (Cal was the ump), but Ratini adjusted and the Holy Ones appeared to get the benefit of the ump's tightness as they garnered 8 walks, including 2 in the fateful 7th inning. After Eric Noebe's sacrifice fly brought the Shepherds within one run, and Mike Slabaugh's rbi double tied the game and put men on second and third with one out, Leon's was faced with a near-impossible task engineered by the genius of Ratini's line-up: face the league's 4th leading hitter - Mark Totta - with a base open, or walk him and face the league's leading hitter Dean Longwell.

Leon's elected to intentionally walk Totta, but Longwell came through again for the Saints as he has all season long, grounding a tough bouncer to the first baseman who could not throw home in time as Mike Welsh slid home with the winning run.

Hitting stars abounded for the Shepherds: Bob Jadick was a perfect 4-for-4 with 3 rbi's. Longwell also had 4 hits, including the game-winner. Ed Freisen was a perfect 2-for-2 with a double, 2 sac flies, and 4 ribbies.

Bill Channel had 3 hits, including a big 2-out, 2-run homer in the 6th that gave the Hippos another temporary lead. Ratini also had 3 hits, one of them a triple. Garnering 2 hits each were Mike Slabaugh, Mark Totta, Andy Bone, and Jim Wolfe. Mike Zwilling was 1-for-1 plus 3 walks.
