2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Eliminated by Fiesta 22-12

Finish Playoffs in Third Place

St. Joe bowed out of the playoffs as Fiesta sported a blistering offense to blast the Hippos 22-12, ending the game in 5 innings by the 10-run rule.

The Saints had a pretty good offense going, with 20 hits in just 5 innings, and they rallied from a 17-7 deficit to make a game of it at 17-12 going into the bottom of the 5th. But Fiesta just couldn't be stopped, as they had carloads of hits and scored runs at will.

Bob Jadick led the Saints with a perfect 3-for-3. Jadick had an amazing 8 consective hits to end the season. Troy Heckathorn was also perfect with 2-for-2.

Also garnering 2 hits each were Mike Slabaugh, Mark Totta, Dean Longwell, Al Ratini, George Zaczyk, and Jim Wolfe. Andy Bone blasted a 3-run homer.
