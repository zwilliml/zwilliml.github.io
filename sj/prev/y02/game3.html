2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Machinists Grind Hippos 15-5

9-Run 6th Sinks Saints

Quality Machine used lots of singles interspersed with well-timed extra-baggers to overwhelm St. Joe and hand the Holy Ones their first defeat of the season.

The Machinists broke open a close game with a 9-run rally in the 6th, and the Hippos could not recover. George Zaczyk's 2-run homer in the bottom of the 6th left the Saints just a run shy of averting the 10-run mercy rule, and they were forced to retire.

Zaczyk added a single to his blast, while Mike Slabaugh double and singled. Mike Zwilling stroked 2 hits for St. Joe.
