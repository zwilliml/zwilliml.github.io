2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Greatest Comeback in Hippo History

Down 16 Runs, Saints Beat Mauro 24-23 in Extra Innings

Trailing 18-2 and in danger of losing by the 10-run rule, St. Joe staged the greatest comeback in team history as Eric Noebe's dramatic 7th inning, 2-out, 3-run homer tied the game at 23-23, sending it into overtime where the Hippos won it on Mark Totta's sacrifice fly.

The Pizza Pocket Men built their huge lead on the strength of a barrage of singles in the early innings, but they contributed to their own demise with poor defense, including 2 dropped flies with the bases loaded, and poor pitching, giving up 11 walks.

The Hippos clawed their way back into the game with a gigantic 11-run 5th inning, featuring 8 hits, 3 walks, and the 2 big dropped flies errors. Then the Saints still needed the 5-run 7th inning outburst to tie the game and send it into extra innings.

Ed Freisen was a perfect 3-for-3 for the Holy Ones, while George Zaczyk and Bob Jadick also had 3 hits each. Head Hippo Al Ratini was a perfect 2-for-2 plus 2 walks. And contrast Big Al's no-walks-allowed pitching against the 11 free passes given by Mauro.

Jim Wolfe doubled and tripled, while Totta added a double, a single, and another sac fly to his game-winning fly ball. Also with 2 hits each were Dean Longwell, Rob Medallis, and Noebe. Mike Zwilling doubled, had 2 walks, and added a sac fly.
