2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Fiesta's 7-Inning Rally Sinks Saints 9-8

Hippos Can't Protect 2-Run Lead

Fortunes were reversed as the Hippos, who rallied to win the previous game, were the victims this time as Fiesta got 4 straight hits to begin the 7th, scoring 3 times and erasing an 8-6 St. Joe lead.

Fiesta used long bombs, including one off the 300-foot sign in left center, to build an early lead, but the Holy Ones erupted for a 6-run 3rd inning on 7 hits and a walk. However, the Saints squandered some chances to fortify their lead, for example hitting into a double play in the 7th with men on 1st and 3rd, one out.

Mike Welsh was 2-for-2 for the Hippos, including a double. Dean Longwell singled and doubled, while Jim Wolfe and Andy Bone had 2 hits each.
