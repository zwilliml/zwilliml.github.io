2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Handle TBD's 16-6

Invoke Mercy Rule After 5 Innings

TBD hit well in the first inning, garnering 4 runs, but St. Joe struck back in their half for 3, then steadily pulled away, featuring an 8-run 4th to subdue the TBD's 16-6.

The Saints racked up 15 hits and 7 walks in the 5-inning affair, and played sound defense.

Mike Slabaugh led the Shepherds with a perfect 3-for-3 night. Andy Bone tripled and singled, while Mike Zwilling slapped 2 hits. Veterans Al Ratini and Bob Jadick were each a perfect 1-for-1.
