2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Squash Hapless Firemen 18-0

Freisen's Triple Triple Leads Saints

Ed Freisen and Jim Wolfe led St. Joe's on a hitting frenzy that devastated Fire Foe 18-0 and ended the game after 5 innings on the 10-run rule.

The Saints put crooked numbers up on the scoreboard every inning as their offense roared in high gear. Freisen garnered a rare triple triple (3 triples) plus 5 rbi's, while Wolfe had a perfecto: 4-for-4, including a double and 2 ribbies. Mark Totta was also perfect, 3-for-3 including a triple and 3 rbi's.

Andy Bone homered for one of his 2 hits. Other Hippos with 2 hits each were Bill Channell, Mike Welsh, Bob Jadick, and Al Ratini.
