2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Blast Montville Plastics 27-18

Offensive Explosion Powers Victory

St. Joe amassed an amazing 27 runs on 31 hits including 8 doubles, 3 triples, and Mike Slabaugh's 2-run homer in a devastating 27-18 victory over Montville Plastics.

The Holy Ones trailed 11-3 at one point in the 3rd inning, but then went on a scoring binge that averaged 6 runs per inning for the last 4 innings.

Slabaugh led the Hippos with a perfect 5-for-5, with 5 runs scored, 3 doubles, and his homer. Also perfect were Dean Longwell and Al Ratini, each going 4-for-4.

The offensive hero list continues: Mark Totta was 4-for-5, including 2 doubles and a triple, plus 5 rbi's. Andy Bone had 6 ribbies on 2 doubles and a triple. Every Hippo had at least one hit.
