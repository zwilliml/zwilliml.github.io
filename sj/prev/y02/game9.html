Saint Joseph Church

2002 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Crush Cortland Trinity 15-3

Offense Impresses

St. Joe's offense was again in high gear as the Catholics downed the Baptists 15-3 in a game halted after 4.5 innings by the 10-run mercy rule.

Ed Freisen and George Zaczyk powered the Shepherd offense, each going a perfect 3-for-3. Freisen clouted a triple and Zaczyk smashed a double. Bill Channell and Andy Bone each tripled and singled. Mike Slabaugh boomed another 2-run homer. Jim Wolfe stroked 2 hits.
