2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Edge Trinity 13-11

9-Run 4th Lifts Saints

St. Joe opened their season with a tight 13-11 victory over Cortland Trinity.

After three sleepy innings, the Baptists staged a 6-run rally in the 4th, based on solid hitting. But apparently this outburst only served to awaken the slumbering Rhinos, as they came roaring back with a 9-run explosion of their own. The teams then traded runs for the remainder of the game and the Shepherds held on for a hard-earned victory.

Dean Longwell, league batting champ last year, picked up where he left off by going 3-for-3, scoring 3 runs, and adding 2 rbi's for the Rhinos. Andy Bone also starred, perfect with 3-for-3 including a double, and adding 3 ribbies.

Ellis Thompson went 2-for-2, and adding 2 hits each were Jim Householder, Jim Wolfe (who doubled), and Luke Grunder. Head Rhino Al Ratini was 1-for-1 plus a sac fly.
