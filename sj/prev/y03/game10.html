2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Stifle Mauro 12-4

Workmanlike Victory Over Pizza Men

St. Joe used a good defense and a smart but plodding offense to grind out a 12-4 win over Mauro Brand. The victory evened the Rhino record at 5-5 on the season.

Mauro was missing their regular pitcher, and the Shepherds capitalized on his replacement, drawing 8 walks, as St. Joe slowly built a decent lead. The Rhino defense, though not sorely tested by the lowly Pizza Men (1-8), was strong.

Mike Slabaugh had 2 doubles for the Holy Ones, while Jim Householder and Andy Bone each collected a double and a single. Luke Grunder chipped in with 2 hits, while Al Ratini and Mike Zwilling each were perfect with a hit plus 2 walks.
