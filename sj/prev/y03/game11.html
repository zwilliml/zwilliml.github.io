2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Turn Tables on James Funeral Home

Win 11-10 in Final Inning Over Morticians

St. Joe avenged two earlier final inning losses to James Funeral Home by staging their own late rally and holding on to beat the Morticians 11-10 in an tight, tough, exciting game. The win lifted the Saints' record to 6-5.

The Shepherds got off on the wrong foot as James scored 6 runs in the top of the first. But the Rhinos answered with 2 runs in the first and a big 7-run explosion in the second. James tied it in the 6th, but Mike Welsch's sac fly pushed across the go-ahead run in the bottom of the 6th, and the Holy Ones held on for the victory.

The Rhinos did not have a big offense with only 11 hits, but they supplemented it by drawing 6 walks from a Mortician pitcher exasperated by the ball-strike calls, while Head Rhino Al Ratinin walked no one. James also hurt themselves with 4 crucial errors, while the Saints played good defense, including a crucial double-play in the 4th when Rob Medallis snagged a Mortician liner and doubled off the runner at first to squelch a James rally.

Andy Bone had 2 hits and 3 rbi's for the Rhinos. Luke Grunder also chipped in 2 hits, while Dean Longwell was perfect with a hit and 2 walks, plus 3 runs scored and 2 ribbies.
