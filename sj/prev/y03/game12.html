Saint Joseph Church

2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Douse Firemen in Slugfest

Win 28-24 in Wild Contest

St. Joe used three gigantic innings and took advantage of some shoddy Firemen defense in amassing 34 hits for a big 28-24 victory over Fire Foe. The win raised the Rhino record to 7-5 on the season.

Fire Foe scored every inning, and the Saints' defense was less than stellar, but the Rhinos posted huge rallies 3 times: 11 runs in the 4th inning, 7 in the 6th, and 8 more in the 7th. The Firemen had several errors that contributed to their defeat.

Fire Foe led 5-3 after 3, 16-13 after 5, and 21-20 going into the final frame, but St. Joe just had too much offense.

Perhaps both teams were rusty--the Holy Ones were in their first game back after a record 11 consecutive games washed out by the floods that covered the fields, as the river got out during constant torrential rains, tearing down ball diamond fences and leaving debris everywhere - see the photo below. The fields actually dried out fairly quickly, but the lights wouldn't work so our games (which are usually at 8:00 or 9:00) had to be cancelled.

Four players had 4 hits each for the Rhinos: Andy Bone included 2 doubles and a triple, Luke Grunder a double and a triple, while Ed Freisen and Bob Jadick had 4 singles each.

Five Rhinos had 3 hits each, including Al Ratini with two triples in the last two innings, burning the Firemen outfielders and driving in 4 runs. Dean Longwell also had 4 ribbies with his 3 hits. Also with 3 hits each were Mike Slabaugh, Bill Channell, and Troy Heckathorn.
