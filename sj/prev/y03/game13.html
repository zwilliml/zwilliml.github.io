2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Beat Fifth Season 15-8

Saints Come Back in Last Three Innings of Lidlifter

St. Joe trailed 5-3 after 3 innings, then began a 3-inning surge to overcome Fifth Season 15-8 in the first game of a doubleheader. The win elevated the Saints' record to 8-5, one game shy of qualifying for the playoffs, with 5 games remaining in the regular season.

The Rhinos scored every inning, with 12 of their tallies taking place in the final 3 frames. Meanwhile, a tenacious Saint defense held the potent Restauranteurs to 3 runs in the last 4 innings. Fifth Season featured several former St. Joe players, like Sean Young, Roy Shaffer, and Mike Sandor.

Bob Jadick and Rob Bauman each went a perfect 3-for-3 for the Holy Ones, with Bauman including a triple. Ellis Thompson smacked a homer and garnered 3 rbi's, while Bill Channell was perfect with 2-for-2, including a triple of his own.
