2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Fall to Baseball Tech 15-13

Last Three Innings Doom Saints in Nightcap

St. Joe built a 6-3 lead going into the 5th inning, but Baseball Tech surged with a 6-run 5th plus 3 runs in each of the last 2 innings to power past the Rhinos 15-13. The loss left the Saints one game away from the playoffs with a record of 8-6, with 4 games left.

Bob Jadick and Rob Bauman starred for St. Joe, each going 2-for-3 with 3 rbi's. Bauman included a double and triple, while Jadick swatted a double. Bill Channell and Jim Householder also collected 2 hits each for the Holy Ones, with Householder blasting a solo homer in the first inning.
