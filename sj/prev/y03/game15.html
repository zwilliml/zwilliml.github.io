2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Reach Playoffs!

Come Back Strong Against O'Donolds 23-6, Sweep Doubleheader

St. Joe propelled themselves into the playoffs by roaring from behind against O'Donolds 23-6 in the lidlifter of a doubleheader sweep. The win gave the Saints their ninth victory of the season, earning them a place in the championship playoffs.

This game began in an ominous fashion on May 27, opening day of the 2003 season, as O'Donolds scored 5 runs in the top of the first. The Saints had answered with 2 runs of their own when the rains came, postponing the game for almost 2 months as multiple rescheduled resumptions were again rained out in this wettest season on record.

When finally the game resumed, the Rhinos were in prime midseason form, scoring 6 runs in the 2nd and exploding for 12 runs in the 3rd. The game ended early when the mercy rule was invoked.

Andy Bone and Ellis Thompson had great nights for the Rhinos, each going 4-for-4. Bone scored 4 runs and banged a double. Jim Householder was a perfect 3-for-3, including a triple and 2 rbi's.

Mike Slabaugh and Al Ratini each added 3 hits, with Ratini collecting 4 rbi's, while Luke Grunder blasted a 3-run homer and also racked up 4 ribbies.
