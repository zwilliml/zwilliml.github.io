2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Squash Paintballers 23-11

Sweep Doubleheader

St. Joe wrapped up a doubleheader sweep by blasting Maximum Paintball 23-11. The win in the nightcap lifted the Rhino record to 10-6, as the Holy Ones eyed the playoffs.

The Shepherds never trailed, and threatened to win by the mercy rule as they scored 19 runs in the last 4 innings.

Luke Grunder went 4-for-5 for the Saints, garnering 4 rbi's in the process. Al Ratini was perfect at 3-for-3, including a triple--the Head Rhino was 6-for-7 on the night.

Jim Householder had 3 hits and 4 ribbies, while Dean Longwell also added 3 hits. Every Rhino had at least 2 hits.
