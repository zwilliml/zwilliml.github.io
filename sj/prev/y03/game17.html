2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Stymied by Hannah's 14-10

Home Cooking Doesn't Suit Rhino Taste Buds

Hannah's Home Cooking once again handled the playoff-bound Rhinos by scoring early and often, building a 14-4 lead after three innings that St. Joe could not overcome. The Shepherds also hit into two double-plays to squash potential rallies.

Bill Channell led the Holy Ones with a perfect 3-for-3 night, plus two rbi's. Jim Wolfe had his own 2-for-2 perfecto. Jim Householder blasted a 2-run homer and a triple, plus a sac fly, garnering 3 ribbies in the process. Andy Bone and Ellis Thompson picked up two hits each for the Saints, while Dean Longwell and Ed Freisen each tripled.
