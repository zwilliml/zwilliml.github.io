2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Triumph Over Fifth Season 17-12

Saints Score Every Inning To Beat Back Restauranteurs

St. Joe scored every inning and had hits from every player to beat back the constant challenges of Fifth Season 17-12 in the last game of the regular season before the playoffs. Fifth Season featured several former St. Joe players, like Sean Young, Roy Shaffer, and Mike Sandor.

Fifth Season led briefly 3-2 and 6-3, but the Saints exploded for 8 runs in the third and answered every challenge after that to hold the pesky Restauranteurs at bay. The Rhinos amassed 24 hits in the onslaught.

Mike Welsch was perfect for the Shepherds with 2 hits and a walk, while Jim Householder, Jim Wolfe, and Ed Freisen slapped three hits each. Dean Longwell slashed two doubles, and two hits each were posted by Andy Bone, Ellis Thompson, Al Ratini, and Luke Grunder, who garnered 4 rbi's, including one on a sac fly.
