2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Edge Hannah's 7-6 In Playoffs

Get Revenge For Two Regular Season Losses

"Revenge is a dish best served cold" -- Klingon Proverb

The only home cooking being served up tonight was the revenge dish by the Rhinos, and Hannah's didn't much care for the taste as the Saints edged out a thrilling 7-6 victory in the first round of the playoffs, thus atoning for two regular season losses to the Restauranteurs.

This game was tight all the way, as the two teams matched run-for-run and hit-for-hit, with the game not decided till the last play, a deep fly ball gathered in by Mike Slabaugh near the fence with the tying run on first moving with two outs.

The Holy Ones crafted the victory from only 10 hits, including two each from Slabaugh, Dean Longwell, and Jim Wolfe. Jim Householder and Andy Bone drove in 2 runs each, as did Wolfe.
