Saint Joseph Church

2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Quality Machine Tames Rhinos 12-11

Teams Trade 6-Run Rallies

Archrival Quality Machine squeeked out a narrow 12-11 victory over the Rhinos, evening the Saints' record at 1-1.

St. Joe staged a 6-run rally in the 6th to take an 11-6 lead, but the Machinists bounced right back with a 6-count of their own, then held on in the 7th as the Rhinos put men on first and third with two out but couldn't push the tying run across.

Andy Bone led the Saints with a perfect 3-for-3, including a triple, 3 runs scored, and 2 rbi's. Dean Longwell added 3 hits, one of them a double. Adding 2 hits each were Mike Slabaugh, Jim Householder, Bob Jadick, and Troy Heckathorn. Jim Wolfe and Bryan Channell drove home 2 runs each.
