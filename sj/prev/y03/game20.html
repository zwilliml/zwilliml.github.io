Saint Joseph Church

2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Firemen Extinguish Rhinos 15-10

Toss Saints to Loser's Bracket

St. Joe fell behind 9-1 early and struggled to come back but ultimately failed as Fire Foe doused the Shepherds 15-10 and sent the Holy Ones to the loser's bracket of the double-elimination playoffs.

A sixth inning Rhino rally was squelched when Ellis Thompson walked off the field, so with nobody on the bench the Saints were forced to take an automatic out when his spot came up with two on and one out. Thompson was apparently irked at being assigned ep duties, but he was not half as steamed as Head Rhino Al Ratini and the rest of the Shepherds at the AWOL act.

Troy Heckathorn doubled and tripled to lead the Saints. Five other Rhinos garnered two hits each: Mike Zwilling, Mike Welsch, Thompson, Luke Grunder, and Dean Longwell. Andy Bone tripled and had 2 rbi's.
