2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Eliminated by Grove Title 11-9

Crucial DP Saps Life From Saints

St. Joe bowed out of the playoffs in a tight 11-9 loss to Grove Title. The Saints finished the year with a fine 12-9 record.

The game was tied 9-9 heading into the fifth, as both teams had scored every inning. St. Joe loaded the bases with none out and a chance to blow the game wide open, but a force-out at home and a killer double-play ended the inning and seemed to sap the life out of the Shepherds, who never threatened again. Grove scored twice in their half of the frame and ran out the game.

Jim Householder and Dean Longwell led the Rhinos with 3 hits each. Householder included a double and 2 rbi's. Mike Zwilling had a double and a single, while Luke Grunder doubled twice. Al Ratini added two hits. Every Rhino had at least one hit.
