2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Sweep Doubleheader!

Saints Drub Mauro Brand 16-5 in Lidlifter

St. Joe dominated the night as they swept a doubleheader, employing the mercy rule in both games.

The Rhinos started off with an easy 16-5 win over Mauro Brand. A 7-run 4th seemed to demoralize the Pizza Men, and a 6-run 6th finished them off.

Jim Householder and Luke Grunder starred for the Holy Ones, with 3 hits each. Grunder had 4 rbi's, while Householder scored 3 times.

Six Rhinos had 2 hits each: Mike Slabaugh, Jim Wolfe, Mike Welsch, Rob Medallis, Ellis Thompson, and Bob Jadick. Ed Freisen belted a triple and added 3 ribbies.
