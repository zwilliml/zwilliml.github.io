2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Blow Away Boomers 23-9

Post 11-Run Explosion in 2nd

St. Joe swept a doubleheader in convincing fashion by shocking Baseball Tech/Boomers 23-9 in the nightcap.

The Rhinos ignited the inferno with 21 runs in the first 3 innings, including an 11-run conflagration in the 2nd. The Boomers contributed to their own demise with a porous defense.

The Rhino heroes started with Andy Bone, a perfect 3-for-3, with 4 rbi's. Head Rhino Al Ratini was also flawless, going 1-for-1 plus a walk and 2 runs scored. And Rob Medallis had a perfecto, 2-for-2 plus 3 ribbies.

Adding 2 hits each were, Mike Slabaugh, Jim Householder, Luke Grunder, Ed Freisen, and Mike Welsch.
