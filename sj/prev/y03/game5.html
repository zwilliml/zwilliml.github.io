2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Falter, Lose 15-5

Don't Like the Taste of Hannah's Home Cooking

Hannah's Home Cooking did not look that formidable, losing the first game of their doubleheader by the 20-run rule against Fifth Season Restaurant. Perhaps the Rhinos became over-confident seeing that and coming off their own convincing doubleheader sweep of 2 nights ago.

In any case, the Saints were cold against Hannah's and could not get anything going, as they were never in this game. And Hannah's employed crucial double-plays in each of the first 2 innings to squelch Rhino rallies and deflate the Saints.

Two bright spots for the Holy Ones were Jim Householder and Ed Freisen, both perfect with 3-for-3. Mike Slabaugh and Dean Longwell did their share by adding 2 hits each.
