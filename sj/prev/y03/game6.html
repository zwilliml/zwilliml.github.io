2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Look Sharp in 14-10 Victory Over Grove

Bone, Zwilling Get 4 Hits

St. Joe played their finest game of the year so far in an impressive 14-10 win over a strong Grove Title team.

The Rhinos exhibited a ruthless offense, scoring every inning but the last, in a relentless drumbeat of hits that wore down the Lawyers.

And the Saint defense was also very strong, with no errors and fine use of fundamentals, such as hitting the cut-off man, that minimized the damage done by Grove.

Andy Bone and Mike Zwilling led the Rhinos with 4-for-4 nights. Bone included a triple, and Mike Z. added 2 rbi's. Ed Freisen was also perfect with 3-for-3 plus a walk.

Bill Channell slapped 3 hits, one of them a triple, while the Holy Ones got 2 hits each from Mike Slabaugh, Jim Householder, Jim Wolfe, and Big Al Ratini.
