Saint Joseph Church

2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Swallow Bitter Pill

Lose 13-12 in Final Inning to James Funeral Home

St. Joe waged and lost a see-saw battle against James Funeral Home, in one of those games where you want to bat last. The Morticians won it in the bottom of the 7th, scoring 2 runs for a heart-breaking 13-12 Rhino loss.

Both teams scored most every inning. After 4 innings, the Saints led 7-6, then trailed 9-7, then led 10-9, then trailed 11-10, then led 12-11 going into the bottom of the 7th.

In the fatal final frame, an infield throwing error allowed a man to reach and go to second. Then a sharp single to the outfield went through for a game-ending error as the batter circled the bases. This, plus several earlier Shepherd miscues, had head Rhino Al Ratini steaming, as he appeared to be on the verge of drumming up some business for the Morticians.

James had their own costly errors in the 6th, allowing all 3 unearned Rhino runs that inning. But the Morticians also turned 2 double plays to aid their cause.

Jim Wolfe and Bob Jadick led the way for the Saints, each with a perfect 3-for-3. Five other Rhinos contributed 2 hits each. The Saints could amass no extra-base hits in the game.
