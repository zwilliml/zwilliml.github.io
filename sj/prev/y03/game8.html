2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Leon's Crushes Rhinos

Explosion of Hits in 21-10 Debacle

Leon's unleashed a nearly unprecedented barrage of hits in a 21-10 drubbing of the Saints. Starting with 7 quick runs in the top of the first, the Barmen were absolutely unstoppable as they rapped out hit after hit, in huge bunches.

Last year, the Shepherds eeked out narrow victories of 9-7 and a thrilling 23-22 win in the playoffs. But Leon's got their revenge this year as St. Joe couldn't get anybody out. The Barmen had dozens of ground ball hits that went perfectly in-between infielders, in what was frankly a big dose of luck and over-their-heads playing, scoring every inning before the 10-run mercy rule took effect.

Dean Longwell and Jim Householder went 3-for-3 for the Saints, while Jim Wolfe had 2 hits, including a double. Mike Slabaugh blasted a 3-run homer for the Rhinos.
