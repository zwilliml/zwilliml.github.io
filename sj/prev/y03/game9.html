2003 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

History Repeats - Another 7th Inning Loss

Lose 10-9 in Final Inning to James Funeral Home

St. Joe repeated the heart-breaker of last week, once again carrying a 1-run lead into the bottom of the 7th against the same James Funeral Home, only to see the Morticians once again score twice to steal the victory from the Rhinos' grasp.

Head Rhino Al Ratini assigned Bob Jadick to the mound this time, but Jadick suffered the same fate that Ratini had the week before, in much the same fashion. The Morticians once again employed 2 deadly double-plays against the Shepherds.

Andy Bone and Jim Wolfe were each 2-for-2 for the Saints. Bone had a double, 2 runs, and 2 rbi's. Dean Longwell and Luke Grunder chipped in with 2 hits each.
