Special Continental League Rules and Notes

Playoffs
  1. Playoffs will be double elimination.
  2. Teams with a .500 record or better will be in the Championship Playoffs, and teams with a below .500 record will be in the Consolation Playoffs.
  3. Any team that forfeits a game will not be eligible for the playoffs unless they pay a $25 forfeit fee.

Batting
  1. All batters start with a 1-1 count. With two strikes, one foul ball is allowed, but on the second foul the batter is out.
  2. Any ball hit over the fence will be ruled an out.

Other Special Team Rules
  1. There is a $15 ejection fee for unsportsmanlike conduct.
  2. Two forfeits and the team may be dropped from the league.
  3. There is a 10-run rule after 4.5 or 5 innings.
  4. There is a 20-run rule after 3.5 or 4 innings.
  5. Teams may start a game with 9 players, but the tenth position in the lineup will become an automatic out.
  6. If a player hits a ball out of play and the ball is unretrieveable, then that team should provide a new backup ball to the umpire.
