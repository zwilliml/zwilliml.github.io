2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Split Doubleheader Season Opener

Saints Fall to Goldberg Construction 12-6 in Lidlifter

Goldberg Construction scored every inning but one as St. Joe just could not hold them down, succumbing 12-6 in the season opener.

Bob Jadick was the only Rhino with more than one hit, going a perfect 2-for-2. Jeff Wilson had 2 rbi's on a first inning single, as the Saints kept it close for 3 innings before Goldberg pulled away.
