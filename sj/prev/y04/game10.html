2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Quality Machine Handles Rhinos 10-3

Power Outage Dooms Saints

St. Joe could muster only 3 runs on 11 hits, all singles, as Quality Machine easily disposed of the powerless Shepherds 10-3.

The Machinists used two double-plays to squelch Rhino rallies, and had 2 big innings of 5 runs each to account for their offense, which was not dominating.
But St. Joe's puny offense could never get them in the game. After the game, Big Al administered a tongue-lashing to the Rhinos, saying that they looked so dead he needed to call the coroner.

Four Saints contributed two hits each: Brad Dangerfield, Jeff Wilson, Paul Pal, and Mike Zwilling.
