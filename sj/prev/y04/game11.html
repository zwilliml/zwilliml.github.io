2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Slovaks Tame Rhinos 19-9

Saints Can't Keep Up With Undefeated Slovak Club

St. Joe played with some spirit but could not match the undefeated Slovaks, losing by the 10-rule 19-9 in 6 innings.

The Saints fell behind 9-5 early, and the Slovaks extended that to 13-5, before the Shepherds put up 4 runs in the 6th to close the gap somewhat. But the Slovaks responded with 6 runs as the Holy Ones finally fell apart in the field, succumbing to the mercy rule.
Brian Zeh was a perfect 3-for-3 for the Rhinos, while Paul Pal homered and doubled. Al Ratini also had 2 hits, garnering 3 rbi's, and Jim Householder added 2 safeties.
