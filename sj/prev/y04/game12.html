2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Hammer Baptists 18-3

Offense Explodes with 25 Hits

St. Joe drubbed Cortland Trinity 18-3 in an impressive display of both offense and defense, improving their record to 5-7.

The Baptists were just coming off an impressive win over Goldberg Construction in the lidlifter of their doubleheader, but a 5-run rally in the top of the first inning by the Saints seemed to subdue Cortland, as they could never mount a serious threat and were never in the game.
Jim Householder and Brad Dangerfield led the Rhinos in offense. Both were perfect on the night, with Householder slamming 3 triples and 5 rbi's, garnering 2 of the rbi's on one sacrifice fly. Meanwhile, Dangerfield went 4-for-4 and racked up 3 ribbies.
