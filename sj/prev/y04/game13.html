2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Swallow Bitter Pill

Leon's Last-Ditch Rally Sinks Saints 3-2

St. Joe guarded a slender 2-1 lead all the way from the second inning to the 7th, only to see Leon's rally from a 2-out, nobody-on, dire situation to snatch a 3-2 victory from the devastated Shepherds.
Neither team was displaying any significant offense the whole game, as Bill Channell's 2-out, 2-run single in the second inning was the only scoring for the Saints in the game. It looked like it might hold up, as Leon's was doing nothing and had lost their first two batters in the seventh inning.
Bill Channell
But just as victory seemed within the Rhino grasp, the next three Leon's players went single-triple-single, and in the space of about one minute the one-run lead had evaporated into a one-run deficit. The Rhinos put two on in the bottom of the seventh, but couldn't push across the tying run.

Bob Jadick was perfect for the Saints, going 2-for-2. Also garnering two hits each were Brad Dangerfield and Brian Zeh. The Shepherds couldn't muster any extra-base hits in the game.
