2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Rally for Amazing Victory

Four-Run Seventh Dooms O'Donold's 5-4

St. Joe rallied for 4 runs in the bottom of the seventh to stage a dramatic 5-4 come-from-behind victory over O'Donold's Tuesday night. The must-win raised the Saints' record to 6-8, as the Holy Ones now need 3 wins in their last 4 games to make the playoffs.

St. Joe seemed demoralized after their heart-breaking 3-2 loss in the lidlifter of this doubleheader, and could manage only one meager run in the first six innings of this soggy nightcap, trailing 4-1 going into the bottom of the 7th.

But the previously stable O'Donold's pitcher started the fatal 7th by issuing his only two walks of the game, and they came back to haunt him.
Paul Pal followed the walks with a single to load the bases, then one out later Ed Freisen drove in 2 runs with a hit. Bob Jadick then singled home the tying run, and with two outs Bill Channell finished the rally and the game by drilling a single up the middle to send Freisen home with the winning run.

Troy Heckathorn was perfect for the Rhinos, going 2-for-2. Also adding 2 hits each were Pal, Freisen, Jadick, and Channell. For the second game in a row, the Saints could not manage any hits for extra-bases.
