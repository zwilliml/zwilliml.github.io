2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Survive Scare

James Funeral Home Rally Falls Short

St. Joe survived a 7th-inning rally by James Funeral Home to take an 8-7 victory that improved the Rhino record to 7-8. The Saints must finish with a record of at least 9-9 in order to reach the championship playoffs.

The Shepherds broke open a tight low-scoring affair with a big 6-run rally in the 6th, banging out 9 consecutive hits. The big blow was a bases-loaded triple by Bill Channell.


Bill Channell
The Holy Ones added a run in the top of the 7th to make it 8-3, but the Morticians chipped away at the lead with hit after hit, before finally bowing with runners on first and second.

Jeff Wilson and Tim Ross were perfect for the Rhinos, each going 2-for-2. Six other Rhinos added two hits apiece.

James Funeral Home featured former Rhino Ryan James.
