2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Another Stunning Rally Lifts Rhinos

Four-Run Seventh Dooms Fifth Season 17-16

St. Joe again rallied for 4 runs in the bottom of the seventh to stage a dramatic 17-16 come-from-behind victory over Fifth Season Tuesday night. It marked the third time this year that the Saints have rallied in the bottom of the 7th for 3 or more runs to gain a come-from-behind win.

The must-win raised the Saints' record to 8-8, as the Holy Ones now need one win in their last two games to make the playoffs.
This was the kind of game where you wanted to bat last, because the offense of both teams was firing on all cylinders. The Shepherds scored in every inning, and Fifth Season scored in every inning but one.

Fifth Season got a key 3-run homer in the top of the 7th to break a 13-13 tie. But Mike Zwilling ignited the winning rally in the bottom of the frame by drawing a one-out walk. Zwilling was a perfect 3-for-3 on the night. Six consecutive singles later the game was over, as Paul Pal applied the coup de grâce.

Brad Dangerfield led the Rhinos with a perfect 5-for-5, including a double and a 2-run homer. Jared Svette had a night with a perfect 4-for-4, including a triple, while Jeff Wilson was also perfect at 3-for-3.

Fifth Season featured several former St. Joe players, like Sean Young, Roy Shaffer, and Mike Sandor.
