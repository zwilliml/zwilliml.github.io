2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Sweep Into Playoffs

Saints Make Believers Out Of Believer's Christians #2

For the sixth consecutive year, St. Joe qualified for the WRA Championship Playoffs, this time by sweeping a doubleheader in dominating fashion by the 10-run rule in each game. The Rhinos finished the regular season with a record of 10-8, and a 5-game winning streak.
In the lidlifter, the Saints broke open the game with a big 8-run rally in the sixth, which broke the backs of the tenacious Believer's. Brad Dangerfield and Jim Householder led the Rhinos, each with a perfect 4-for-4. Dangerfield walloped two homers, and added a double with four rbi's. Householder swatted a triple.
