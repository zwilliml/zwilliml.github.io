2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Crush Alex's Cafe 20-5

Saints Win Fifth Straight

St. Joe ended their regular season with an exclamation point, slaughtering Alex's Cafe 20-5 in a final tune-up for the playoffs. The Holy Ones ended the season with a 10-8 record and five consecutive victories.
The Rhinos had big rallies every inning in the mercy-rule shortened affair. Once again, Brad Dangerfield led the Saints with a perfect 4-for-4 and 4 rbi's. Dangerfield slugged a 3-run homer and added a triple. Big Brad now has an incredible 15 consecutive hits, dating back 4 games, with 4 homers, a triple, 3 doubles, and an amazing 2.133 slugging percentage during the streak.
Brad Dangerfield
Garnering 3 hits each for the Shepherds were Jim Householder and Jeff Wilson. Householder had 2 doubles and a triple, while Wilson collected 5 ribbies.
