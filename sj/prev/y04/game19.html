2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Grove Title Too Tough For Rhinos

Saints Fall 17-9 in First Playoff Game

St. Joe lost their first game of the double elimination playoffs 17-9 to Grove Title, sending the Saints to the loser's bracket.

The Titlists (17-2) had their offense in high gear, scoring every inning. They broke open a close game with a couple of 5-spots in the 5th and 6th innings.

The Rhinos played decent ball, like when they threw out two runners at home on one play. Grove dropped a few flies in the outfield to give the Saints some unearned runs, but the Titlist offense was relentless.
Garnering two hits each for the Shepherds were Jim Householder, Rob Medallis, Brian Zeh, and Jeff Wilson. Householder and Medallis each had a double as one of their hits.
