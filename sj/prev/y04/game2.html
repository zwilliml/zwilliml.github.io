2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Get First Win, Defeat Believer's Christians #1 8-3

Split Doubleheader with Nightcap Win

St. Joe racked up their first win of the season by defeating Believer's Christians #1 8-3 in the nightcap of a split doubleheader.

After each team scored twice in the opening frame, the Saints tallied 3 runs in the second to take a lead they would never relinquish, pulling away steadily.

Jared Svette and Ed Freisen each went a perfect 2-for-2, with Svette including a double. Troy Heckathorn had 2 rbi's.
