2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Hammer Realtors 12-1

Dangerfield's Grand Slam Powers Saints

St. Joe breezed to the next round of the playoffs with an easy 12-1 win over Century 21. The Holy Ones had two big innings, while the Realtor offense was anemic.

The Shepherds built up a 7-0 lead in the fourth, then Brad Dangerfield capped the rally with a grand slam to seal the victory.
