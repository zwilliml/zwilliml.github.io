2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Bow To James Funeral Home 11-4

Saints Eliminated From Tournament

St. Joe ended their season with an 11-4 loss at the hands of James Funeral Home, their second loss in the double-elimination playoffs. The Holy Ones finished the year with a record of 11-10.

The Saints had a respectable 17 hits, but stranded 14 runners thanks to a stingy Mortician defense that turned three double-plays, squelching Rhino rallies.
Brad Dangerfield did all he could for the Saints, going a perfect 4-for-4, including a double (the only extra-base hit for the Holy Ones).
