2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Realtors Rally to Edge Rhinos 14-13

Saints Swept in Doubleheader Squeakers

St. Joe carried a 13-10 lead into the bottom of the 7th but couldn't hold it as Century 21 rallied for 4 runs to edge the Saints 14-13 in the lidlifter of a doubleheader.
The Shepherds had an impressive 23 hits and generally played well, but two Realtor double-plays squelched promising Rhino rallies in the 4th and 5th innings.

Jim Householder led the Holy Ones with 3 hits, including a triple, scored 3 runs, and had 5 rbi's. Bill Channell was also 3-for-4, plus an rbi.
