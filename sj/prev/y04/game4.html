2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Holiday Bar Survives Rhino Rally 8-7

Saints Swept in Doubleheader

St. Joe fell to 1-3 as Holiday Bar edged the Saints 8-7 in the nightcap of a doubleheader loss.

The Shepherds trailed 8-6 going into the final frame, then put men on first and third with just one out and potent hitter Jim Householder up. Householder had drilled a 2-run homer in his previous at-bat.

But this time Householder slugged a prodigious blast that just cleared the outfield fence, an automatic out in D-League, so instead of a 1-run lead the Holy Ones still trailed by two. Jim Wolfe singled home one run, but Mike Slabaugh's wicked liner was snagged by the pitcher to end the threat and the game.
