2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Top Trumbull Courier 13-10

Saints Split Doubleheader

St. Joe scored every inning but one as they beat back Trumbull Courier 13-10.

The Editors also scored in most innings, but the big rally for the Saints came in the second, as they scored 4 runs after starting the inning with 2 outs.

Jim Wolfe was a perfect 3-for-3 with 2 rbi's for the Rhinos, while Doug Cochran was 2-for-2 with 3 ribbies and a triple. Jared Svette was also perfect at 2-for-2, including a homer.
