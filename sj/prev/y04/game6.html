2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Bow to Mauro Brand 12-8

No Power in Holy Bats

St. Joe got 8 runs on 15 hits, but all the hits were singles, as Mauro Brand used some big innings to quell the Saints 12-8.

The Shepherds were never in this one as they fell behind early and could not mount a serious threat.

Mike Zwilling was a perfect 2-for-2 for the Rhinos, while Jeff Wilson and Rob Medallis added 2 hits each.
