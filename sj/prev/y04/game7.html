2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Dangerfield's Homer Lifts Rhinos

Saints Sweep Doubleheader with 7-th Inning Rallies

Trailing 8-6 in the bottom of the seventh, Brad Dangerfield blasted a 3-run walk-off homer to cap a dramatic 9-8 victory for St. Joe over Elston Drywall.

Dangerfield also sparkled on defense, making a diving catch in right field and doubling off the runner at first to squelch a potential Elston rally in the 6th.

The victory in the lidlifter was matched by another thrilling Rhino win in the nightcap.


Brad Dangerfield
Defense Shines

A stout Rhino defense played a large part in the triumph, as the middle-infield duo of Jared Svette and Rob Medallis twisted two double-plays, one in each of the first two innings.

Svette and Medallis also shined on offense, with each garnering two hits, a sacrifice fly, and an rbi.


Jared Svette
Rob Medallis
Head Rhino Al Ratini hurled the victory for the Holy Ones on the mound, while going 3-for-3 plus an rbi. Jim Householder doubled and tripled, adding a ribbie. Jim Wolfe and Brian Zeh each had two hits.

The Saints trailed 8-4 in the fifth before shaving the deficit to 8-6 heading into the final frame. Zeh started the 7th with a single, and one out later Paul Pal did the same, setting the stage for Dangerfield. His shot burned the right-fielder, and Brad scampered around the bases on Pal's heels.
