2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Rally to Beat BW3 15-14

Saints Score Seven in Seventh to Sweep Doubleheader

St. Joe exploded for 7 runs in the 7th inning to stage a dramatic comeback victory over BW3 by a score of 15-14. The win evened the Rhino record at 4-4.

The outlook was bleak for the Saints as they headed in to the 7th trailing 13-8, having looked flat for most of the game. But the Holy Ones strung together 8 singles and a walk, with the key hit coming off the bat of Ed Freisen, whose bases-loaded safety drove home the tying and go-ahead runs. Freisen had 3 hits and 3 rbi's in the game.


Ed Freisen
Jim Householder smashed a bases-loaded triple in the 5th, then scored to give the Shepherds a brief 8-7 lead, but BW3 came right back with 4 runs of their own, then 2 more in the 6th, to take seeming command of the game, until the Rhino explosion in the 7th.

Jeff Wilson had 3 hits for the Holy Ones, including a double, plus 3 rbi's, while Brian Zeh added 3 singles.
