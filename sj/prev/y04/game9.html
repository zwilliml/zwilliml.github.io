2004 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Titlists Thrash Rhinos 16-8

Grove Showcases Power as Saints Falter

Grove Title powered their way to an easy 16-8 win over St. Joe Thursday night at Vidu. The loss dropped the Shepherds back below .500 at 4-5.

The Realtors smashed a ton of extra-base hits, and added plenty of singles as they easily overpowered the Holy Ones. The Rhinos contributed to their own demise with some shoddy defense.
Jared Svette led off the bottom of the first with a home run for St. Joe. Jim Householder followed with a double, and was driven home by Jeff Wilson to tie the game.

But that was the last time the Rhinos could manage to get that close, as Grove continued to score every inning but one, leaving the struggling Rhinos in their dust.


Jared Svette

Svette added 2 more hits for the Shepherds, while Brad Dangerfield had 3 singles. Householder and Wilson each had 2 hits, while Rob Medallis had 2 rbi's from his single and sac fly.

The Saints staged a modest 4-run rally in the third to get back in the game, but eventually succumbed under the constant Titlist barrage.
