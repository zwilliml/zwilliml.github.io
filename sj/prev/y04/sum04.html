Quality Players

Story by Michael Heeter

Mike Cobb knows a few things about Softball, because he knows a few things about friendship.

"The thing of it is, we're all good ball players, but we're all friends too" says Mike, with a gleam in his eye.

Mike plays for Quality Machine, the 2004 D2 Champion team in the Warren Recreation Association. What's more, he's been playing with that team for 27 years.

The Team was founded in 1971 by Rusty Sewell, owner of The Quality Machine Shop in Warren, and second basemen/manager of the 33 year old Softball team.

Seeded third this year, the team didn't have high hopes starting 0-2, after coming off a six year slump. But after a 9-win stint the team went on to finish the season 14-4. Then, according to Mike, "peaked at the right time, in the payoffs", winning the double elimination tournament.

The key to victory is of course their longevity. Most of the players on the team have been there for 20-some years. They drink together after the game, and exchange gifts on the holidays. And every year, win or lose, Rusty Sewell still throws a party at the end. On getting together with his team, Mike has to admit "you know you're in for a good game."
