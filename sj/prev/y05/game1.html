2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Bushwacked 8-5 in Season Opener

Holy Offense Sputters Against Barmen

St. Joe dropped the season opener 8-5 to the Bushwackers.

The Barmen opened an early lead with a combination of singles and tweeners, but the Saints battled back to tie it, going into the sixth inning. The Bush Leaguers then exploded for four runs and the Holy Ones could not recover.

Nate Krol and Jeff Wilson had two hits each for the Rhinos, while Eric Rupert and Jared Svette each swatted a double.
