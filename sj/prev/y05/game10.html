2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Sweep Doubleheader

Defeat Dr. Jones Dentistry 10-7 in Lidlifter

St. Joe took a giant step toward the playoffs with a doubleheader sweep Monday night that raised their season record to 7-4 and gave them a 4-game winning streak.

The Holy Ones downed the Dentists 10-7 in the opener, maintaining a modest but consistent lead throughout the game.
Jim Householder had the big blow for the Shepherds, as he hammered a towering 2-run homer over the left field fence in the 4th inning. It was the first over-the-fence Rhino tater in years, because the home run rule was changed this year to permit at most one more blast than your opponents. Householder also added a single.
Jim Householder
Eric Rupert tripled and singled for St. Joe, while Bryan Channell doubled and singled. Zac Svette also had two hits.
