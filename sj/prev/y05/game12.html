2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Use Walks To Beat Cafe 83

Garner 13 BB's in 15-6 Win

St. Joe took merciless advantage of the Cafe 83 pitcher's inability to throw strikes as they drew 13 walks in the late innings to fashion a 15-6 victory that was closer than the score might indicate. The win placed the Rhinos just one victory away from qualifying for the Championship Playoffs, with 8 games remaining.

The Shepherds had fallen behind the usually hapless Restauranteurs 6-2 going into the top of the 5th, and the situation was starting to look serious. Since swinging the bat didn't seem to be doing much good, the Saints decided to rest the lumber on their shoulders, and immediately garnered so many walks that the runs practically manufactured themselves.
Jim Wolfe was not one to settle for bases-on-balls, as he led the Rhinos with 3 hits, 2 of them doubles. Bill Channell was perfect on the night, going 3-for-3, including a double, plus a walk.
