2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Sweep Into Playoffs

Saints Handle Trumbull Courier 11-3 in Lidlifter

St. Joe easily bested Trumbull Courier 11-3 to post their ninth win of the season, thus guaranteeing themselves at least a .500 record and a spot in the Championship Playoffs.

The Holy Ones scored 3 in the first, and steadily widened their lead against an impotent PaperBoy offense that garnered only a handful of hits against Head Rhino "Big Al" Ratini.

The Couriers did manage to get a rare triple play on the Shepherds: with the bases loaded and no outs in the sixth, Brad Dangerfield lofted a pop fly that the second baseman ranged far out to make a fine catch. Jared Svette tagged up at third but was gunned down at the plate by the second baseman, and the catcher then threw to first to triple off Dean Longwell sliding back into the bag. The play seemed likely to change momentum as the Couriers rushed off the field enthused and the Saints bummed at the squelched sure-fire rally, but the anemic Courier offense failed to press their advantage and they meekly inherited their fate.
Mike Zwilling was perfect for the Rhinos, going 2-for-2, plus 2 walks and 2 rbi's. Jeff Wilson had 3 hits and a walk, while Jared Svette had 2 singles and a double.
