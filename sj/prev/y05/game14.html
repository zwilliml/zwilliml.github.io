2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Trounce Baptists, Avenge Loss

Shepherds Invoke 10-Run Mercy Rule, 15-3

St. Joe streaked to their seventh straight win as they tromped Cortland Trinity 15-3 and upped their record to 10-4 in the nightcap of a doubleheader sweep.

The Saints thus avenged an earlier season 20-1 thrashing at the hands of the Baptists. In this one, the Rhinos had more hits and runs in the first inning than in their entire previous game with Cortland. A 5-run first and another 5-run rally in the 4th effectively sealed the victory.

Jeff Wilson led the Rhinos with a perfect 4-for-4, going 7-for-8 in the doubleheader. Jim Wolfe had 2 triples and a single, while Jared Svette had a triple and 2 singles.
