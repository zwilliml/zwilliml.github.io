2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Stun Florists 14-13

Win in Extra Innings on Dangerfield's Walk-Off Hit

St. Joe rallied with two runs in the seventh to tie it, then won it in the eighth, as Brad Dangerfield's two-out walk-off single plated Jim Householder with the winning run.

C's Floral struck first with four runs to start the game, but the Shepherds came right back with four of their own, followed by a five-run explosion the next inning. The Saints coasted along with a modest lead until the top of the seventh when, with two out and the game nearly over, the Florists suddenly exploded for five runs to take a two-run lead.

But the Rhinos refused to panic as they calmly manufactured two runs in the bottom of the seventh to tie, then won it in the extra frame on Dangerfield's dramatic blow.

Brad Dangerfield was the Star of the Game for the Rhinos with three hits, driving in the winning run, blasting a solo inside-the-park homer, and garnering four rbi's. Jim Householder also had four ribbies, generated by his two-run double and two-run triple.
