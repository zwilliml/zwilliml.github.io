2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Drill Dentists 18-7

Bill Channell Hits Two Homers

St. Joe easily defeated Dr. Jones Dentistry 18-7 as the Rhinos streaked to their ninth consecutive victory and raised their season record to 12-4. The game was called early by the 10-run mercy rule.
The Holy Ones had several offensive heroes: Bill Channell was 2-for-2, walloping two solo inside-the-park home runs and adding a walk, while Jeff Wilson was a perfect 4-for-4.
