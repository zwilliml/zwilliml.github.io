2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Florists Shade Rhinos 3-2 in Eight Innings

Winning Streak Snapped; First Place Imperiled

St. Joe bats went silent as C's Floral edged the Saints 3-2 in 8 innings, snapping the record 9-game Rhino winning streak, and setting up a must-win game on the final day of the regular season if the Holy Ones are to capture their first ever Division Crown.

The Rhino record now stands at 12-5 with one game left, while the Crushers finished their season tonight with a 13-5 record, and what the Saints hope is only a temprary hold on first place. If St. Joe wins their final game of the season against Pocket Foods, they will match the Crushers' 13-5 record, but the Saints will take first place because in head-to-head competition the Crushers beat the Rhinos by one in their first match-up, but the Saints defeated the Crushers by 3 in their second game.

In this game, the Shepherds could garner only 7 hits, while the Florists did little better. It looked like the Florists would eke out a 2-1 win in 7 innings, but with 2 outs and a man on second, Al Ratini came through with the big base hit to drive in the tying run and send the game into overtime. But the Holy power outage continued (the Saints had no extra base hits on the night), and C's Floral pushed across the winning run in the 8th, thus exacting a costly revenge for the Rhinos' great 8-inning victory over the Florists just last week.
