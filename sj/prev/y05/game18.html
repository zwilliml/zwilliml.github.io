2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

RHINOS CLINCH DIVISION TITLE!

Pound Pocket Foods 19-9

St. Joe won its first ever division crown by pounding Pocket Foods 19-9 in their must-win final game of the regular season. The Saints took first place by virtue of run margin in head-to-head competition with the Crushers, who had an identical 13-5 season record.

The Shepherds came through in the clutch and put this one away early, scoring an incredible 12 runs in the second inning, and eventually winning the game by the 10-run rule.

The Holy Ones had many offensive stars in this title game. Dean Longwell was a perfect 4-for-4 with 4 rbi's, including a double. Jared Svette had 3 hits, 2 ribbies, and scored 4 runs.

Eric Rupert pounded an over-the-fence two-run homer and an rbi triple, both coming in the 12-run second inning. Jeff Wilson had 2 hits and 3 rbi's for the Saints, and Bryan Channell also added two hits.
