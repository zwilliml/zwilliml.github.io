Rhinos Crush Dentists 14-1

Breeze to First-Round Playoff Victory

St. Joe swamped Dr. Jones Dentistry 14-1 as the Rhinos opened the playoffs with an easy win by the 10-run rule.
The Saints had five players who were perfect on offense: Dean Longwell and Eric Rupert were each 3-for-3.
