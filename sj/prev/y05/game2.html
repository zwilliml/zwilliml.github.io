2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Falter Against Crushers

Little Offense in 3-2 Loss

St. Joe could muster only 2 runs, one of them unearned, in a 3-2 loss to the Crushers.

The only Rhino runs came on an rbi triple by Jim Wolfe in the 6th, who then scampered home on an error on the play. The Saints threatened with men on first and third with two out in the seventh, but Mike Zwilling's liner was snagged by Crusher second baseman Mike Sandor to snuff the rally.


Jim Wolfe
Sandor was joined by teammate Roy Shafer and most of the former Fifth Season Restaurant team to make up the Crushers. Sandor and Shafer are former St. Joe players.

Some would attribute the lack of offense to the league switching to the yellow 44 COR softballs, which have been likened to hitting a sock. But the Saints will need to bust out of their offensive woes if they are to challenge for a playoff spot this year.

Jared Svette had no trouble on offense, going a perfect 3-for-3. Jeff Wilson was also perfect at 2-for-2 plus a walk. Eric Rupert pitched in with 2 hits. Head Rhino Al Ratini pitched a fine game, striking out 3 and yielding no walks.
