2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Baptists' Late Surge Sinks Rhinos 17-13

Shepherds Blow 11-2 Lead in Playoff Loss

St. Joe blew an 11-2 lead and suffered their first playoff loss in the double elimination tournament 17-13 as the Baptists scored 15 runs in the last 3 innings.

The Saints enjoyed a comfortable 11-2 lead after 4 innings and may have been having premature thoughts of another 10-run rule victory like the one over Dr. Jones just 2 hours earlier, when suddenly the momentum shifted and the Baptist bats came alive with a vengeance, while the Shepherds cooled off.

Dean Longwell, Jim Wolfe, and Brad Dangerfield each had 3 hits for the Rhinos, who must now battle back through the losers' bracket.
