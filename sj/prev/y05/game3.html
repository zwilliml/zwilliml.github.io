2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Roar to Victory

Crush Elston Construction 11-3

St. Joe notched their first victory of the season by cruising past Elston Construction 11-3.

Every Rhino had at least one hit as the Saints took an early lead and never looked back. Once again, Jared Svette led the Holy Ones on offense, with 3 hits.
