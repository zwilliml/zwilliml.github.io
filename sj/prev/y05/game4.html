2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Breeze Past Cafe 83

Mercy Rule Invoked By 17-2 Drubbing

St. Joe pounded Cafe 83 by a score of 17-2 in a game shortened to 5 innings by the 10-run rule.

Once again every Rhino had at least one hit as the Saints featured a 13-run explosion in the third. Jared Svette again led the Holy Ones on offense, with 3 hits, including a triple. Nate Krol also had 3 hits, plus 2 rbi's.


Jared Svette
Nate Krol
Jeff Wilson and Mike Zwilling were each perfect on the night in 3 plate appearances. Wilson had a hit and 2 walks, while Zwilling had a hit, a walk, and a sac fly. Five Rhinos added two hits each.

The league switched back to the livelier balls this evening, and it showed (at least for the Holy Ones).
