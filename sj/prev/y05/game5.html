2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Win 8-Inning Thriller

Out-Slug Tackett 19-14

St. Joe pounded out a season-high 26 hits, including 6 for extra bases, as they came from behind to beat Tackett Law/Goldberg Construction 19-14.

Big innings were the order of the day for both teams, with the Saints saving the biggest for last. The Lawyers led 10-5 going into the 6th, when the Holy Ones exploded for 6 runs to take a narrow lead. The Constructors tied it in the bottom of the seventh by staging a two-out, none-on rally.

But it all fell apart for the Tackett in the 8th, as the Shepherds scored 8 runs on 8 hits and several Lawyer errors, including a bases-loaded single by Nate Krol that skipped through the legs of the right-fielder all the way to the wall to clear the bases and effectively seal the game for the Rhinos.
Eric Rupert and Jeff Wilson led the Saints with 4 hits each. Rupert had 2 triples while Wilson had one.
