2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Baptists Trounce Rhinos 20-1

Shepherds Crumble Under Fierce Assault

It was the Reformation over the Counter-Reformation as the Baptists humiliated St. Joe 20-1 in a game ended early by the 10-run mercy rule (almost ended by the 20-run rule).

Trinity scored every inning on a barrage of hits, while the puny Saints offense could muster only 5 hits. The Baptists also hit an over-the-fence 2-run homer (this year you are allowed to have one more homer than your opponent, rather than being an automatic out as in previous years).

An 11-run Cortland first inning settled the result early. The game was delayed twice by lightning for about 10 minutes each time, and play was conducted in a light but steady rain.

Only Nate Krol could shine on offense for the Holy Ones, as he was a perfect 2-for-2.
