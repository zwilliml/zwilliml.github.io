2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Tackett Tames Rhinos 11-4

Holy Offense Sputters

St. Joe fell to 3-4 on the season as Tackett used a 6-run second inning to stifle the Saints 11-4. The Lawyers thus took revenge for a 19-14 loss to the Shepherds just two weeks ago that contrasted sharply the Rhino offensive output in the two games.
Al Ratini was perfect on the night for the Holy Ones, going 2-for-2 and adding a walk. Jared Svette and Brad Dangerfield added two hits each.
