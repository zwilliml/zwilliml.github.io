Saint Joseph Church

2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Crush Elston 20-9

Errors, Walks Hurt Constructors

St. Joe pelted Elston Construction with 22 hits en route to a 20-9 victory, but the Saints were helped along the way by several Elston errors and walks.

Six of the 20 Rhino runs scored directly on Elston errors, and the Shepherds also benefited from 4 free passes. Elston could field only nine men, but the Rhinos were unable to capitalize on the three-man outfield as they garnered only two extra-base hits, both doubles.

Elston took an early lead with a 6-run 2nd inning, but St. Joe recouped with a couple of 7-run innings to put the game away.
Jared Svette once again led the Holy Ones with 4 hits, while Jeff Wilson contributed 3 hits plus a walk. Jim Wolfe had 2 hits (one of them a double) plus 2 walks.
