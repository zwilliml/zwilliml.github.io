2005 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Shade Crushers 8-5

Saints Rally in 5th and 6th

St. Joe avenged an early season loss to the Crushers by staging 5-hit rallies in the 5th and 6th innings to win 8-5 and raise their record to 5-4 at the halfway point in the season.

The Holy Ones were nursing a 1-0 lead when the Restauranteurs rallied to score 3. St. Joe answered with 3 runs of their own, then followed with a 4-run 6th. Fifth Season replied with 2 runs but fell short.
Brad Dangerfield was perfect on the night for the Rhinos, going 3-for-3 with a double. Jared Svette also had 3 hits for the Shepherds.
