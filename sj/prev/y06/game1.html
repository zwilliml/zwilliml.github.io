2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Clobber US Safety 23-11

Score 11 Runs in 2nd Inning

St. Joe opened their season in fine fashion with a 23-11 drubbing of US Safety. The game was called after 5 innings by the 10-run mercy rule.

The Saints started off the game with 5 consecutive hits and 7 consecutive runs, then followed up with an even bigger 11-run 2nd inning in which the first 10 batters got 9 hits and a walk as they batted around.

Jim Householder led the Rhinos on offense, going 4-for-5 with a double and a triple, plus 3 rbi's. Kevin Exley had 3 hits, including a homer and a triple, while Nate Krol had 3 hits including a homer.
