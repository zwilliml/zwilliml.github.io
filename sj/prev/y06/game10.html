2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Struggle To Defeat Timken 19-14

Potent Offense Scores Every Inning

St. Joe struggled more than it seemed they ought to in overcoming a determined Timken Industrial 19-14. The potent Saint offense scored every inning, amassing 20 hits plus 9 walks and 2 sac flies, with 9 of their hits going for extra bases.

Timken led for the first half of the game, before an 8-run Shepherd explosion in the 4th inning put the Holy Ones in charge.
Mike Zwilling had a perfect night at the plate for the Rhinos, going 3-for-3 plus a walk. Ryan James was also perfect with 2-for-2 (a double and a homer) plus 2 walks. Jim Householder had a big night, with a single, double, and triple, for 3 rbi's.
