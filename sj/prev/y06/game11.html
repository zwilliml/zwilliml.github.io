2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Shine in 18-12 Win Over Team Rosa

Offense, Defense Both Click

St. Joe defeated a very tough Team Rosa 18-12 Wednesday night. Rosa was just coming off an easy 10-run rule win over Ianazone's Pizza in the preceding game, but Al Ratini held down their vaunted offense with his great pitching, while the Shepherds mounted another fine effort with their bats. Fortunately for St. Joe, Rosa's big number 4 batter hit 3 over the fence for outs.
Jim Householder had a perfect night at the plate for the Rhinos, going 4-for-4 with a double and a triple. Mike Slabaugh was also perfect, at 3-for-3 plus a sac fly.
