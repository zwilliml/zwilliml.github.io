2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Cruise But Lose

Fall 11-7 to Goldberg Construction

St. Joe seemed to be cruising to an easy victory, leading 6-2, as Goldberg could barely mount an offense, even making 3 outs on 3 pitches one inning. But then, inexplicably, the Construction Workers exploded for 9 runs in the last inning on a barrage of hits to stun the Saints 11-7.

"What happened?" asked Head Rhino "Big Al" Ratini. "They adjusted" replied Jim Wolfe.
Mike Slabaugh led the Shepherd with 3 hits, while Jim Householder tripled and singled, driving in 3 runs.
