Saint Joseph Church

2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Suffer Power Outage

Lose to Yacht Club 15-5

St. Joe struggled on offense as Mosquito Yacht Club cruised to a 15-5 victory Wednesday evening, in a game shortened by the 10-run mercy rule.

After scoring at least 20 runs in 5 of their first 6 games, the Rhinos have failed to reach that mark in their last 7 games, due at least in part to the offensive power outage. In this game, the Saints had only 10 hits, 8 of them singles.
Scott Daniels and Bill Channell each had 2 hits plus an rbi for the Holy Ones.
