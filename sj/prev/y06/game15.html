2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Win Tense Battle with BW3

Survive 14-13 Scare

St. Joe entered the 7th inning tied 11-11, scored 3 times, then held on as a furious BW3 rally fell just short, earning the Saints a hard-won 14-13 victory.

The Shepherds had one bad inning - the second - when BW3 scored 8 times. The BW3 pitcher didn't help himself, issuing 10 walks.
Kevin Exley had a night for the Holy Ones, going a perfect 4-for-4 with 2 doubles. Scott Daniels also shined, with 2 doubles, a 3-run homer, 6 rbi's, and 2 sensational diving catches in the outfield.
