2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Swamp Yacht Club 21-9

Avenge Earlier Loss

St. Joe avenged last week's 15-5 loss with a 10-run rule drubbing of their own, defeating Mosquito Yacht Club 21-9 in 5 innings.

The Shepherds had a big 8-run first inning, and an even bigger 9-run 5th against the listless Yachtsmen, who were playing the nightcap of a doubleheader.
Brad Dangerfield was perfect on the night for the Rhinos, going 3-for-3 with a double and 4 rbi's. Mike Zwilling was also perfect at 2-for-2 plus 2 walks.
