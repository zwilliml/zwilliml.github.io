2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Powerhouse Powers Past Rhinos 19-6

11-Run First Dooms Saints

Powerhouse (Callahan's) used an 11-run explosion in the top of the first inning to crush St. Joe 19-6 in a game shortened by the 10-run mercy rule. The Powermen thus avenged an earlier 10-run rule loss to the Shepherds, in much the same way that the Rhinos avenged an earlier loss to Yacht Club just the previous night.

Not living up to their name, however, Powerhouse relied almost exclusively on singles, many of the bloop variety, to score their many runs. But they amassed those singles in large quantities to swamp the Holy Ones.
John Manos was perfect on the night for the Rhinos, going 3-for-3 with two triples. Ryan James was also perfect at 2-for-2 including a triple. And Mike Zwilling had a perfect night with 1-for-1 plus a walk.
