2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Hammer Cleary's 22-13

Score 17 Runs in First Two Innings

St. Joe scored early and often then coasted to an easy 22-13 win over Cleary's. The first 8 batters for the Saints all scored, then 9 more runs crossed the plate in the second inning.

Mike Slabaugh was perfect at the plate for the Holy Ones, going 4-for-4. Scott Daniels also added 4 hits, and knocked in 4 runs, while Nate Krol had 3 hits and 5 rbi's.
