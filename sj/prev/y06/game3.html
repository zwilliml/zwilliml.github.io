2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Rout Plumbers 21-5

Win Third Straight; Remain Undefeated

St. Joe scored more than 20 runs for the third game in a row, blasting Carbone Plumbing 21-5 in a game shortened by the mercy rule. The Saints scored 6 runs the first inning and 8 more the second to put the game away.

Scott Daniels and Head Rhino Al Ratini were each perfect at the plate for the Rhinos. Daniels went 4-for-4 with 4 runs scored and had 3 rbi's, while Ratini was 1-for-1 plus 2 walks, and also had 3 ribbies.
