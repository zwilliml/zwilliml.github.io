2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Sputter, Fall to UAL 10-2

First Loss of Season

St. Joe's high-flying offense came to a screeching halt Friday night as they lost to Blackies UAL (University at Larchmont) by a score of 10-2. It was the first loss of the season for the Holy Ones, coming after 3 victories in which they scored 21, 22, and 23 runs.

Dennis Ellwood had the only multi-hit game for the Rhinos, with 2 hits and an rbi, while Nate Krol tripled for the only extra-base hit by the Saints.

The Shepherds were lucky to score any runs at all. Their first was unearned, and their second would never have happened if not for a base-running blunder by UAL in the bottom of the sixth, when a potential 10-run rule game-ending single was converted into the third out of the inning because the runner on first trotted into the dugout instead of second base, and was forced out.
