Saint Joseph Church

2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Come From Behind, Defeat US Safety 25-19

Jim Householder Gets Rhino Record 8 RBI's

St. Joe pounded used a relentless offense to come from behind and defeat US Safety 25-19. Jim Householder led the offensive firestorm with a record breaking 8 rbi's, eclipsing the mark of 7 ribbies in one game set by Bill Channell in 1999.

Householder belted a 3-run homer, clubbed two doubles, and had two sacrifice flies in his offensive rampage.


Jim Householder
The Rhinos trailed 11-2 early on, but kept putting up a steady stream of crooked numbers on the scoreboard, finally shutting down the Safety Forces in the fifth with momentum-shifting snags of two consecutive line drives. The Saints then piled on with their two biggest innings of the night to seal the victory.

Kevin Exley and Dennis Ellwood each had four hits for the Rhinos. Exley slugged a 2-run homer in the third, while Ellwood doubled and tripled.


Kevin Exley
Dennis Ellwood
Nate Krol also shined with the bat, slashing 4 hits, including 2 doubles and a triple, scoring 5 runs, and adding a sac fly.

Pitching played a big part of this game as well, with the US Safety pitcher issuing 7 costly walks, while head Rhino Al Ratini walked nobody. Ratini garnered 2 walks himself, and added 2 hits.
