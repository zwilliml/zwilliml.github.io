2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Crush Ianazone's Pizza 21-7

Score Record-Tying 14 Runs in One Inning

St. Joe exploded for a record-tying 14 runs in the 4th inning as they swept past Ianazone's Pizza 21-7. Not since a victory over Leon's in 2000 have the Saints posted so many runs in one frame. The win improved the Rhino record to 5-1 on the season.
Kevin Exley and Jim Householder led the Rhino offense, each going a perfect 4-for-4. Exley had a double and 2 rbi's, while Householder followed up his record-setting performance last game with a double, a triple, 4 runs scored, and 3 ribbies.

Jim Wolfe and Bob Jadick also shined with the bat, each going a perfect 3-for-3. Scott Daniels added 3 hits of his own, plus 4 rbi's. Daniels sports a spiffy .824 batting average for the season thus far, best on the team.
