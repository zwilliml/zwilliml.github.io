2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Romp Over Powerhouse 16-5

Improve Record to 6-1

St. Joe played a near-flawless game as they overwhelmed Powerhouse 16-5 in a game shortened by the 10-run mercy rule. Offense, defense, and pitching all clicked for the Saints as they played their finest game of the season.
Scott Daniels led the Shepherd offense with a perfect 3-for-3, with two doubles, a triple, and 3 rbi's. Daniels increased his league-leading batting average to an astonishing .850 (17 for 20).

Also sporting perfect 3-for-3's on offense were Nate Krol and Brad Dangerfield.
