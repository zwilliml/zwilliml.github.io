2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Warren Outreach Crushes Rhinos 29-11

15-Run Inning Dooms Shepherds

Warren Outreach reached out and crushed someone - the Rhinos. Outreach started off with a 6-run first inning, but the Saints gamely answered with 5 of their own. The Outreachers piled on more, but the Saints still stayed within striking distance - until the disastrous 4th inning, when Outreach scored 15 runs. It was one of the worst innings and worst losses of all time for St. Joe (but not **the** worst - see this game).
Jim Householder, Mike Zwilling, and Bob Jadick were each perfect at the plate for the Rhinos, going 3-for-3. Jadick is batting .889 in his last 3 games (8-for-9).
