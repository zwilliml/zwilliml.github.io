2006 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Swallow Bitter Pill

Lose To Ianazone's 12-11 in Last Inning

St. Joe led this game from the second inning till the last batter, but Ianazone's Pizza rallied for 4 runs in the bottom of the 7th to defeat the Saints 12-11. The Pizzamen won it with one out on a series of singles, and the defeat dropped the Rhinos' record to 6-3 on the season.
Mike Zwilling had a perfect night at the plate for the Holy Ones, going 1-for-1 plus a walk and 2 sac flies. Ryan James and Jim Householder each had 2 doubles.
