2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

UAL Edges Rhinos 16-15 in Season Opener

13-Run First Inning Dooms Saints

St. Joe mounted a furious 7th inning comeback that fell just short as they opened their season with a 16-15 loss to Blackies UAL.

The Holy Ones came out of the box strong with 5 runs in the top of the first inning, but then UAL responded with a 13-run explosion in their half of the first. The Rhinos shaved the deficit to 16-8 going into the final frame, when they scored 7 runs and had runners on second and third but couldn't push them across.

Jimmy Thompson led the Rhinos on offense, going a perfect 3-for-3 with a triple and a walk. John Guerrieri also had 3 hits for the Saints.
