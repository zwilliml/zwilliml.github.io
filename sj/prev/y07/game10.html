2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Powerhouse #2 Shuts Out Rhinos 12-0

Saints Suffer Complete Lack of Offense

St. Joe could muster only 3 measly hits in a 12-0 shut out by Powerhouse #2. Just to rub salt in the wound, all 3 were erased on double plays, one of which was inches from being a triple play. The game was called after 5 innings by the 10-run rule.

John Guerrieri, Jim Wolfe, and Mike Zwilling had the only Rhino hits.
