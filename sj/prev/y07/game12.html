2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Win First On-Field Victory!

Saints Defeat Callahan's 10-8 in Tense Battle

St. Joe finally broke through in the win column on the field, outlasting Callahan's 10-8 in a close duel. The Saints improved their record to 2-10, the other win coming via forfeit.

Each team featured a big 6-run inning and a smaller 2-run rally. Then the Shepherds tacked on singletons in the 5th and 6th that the Carpet-Layers couldn't match.

Alex Blake went 3-for-4 for the Rhinos, while John Guerrieri also had three hits. Six other Holy Ones sported 2 hits each.
