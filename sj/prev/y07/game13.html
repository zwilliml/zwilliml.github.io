2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Stun Wilson Construction 11-1

Saints Win By 10-Run Rule

St. Joe played a near-perfect game, easily defeating Wilson Construction 11-1 in a game called after 5 innings by the 10-run mercy rule.

The Holy Ones never trailed, as they scored every inning. Meanwhile, the Construction Workers never got their offense on track.

Eric Fenstermaker and Chauncey Harris each had a perfect 3-for-3 night for the Shepherds.
Eric Fenstermaker
Chauncey Harris
Kevin Exley went 3-for-4, including a 5th-inning 2-run homer that gave the Rhinos their 10-run margin. Al Ratini pitched a 4-hitter, striking out one and walking nobody, to hold the mighty Wilson offense in check.
