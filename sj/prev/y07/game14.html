2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Bushwackers Stop Rhinos 16-11

Saints Play Decent Against Top Ranked Team

The Bushwackers beat St. Joe 16-11, but the Rhinos played a decent game against the top ranked team. The Bushwackers just had too much offense, with a couple of 6-run explosions against the Saints.

Mike Zwilling had a perfect night for the Holy Ones, going 4-for-4. The Shepherds also got 3 hits each from Jimmy Thompson, Jon Bolha, and Bob Jadick, who also knocked in 4 runs.
