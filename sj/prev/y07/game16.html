2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Callahans Overwhelms Rhinos 19-11

Saints Fight Back But Falter

Callahans just had too much offense for St. Joe, posting a 19-11 victory over the Rhinos. Every time the Saints cut into the Callahan lead, the Pubsters responded with even more runs, including a 6-run 6th to drive the final nails in the coffin.

Jim Wolfe was an offensive force for the Holy Ones, going 3-for-4 including 2 booming triples over a disrespecting pulled-in Callahan outfield that didn't learn its lesson after the first time he burned them. The Shepherds also got 3 hits from Tom Volpone.
