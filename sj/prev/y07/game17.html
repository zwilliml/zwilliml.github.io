2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Sweep Doubleheader Against St. Demetrios

Crush Greeks 15-4 in Lidlifter

St. Joe ended the regular season in fine fashion with a doubleheader sweep of St. Demetrios, finishing up with a 5-13 record and sailing into the under-.500 playoffs on a high note.

In the lidlifter, the Rhinos roared out to a 10-0 lead in the top of the first inning on 7 hits and 4 walks, then never looked back as they breezed to an easy 15-4 victory, called after 5 innings by the 10-run mercy rule.
Bill Channell went a perfect 4-for-4 for the Shepherds, while Eric Fenstermaker had 3 hits and 4 rbi's.
