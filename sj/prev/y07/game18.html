2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Take Nightcap 15-12

Battle Back From 8-1 Deficit

St. Joe took the nightcap of the doubleheader sweep of St. Demetrios in a tough 15-12 come-from-behind victory.

Trailing 8-1 after 2 innings, the Rhinos scored 5 in the 3rd to get back in the game, then another 5 in the 5th to take command.
Al Ratini led the Shepherds with a perfect 3-for-3, including a booming triple plus 5 rbi's. Big Al also pitched both games of the doubleheader, walking nobody while the St. D. pitchers walked 14.

Eric Fenstermaker also went 3-for-3, while Bob Jadick also had 3 hits.
