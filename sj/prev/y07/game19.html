2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos End Year On Sour Note

Lose To St. Demetrios 10-6 In Playoffs

St. Joe ended their year with a 10-6 loss to St. Demetrios in the first round of the single elimination under-.500 playoffs.

The game and the year ended on a controversial double-play. With the Rhinos trailing 10-6 in the bottom of the seventh with one out and a man on first, Tom Volpone lifted a fly ball to deep right-center. The St. D. fielder retreated, gloved the fly, then fell backwards and appeared to lose the ball. Neither umpire signaled a catch or an out. The runner on first appeared to have held his base till the ball was touched, then he ran to third as the outfielder scrambled. The Greeks appealed, saying a catch was made and the runner left early. The umpires conferred with each other and called a double-play, ignoring Rule 10 which says that in just such a situation where the umpires put the runner in jeopardy that the runner should be returned to first base. Protests were to no avail.
Mike Zwilling led the Shepherds with a perfect 2-for-2, including a double plus 2 rbi's and a walk. Also sporting 2 hits each were Bob Jadick and Tom Volpone.
