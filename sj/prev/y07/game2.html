Saint Joseph Church

2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rhinos Fall to Wilson Construction 20-8

Wilson Scores Every Inning

St. Joe could not contain a hot Wilson offense as the Construction Workers pounded the Saints 20-8 in a game halted after 5 iinings by the 10-run mercy rule.

Wilson sported a 7-run top of the first inning from which the Rhinos could not recover. They added 4 more in the second and a six-spot in the 4th to seal the deal.

Jason Stouffer led the Rhinos on offense, going 2-for-3 with a triple for the only Holy extra base hit.
