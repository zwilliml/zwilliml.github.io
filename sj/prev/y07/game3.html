Powerhouse #2 Blasts Rhinos 22-2

Saints Can't Stop Powerhouse Offense

Powerhouse #2 overwhelmed St. Joe 22-2, scoring every inning. The game was called after 4 innings by the 20-run mercy rule.

Al Ratini was a perfect 2-for-2 for the Rhinos. Kevin Exley and John Guerrieri also had two hits each.
