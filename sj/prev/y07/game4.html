Bushwackers Trample Rhinos 23-1

Saints Losing Streak Extended to 4 Games

The Bushwackers lived up to their name as they hammered St. Joe 23-1 in a game shortened to 4 innings by the 20-run rule.

The Holy Ones could do nothing right on either offense or defense, garnering only 5 hits and a walk.

Jimmy Thompson and Jon Bolha each tripled for the Saints, while Mike Zwilling was 1-for-1.
