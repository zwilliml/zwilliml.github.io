2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Sons of Italy Dominate Rhinos 18-7

Saints Have One Big Inning

St. Joe had a mammoth 7-run outburst in the second inning to take a temporary lead, but never dented the scoreboard again as the Sons of Italy scored every inning in an 18-7 victory decided early by the 10-run mercy rule.

The only offensive action for the Holy Ones came from a sudden string of six consecutive singles, capped off by a bases-loaded triple from Rob Bauman, the only Saint with more than one hit.
