2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Powerhouse #1 Too Powerful for Rhinos

Saints Fall 19-6

Powerhouse #1 used a potent offense to subdue St. Joe 19-6 in a game called after 5 innings by the 10-run mercy rule.

After the Powerhousers started off the game with 7 runs, the Holy Ones nearly matched it with 6 runs of their own in the bottom of the first. But that was it for the Rhino offense, while Powerhouse was far from through.

Tom Volpone and Erik Fenstermaker were perfect at the plate for the Saints, each going 2-for-2. Mike Zwilling chipped in with 2 hits of his own.
