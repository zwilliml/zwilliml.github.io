2007 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Relentless BW3 Stops Rhinos 14-9

B-Dubs Score Every Inning

BW3 used a relentless offense to overcome a determined St. Joe team 14-9.

The Saints played a decent game, but just could not stop the B-Dubs' offensive train.

John Guerrieri led the Rhinos with a 3-for-4 night, including a big 7th inning 3-run homer over a disrespectingly shallow BW3 outfield, giving the Holy Ones a glimmer of hope at the end.
John Guerrieri
Brian Claggett had 2 hits for the Shepherds, including a double, while Bob Jadick also swatted 2 safeties, garnering 2 rbi's.
