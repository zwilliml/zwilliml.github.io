Dennis Ellwood

Man killed aiding foster son
Beacon Journal staff report
May 20, 2007

A Niles man was struck and killed by a van Saturday night while trying to get help for his foster son.

Dennis Ellwood, 46, of Vienna Avenue in Niles was in the Akron area visiting friends with his foster son, according to the Summit County Medical Examiner's Office.

When the two were in a car near the 4100 block of Manchester Road in Coventry Township at about 10:25 p.m., the foster son became upset, jumped out of the vehicle and laid down in the middle of the road, according to the medical examiner's office.

After Ellwood got out of the car to try to flag down oncoming vehicles, he was struck by a full-sized van, the medical examiner's office said.

Ellwood was taken to Akron General Medical Center, where he was pronounced dead at 11:15 p.m.

No further information was available.

The Summit County Sheriff's Office is investigating.
