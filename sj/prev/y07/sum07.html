2007 Season Summary

The Rhinos had a rough year, posting a regular season record of 5-13, then losing their first playoff game. They finished the year with a decent 4-3 mark in their last 7 games on the field.

Mike Zwilling led the Rhinos for the season in batting average, rbi's, walks, and on-base percentage. Al Ratini pitched every inning for the Saints, walking only 5 while the Rhinos drew 40 walks from opposing pitchers, a significant differential.

John Guerrieri led the team in hits and total bases, playing every inning of every game. Kevin Exley scored the most runs, and was second in total bases and in times on base.

Eric Fenstermaker was the team leader in slugging average and doubles. Chauncey Harris was second in rbi's and walks.
