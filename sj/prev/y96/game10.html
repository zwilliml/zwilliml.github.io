1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Stun First-Place Remodelers 12-9

St. Joe posted their biggest win in years by defeating the first-place Warren Home Remodelers 12-9. WHR's only previous losses had come in extra innings, but the Hippos needed only regulation to post their fifth win of the season, the most victories since 1989's 6-13 record, and we've only played 10 games!

Playoff hopes are now high with the spirit-lifting victory, as the Saints featured a splarkling defense supplemented by yeoman-like run production. The Holy Ones trailed 7-4 enetering the 5th, but posted 4 runs, with the go-ahead counter scoring from first as Mike Zwilling singled and occupied the Remodelers in a rundown between first and second as Bill Channel scampered home. Four more runs in the sixth solidified the win.

Ed Freisen went 4-for-4 to lead the Hippos, with a double and 3 rbi's. Freisen is tearing up the league with a .667 batting average. Chris Hanzel had two hits, including a two-out rbi single in the 2nd, and a 2-run triple in the 6th. Jim Wolfe and Dennis Downing added 3 hits each.
