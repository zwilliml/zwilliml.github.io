1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Rain, Big Innings Help Hippos Beat Army/Navy 15-13

St. Joe could score in only two innings, while Army/Navy scored every inning, but the Hippos triumphed by virtue of the big first and sixth innings, raising their season record to 6-5. It marks the first time the Holy Ones have been over .500 this late in the season since 1988's 9-8 record.

Army/Navy had scored two in the top of the 7th, had one on, and nobody out, when a thunderstorm struck and the game was called. The Soldiers were understandably upset that their comeback attempt was snuffed out by the rain.

The Saints scored 7 in the first and the other 8 in the sixth, with poor baserunning in between--two runners doubled off on liners, and two others thrown out by a mile unwisely trying to advance. But the Army/Navy pitcher hurt himself badly by walking 12 Hippos, 4 of which scored.

Dave Smith's 3-run homer in the 6th brought St. Joe within one run, then Ed Freisen tripled and scored to tie the game on Bill Channel's single. Al Ratini then singled, and Jim Wolfe's rbi hit put the Hippos ahead.

Smith, Freisen, and Steve Barnes each had 3 hits for St. Joe.
