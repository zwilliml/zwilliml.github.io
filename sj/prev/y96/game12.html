1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Edge Commercial Construction 19-18

St. Joe avenged an early season 15-6 loss to Commercial Construction with an extra-inning 19-18 victory over the Builders. The Holy Ones continued this season's impressive offensive display, scoring every inning but one. The victory raised the Saints record to 7-5, just 3 wins shy of the magic number needed to make the playoffs for the first time since 1988. 

The Hippos never trailed, but squandered an early 8-0 lead to find themselves tied 14-14 going into the 7th. Each team scored 4 times to send the contest into overtime.

Steve Barnes led off the deciding 8th with a booming triple, scoring the decisive tally on Mike Slabaugh's rbi single. The Hippos held on in the bottom of the 8th to preserve the triumph.

Mike Zwilling had 4 hits for the victors, while Ed Freisen, Al Ratini, Chris Hanzel, and Slabaugh had 3 hits each. The hot-hitting Freisen slugged two triples, and has at least 3 hits in each of his last 7 games for an astonishing .786 batting average during the streak.
