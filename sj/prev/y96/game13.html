1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Sloppy Win for Hippos 19-18 over D & K Roofing

St. Joe blew a 10-run lead, but pushed across the winning run in the bottom of the 7th to defeat D & K Roofing 19-18 and raise the Hippo record to 8-5.

The Holy Ones held a 17-7 lead going into the 5th, but the Roofers scored 4 in the 6th and 5 more in the 7th to tie. They threatened to take the lead, but they lost a runner on appeal who unwisely left 2nd base early on a fly ball that was the 2nd out.

The Saints began the decisive 7th with 2 walks, out of a ruinous 12 BB's the Roofing pitchers granted. A fielder's choice put runners on first and third, and a Steve Barnes sacrifice fly to left drove home the game winner.

The Hippos scored 10 runs in the first inning and had thoughts of coasting to victory in 5 innings. Dave Smith led the winners with two 3-run homers for 6 rbi's. Roy Shafer slugged a 2-run homer in the first. Ed Freisen, Shafer, and Sean Young each had 3 hits, with Freisen adding two more triples to his league-leading total.
