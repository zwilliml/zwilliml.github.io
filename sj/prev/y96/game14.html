1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Hammer Wollam Chevrolet 21-2

St. Joe dominated Wollam Chevrolet 21-2, invoking the mercy rule in the 5th inning and winning their 5th straight, a team record. The Hippos need only one more win in their last 6 games to make it to the Big Show! All 6 games are against tough teams.

A 9-run 2nd inning salted the game away against the Salesmen. The Holy Ones scored every inning of both games against Wollam.

Roy Shafer led the Saints with a 4-for-4, 4 runs scored, and a 2-run homer. Dave Smith went 3-for-3 and had another 3-run homer. Steve Barnes had 2 triples and 3 rbi's, while Ed Freisen had yet another pair of 3-baggers.

Al Ratini and Jim Wolfe are suffering from minor injuries, but should be full strength for the decisive 6 games next week.
