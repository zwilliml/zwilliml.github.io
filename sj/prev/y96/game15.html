1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Head to Playoffs with 10-5 Win over K of C

St. Joe's got that coveted tenth win of the season that propels them into the playoffs for the first time since 1988 by beating the Knights of Columbus 10-5 on Sabino Field Monday night.

It was their 6th straight victory, showing that once a herd of Hippos starts moving, they are hard to stop. Five games remain in the season as the Saints now set their sights on the all-time St. Joe victory total of 11, recorded in 1984.

The Holy Ones fell behind twice early on, but their relentless offense scored in every inning but one, and a near flawless defense against a fine 2nd place K of C club cemented the triumph.

The Hippos took the lead for good in the 5th when Al Ratini's sacrifice fly drove home Dave Smith. Jim Wolfe's 2-run single in the 7th provided insurance.

Bill Channel led the Hippos with 3 hits and 2 rbi's. Chris Hanzel added 2 hits and 2 ribbies.
