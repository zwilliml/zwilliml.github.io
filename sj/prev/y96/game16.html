1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Lose Doubleheader

Hand Over First Game to Pizza Joes 9-8

The high-flying, playoff bound, St. Joe Hippos plummeted to earth with a loud "thud" Wednesday, dropping both games of a doubleheader. In the lidlifter, the Holy Ones squandered an 8-4 lead in the 6th, as Pizza Joe's scored 5 runs in the last 2 innings in their come-from-behind victory.

The Saints stranded too many runners and couldn't get the big hit to break it open, and the defense crumbled in the end.

Ed Freisen had 3 hits for the Hippos. Mike Slabaugh had 2 hits, including a double, the only St. Joe extra base hit.
