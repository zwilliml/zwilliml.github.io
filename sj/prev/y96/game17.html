1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Dominated by Warren Home Remodelers 22-10

St. Joe dropped the 2nd game of the doubleheader to the Remodelers 22-10 as the Builders scored 7 in the top of the first, and, when St. Joe got close, 8 more in the 6th to cement the win.

It became apparent that the Holy Ones suffered a letdown after achieving their season goal last Monday by making the playoffs. The Remodelers scored at will, crossing the plate in every inning but one.

Steve Barnes had 3 Hippo hits, including a double.Sean Young smacked a 2-run homer in the 5th.
