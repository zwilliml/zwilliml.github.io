1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Another Doubleheader Disaster for Hippos

Fiest Hardwoods Hammers Hippos 18-13

St. Joe lost another doubleheader, their 2nd in as many days, and saw their once illustrious 10-5 record plunge in 30 hours to 10-9. In a game much like the previous one, Fiest jumped on the Hippos for 6 runs in the 1st, then when the game got close, salted it away with 5 in the 5th and 4 more in the 7th.

Jim Wolfe had 3 hits, including a double and a triple, for the Holy Ones. Ed Freisen had a triple among his 3 safeties. Mike Slabaugh slammed a 3-run homer in the 4th that brought the Saints to within 3 runs of the Woodsmen. First Baseman Mike Zwilling pulled off an unassisted doubleplay in the 2nd inning, fielding a grounder, tagging the runner off first, then running to the bag.
