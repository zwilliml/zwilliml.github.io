1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Army-Navy Explosion Devastates Hippos 21-15

Oh, this one hurt! St. Joe jumped on the Soldiers for an 8-spot in the first, and were nursing a 12-9 lead in the 5th when the roof caved in. The first two Soldiers went out quietly, but then Army-Navy exploded for 10 runs before the next out, in a total defensive debacle for the Hippos.

Just as in their previous meeting, the Soldier hurler experienced massive control problems, walking 12 Saints. But 21 runs from your teammates (and the Hippo lackluster defense) can erase such mistakes, giving the Soldiers revenge after they almost came from behind in game 11, only to have rain wash out their rally.

Chris Hanzel led the Hippos with 3-for-3 plus a walk and 2 rbi's. Roy Shafer had 2 doubles and a solo homer. Mike Slabaugh added 3 ribbies with 2 doubles.
