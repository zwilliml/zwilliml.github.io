Saint Joseph Church

1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Roar Past ITAM 16-7

The St. Joe Hippos (new name this season!) scored 15 unanswered runs to overcome a 7-1 deficit and post a 16-7 victory over ITAM 30. It was the first ever St. Joe win over the Italian Americans after 6 consecutive defeats over the past ten years. The victory evened the Hippo record at 1-1.

The rally from 6 runs down matched the greatest comeback in St. Joe recorded history: on May 25, 1990, the Saints erased a 9-3 Niles Expanded Metal lead and won it 11-10 in the bottom of the 8th on a Mike Slabaugh two-out single.

Head Hippo Al Ratini ignited this year's comeback with a lead-off single in the third. After 3 consecutive costly ITAM infield errors, Jim Wolfe (back with St. Joe this year after a stint with Keiffer's DDS) whacked a two-run single, followed by Chris Hanzel's sacrifice fly to narrow the gap to one run.

The rally continued in the fourth, as Steve Barnes tripled off the 300 foot sign (narrowly missing a duplicate of his over-the-fence out from game 1--this is D-League you know). Mike Zwilling's sac fly tied the game, and Ratini again singled to start another rally, as Roy Shafer tripled him home, and later scored on Bill Channel's single plus 3-base error.

The Hippo fifth started with five consecutive singles, followed by 2 more sac flies, then two more singles to cap a six-run uprising.

Jim Wolfe and Dennis Downing each had 3 hits for the Holy Ones. The Hippos also turned a snappy 5-4-3 DP in the sixth to squash ITAM hopes.
