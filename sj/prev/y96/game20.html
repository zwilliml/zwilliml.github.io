1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Beat K of C Again

St. Joe defeated the Knights of Columbus for the 2nd time this week, matching the 5-run margin of victory that propeled them into the playoffs Monday night, and led to the subsequent double-doubleheader letdown/meltdown Wednesday and Thursday. The final score was 13-8 to end the regular season and give St. Joe the season series in the Battles Between the Catholics.

The Saints defense firmed up this game, including a game-ending doubleplay when Dennis Downing fielded a grounder at 2nd, tagged the runner, and threw to first. Mediocre but timely hitting combined with K of C errors to supply the run production.

Dave Smith's 3-run homer in the 2nd turned a slender 4-2 lead into 7-2, but then the Holy bats went silent for 9 straight batters until 2 consecutive Knights errors set up another Ed Freisen triple in the 5th. Mike Slabaugh doubled home a much-needed insurance run in the 6th, followed by Sean Young's rbi single to seal the win.

Jim Wolfe injured his knee/ankle at 2nd base when he was undercut making a throw to first, and is doubtful for Wednesday's playoff game.
