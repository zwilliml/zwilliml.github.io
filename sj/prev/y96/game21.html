1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Ousted from Playoffs by K of C 11-10

In their first playoff appearance since 1988, the St. Joe Hippos played well but fell to the Knights of Columbus 11-10, ending a successful season with a winning record of 11-10.

After K of C patiently built a 9-3 lead, the Hippos staged a furious 6th inning comeback to tie the game, only to lose it in the bottom of the 7th.

The 6-run Hippo eruption began with singles by Chris Hanzel and Dave Smith. Then, with one out, an rbi single by Bill Channel was followed by an Al Ratini sac fly. Then the string of big 2-out hits began: Mike Zwilling, Mike Slabaugh, Steve Barnes, and Sean Young all hit safeties, followed by the game-tieing single by Dennis Downing.

K of C retook the lead 10-9 in the bottom of the 6th, but St. Joe came back in the 7th as Hanzel again ignited the rally with a lead-off single, taking 2nd base as the left fielder misplayed the hit. He advanced to 3rd on Smith's fly to right, then Jim Wolfe drew a walk. This set up the strangest and most important play of the game: Hanzel steamed for home plate as Bill Channel grounded to short for what looked like a game-ending doubleplay. The force at 2nd was made, but the throw to first barely pulled the first baseman off the bag and the Saints got the call as Hanzel scored the tieing run. As K of C argued the call, Wolfe kept running and drew a wild throw home, even though he had been forced out at 2nd. Channel then took 2nd base on this wild throw, but was forced to return when the umpires sorted out the mess. Al Ratini then singled Channel to 2nd, but the rally died as Zwilling lined out to center.

The Holy Ones managed to get one out in the bottom of the 7th, but three clean hits and the game-winner scored, ending the game and the season.

Dave Smith had 3 hits for the Saints, while Bill Channel had 2 hits and 3 rbi's. Chris Hanzel was 2-for-2. All Hippo hits were singles, only the 2nd time this season, as St. Joe missed the services of league triple leader Ed Freisen.
