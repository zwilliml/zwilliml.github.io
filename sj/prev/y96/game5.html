1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Trample Wollam Chevrolet 20-7

The St. Joe Hippos scored every inning as they roared past the Chevy Men 20-7. The Hippo offense was in high gear again as St. Joe banged out 19 hits, including 3 doubles and 2 triples, supplemented by 8 walks.

The Hippos took control of the game with the first at bat, as Dave Smith's intentional soft swing infield single was thrown past first, allowing the fleet Smith to go all the way to third. He scored on Mike Slabaugh's sacrifice fly.

A Hippo six run explosion in the 4th was ignited by Chris Hanzel's booming 300 foot triple. Mike Zwilling's rbi single was followed by manager Al Ratini's walk and a Smith ribbie hit. Jim Wolfe then cleared the bases with a towering triple of his own. Slabaugh doubled him home and scored later on an infield out.

Smith, Wolfe, Hanzel, and Zwilling each had 3 hits, and Wolfe had 5 rbi's. The Hippos are batting at a .456 clip as a team, much improved offensively over recent years.
