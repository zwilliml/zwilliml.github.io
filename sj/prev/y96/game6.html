1996 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Survive ITAM Rally 22-18

The St. Joe Hippos evened their season record at 3-3 Thursday night against ITAM #30, but not before getting a huge scare as they blew a 10-run 7th inning lead.

The Hippos coasted into the bottom of the 7th ahead 17-7 when the Italians exploded for 10 runs to tie the game, with the winning run gunned down at third by Mike Slabaugh as the runner unwisely attempted to stretch a double into a triple.

The 7th started as Hippo manager Al Ratini gave himself a rest after pitching 6 innings, bringing in Tom Moorehead to mop up. But the Hippo defense collapsed around Moorehead, plus a couple of walks and several hits, and Ratini returned to the mound when ITAM had closed the gap to 3 runs.

After the Italians tied the game, a scoreless 8th inning was followed by a 5-run Hippo outburst in the 9th. The Holy Ones loaded the bases on singles by Jim Wolfe and Bill Channel, followed by a Mike Zwilling walk. Roy Shafer then delivered the key hit--a 2-run single, followed two outs later by an rbi single from Moorehead. Two more Hippos scored when Ratini's swinging bunt was tossed into right field by the ITAM catcher.

Dave Smith, Moorehead, and Ratini were each 4-for-5 for St. Joe, and Steve Barnes collected 4 rbi's as this season's offensive robustness continued for the Holy Ones. The ITAM pitcher surrendered 7 walks, including 4 in the first inning when St. Joe scored 5 times on no hits, with 2 dropped flies added.
