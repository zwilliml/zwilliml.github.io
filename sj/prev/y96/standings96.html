St. Joe Softball 1996 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1996 Season Summary

For the first time since 1988, St. Joe made the playoffs and had a winning record. With the improvement already apparent at midseason with a 4-5 record, the Saints went on an all-time team record 6-game winning streak to reach 10 wins and the playoffs. After that mighty effort, the team suffered a letdown with two doubleheader losses in two nights, played well but lost in their playoff game, and settled on an 11-9 regular season record, 11-10 overall.

The St. Joe team name changed to Hippos, and with the change and new players Steve Barnes, Bill Channel, Dennis Downing, Dave Smith, and Jim Wolfe came one of the most potent St. Joe offenses in years, as well as a somewhat improved defense.

The newcomers were not the only ones responsible for the resurgence - veterans Chris Hanzel and Al Ratini had career years. Ed Freisen returned to the team after a 5-year hiatus and led the offense with a .603 average and a league-leading 12 triples.

The team batting average of .472 was the highest ever, and the first time in the .400's since 1985. The Hippos placed a team record 5 players in the league leaders for batting average. Fleet-footed Dave Smith led the league in homers with 6. Steve Barnes provided power, even hitting 3 over the fence for outs (D-League rules).
