1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Dilucia's Detonates Doubleheader Disaster

The St. Joe 1997 season got off to a rocky start as the Hippo's dropped a doubleheader, losing the first game to Dilucia's 15-8, then getting blasted by tough Commercial Construction 24-12. St. Joe fielded only the minimum 9 players.

The Caterer's scored early and often, building a 14-3 lead after 4 innings. The Holy Ones had a 4-run spurt in the 6th but were never in it.

Bill Channel and Bill Pelyak headed the Hippo offense, each going 3-for-3 with 2 rbi's. Pelyak included a double, as did Steve Stiles.
