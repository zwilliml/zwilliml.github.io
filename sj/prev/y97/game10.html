1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Commercial Construction Blasts Hippos 26-8

First place Commercial Construction demolished last place St. Joe 26-8, dropping the Holy Ones' season record to 1-9.

The Shepherds managed to hold the Builders scoreless in the first inning, thanks to a 6-3 double play. It was to be the only such inning in 10 this season with the Construction Workers.

The Hippo defense crumbled under the offensive pressure of the Constructionists, who scored 11 in the 2nd inning and 8 more in the 3rd, making the 12-run mercy rule again inevitable.

Karl Lyngstad led the Hippo offense with an rbi triple and a run-scoring single. Ken Nyers was perfect with 2 hits and a walk. Mike Zwilling knocked in 3 and had 2 hits. Brian Kurtz also registered 2 safeties.
