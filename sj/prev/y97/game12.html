1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Come From Behind To Beat ITAM 17-16

7th Inning Rally

St. Joe trailed the entire game until the last batter of the game, when Steve Stiles singles home the winning run with 2 out in the bottom of the 7th, giving the Hippos their 2nd win of the season, against 10 losses.

The Holy Ones trailed 16-14 going into the final frame. Chris Hanzel ignited the late-inning lightning with a single, but was forced at 2nd. The Saints then strung together 5 consecutive singles to win it. Tim Beckner's single got by the ITAM left fielder to put men at 2nd and 3rd. Chris Verbosky singled home one run, then Brian Channel tied it with a single, but Verbosky was gunned down trying to reach 3rd. Mike Zwilling set the table with a single, and Stiles' drive over the right center's head sealed the victory.

Both teams scored pretty much at will, similar to ITAM's 22-20 victory earlier in the season. The Shepherd defense finally settled down and allowed only 2 runs the last 3 innings.

Hero Stiles had 4 hits, as did Bill Channel, who drove home 4 runs. Beckner had 3 hits, and 6 Hippos had 2 hits each in the 24 hit attack. Adam Wytko had the rare sacrifice fly deep enough to score 2 runners.
