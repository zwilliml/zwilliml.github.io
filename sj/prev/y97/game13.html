1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Fall to Dentists 15-10

Blow 10-2 Lead

St. Joe built an early 10-2 lead but saw it evaporate as Purdy Excavating/Keifer Dentistry scored 13 unanswered runs to defeat the Holy Ones 15-10.

The Dentists feature several former Hippos, including Mike Slabaugh, Jim Wolfe, Sean Young, Roy Shafer, Dennis Downing, Pete Bartlett, Mike Sandor, and Donovan Garner, so this was a primo grudge match.

The Hippos capitalized on some shaky Dentist defense to build their lead, but then the Excavators solidified and turned two 5-4-3 double plays in shutting down the Hippo offense, while unleashing a hitting attack with 6 runs in the 3rd and another 6-spot the next inning. Shafer's solo homer in the 5th provided insurance.

Brian Channel led the Hippos with 3 hits, including a double. Adam Wytko had 2 hits, including a 2-out, 2-run single. Mike Zwilling added 2 safeties, and Brian Kurtz walloped a triple. Bob Jadick and Dan Wytko each had 2 hits.
