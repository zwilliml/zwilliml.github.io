1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

ITAM#33 Edges Hippos 15-13

Rally Falls Short

St. Joe battled back from a 15-6 deficit with 5 runs in the 6th and 2 more in the 7th, but the rally ended with the tieing run at the plate, as Brian Channel's 2-strike attempt to go down the right field line went foul. The loss dropped the Holy Ones' record to 2-12.

The Hippos got into their predicament in the 5th. The first place Italian Americans had already scored 3 to build an 11-6 lead and had loaded the bases with 2 out. The next batter sent a slicing line drive to right center, where Mike Zwilling over ran and muffed the ball for a 3-run error. The runner scored on the next batter, and what should have been a 3-run rally turned into a 7-run explosion.

St. Joe was led by Bob Jadick, who was 4-for-4 with a double and an rbi. Chris Verbosky was also perfect, 3-for-3 with a bases-loaded double in the 6th for 3 ribbies. Zwilling and Steve Stiles each had 2 hits and a double, and Adam Wytko and Bill Channel each had 2 safeties.
