1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Suffer Narrow Loss to Truckers 12-9

Record Drops to 2-13

Rood Trucking used a steady offense, scoring in every inning to defeat the Holy Ones 12-9. St. Joe built an early 5-1 lead, but that crumbled under the relentless offensive pressure from the Semi Drivers.

Bill Channel led the Hippos with a perfect 3-for-3 night, including a 2-run triple in the first. Tim Beckner was also perfect, with two hits and a sacrifice fly. Karl Lyngstad had a big night with two singles that generated 3 rbi's.

Mike Zwilling and Chris Verbosky each added two safeties for the Saints. Adam Wytko had a 2-run single in the 6th as the Hippos closed the score to 10-9, but in the bottom of the inning the light-hitting Trucker pitcher dumped a 2-run single between the pulled-in Hippo outfielders to give the Big Rig a 3-run insurance gap that they held onto.
