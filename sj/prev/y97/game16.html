1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Pizza Joe's Annihilates Hippos 32-11

2nd Worst Loss Ever

Lowly Pizza Joe's, with only 2 wins on the season (just like St. Joe), exploded for a 32 run eruption that demoralized the Saints and sent them reeling to a 2-14 record.

The Holy Ones had high hopes entering the battle of last place teams, but the Pizza Men firmly relegated the Hippos to the basement with their unprecedented detonation. Although the St. Joe defense was definitely lacking, never before have the Pizza Brothers demonstrated such an awesome offense, littering the field with scores of hits, interspersing line drive singles with gap shots, bouncers up the middle, and grounders between short and third.

After the initial shock of the 10-run first inning and the 11-run third wore off, the Hippos finally roused themselves from their slumber to stage a 9-run rally, but low and behold the Pizza Boys came right back with 9 runs of their own. The mercy rule was of course invoked.

Chris Verbosky was perfect for the Hippos, 2-for-2 with 2 rbi's. Chris Hanzel and Brian Kurtz also delivered 2 hits each, one of Hanzel's being a two-run triple. Karl Lyngstad walloped a 3-run homer for the Shepards.
