1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Tame Horseshoe Bar 13-10

Stiles Hits for Cycle

St. Joe held off a late rally by the Bartenders, despite all attempts to the contrary by the base umpire, who blew 2 consecutive out calls at first base in the 6th inning to contribute to a 5-run Horsemen rally.

The Shepherds built an 11-3 lead going into the 6th, thanks to heroics by Steve Stiles, who not only hit for the cycle but added an extra single for good measure in an astonishing 5-for-5 performance. He supplemented his first inning solo homer with an rbi triple in the 6th, a double in the 7th, and singles in the 2nd & 4th.

Ron Sanders had 3 hits for the Hippos, and Bob Jadick had 2 hits and 2 rbi's. Bill Channel also had 2 hits for the Saints.
