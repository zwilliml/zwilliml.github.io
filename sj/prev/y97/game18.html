1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippo Rally Drills Dentists 13-12

Come-From-Behind Victory

St. Joe, trailing 9-2 going into the bottom of the 6th, staged an 8-run uprising to lead 10-9, then fell behind 12-10 in the top of the 7th, then won it in the bottom with 4 straight singles supplemented by a crucial error.

Bob Jadick completed a perfect 4-for-4 night by leading off the final frame with a single, followed likewise by Bill Channel and Dan Wytko. Brian Kurtz came through with a sharp single to left center, which went through the outfielder to clear the bases and win the game for the Hippos, who have now won 2 in a row and raised their season record to 4-14, climbing out of last place (now inhabited by ITAM #30).

In addition to Jadick's heroics, which included 4 rbi's, Chris Verbosky and Channel each had 3 hits for the Holy Ones. Wytko, Kurtz, and Ron Sanders had 2 hits each against the arch-rivals Purdy.
