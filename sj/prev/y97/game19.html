Saint Joseph Church

1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

DiLucia's Dominates Hippos 17-5

St. Joe could do little to slow down the relentless first-place DiLucia offense as they scored every inning in a 17-5 drubbing.

Trailing 13-1, the Hippos needed to score in the 5th to extend the game, and they did--a 4-run rally with the help of some spotty defense. But the Restauranteurs came right back with 4 of their own to invoke the 12-run mercy rule. Three DiLucia double plays squelched the Saint hopes.

Steve Stiles had 2 of the 7 Hippo hits. Bill Channel whacked a triple and a sacrifice. Head Hippo Al Ratini returned to active duty after a broken foot and contributed a single, as did Ron Sanders, Chris Verbosky, and Brian Channel.
