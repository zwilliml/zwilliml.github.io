1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Commercial Construction Wallops Hippos 24-12

The 12-run mercy rule was invoked in the 5th inning as St. Joe was swept in the doubleheader. The Builder's relentless offense scored in every inning, including the last when they pushed across the game-ending run with no outs. Having only 3 outfielders really hurt the Hippos.

St. Joe trailed 23-4 going into the 5th when they staged an 8-run rally, but that extended the game by only a few batters in the bottom of the 5th.

The Holy offense was keyed by Brian Kurtz, with 2 doubles and a triple. Mike Zwilling also added 3 hits. Steve Stiles walloped 2 doubles, while brothers Bill and Brian Channel each had 2 safeties. Chris Hanzel had 2 hits and 3 ribbies.
