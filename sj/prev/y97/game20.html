1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Horseshoe Bar Hammers Hippos 14-1

Season Ends at 4-16

Horseshoe Bar took revenge for an earlier loss by trouncing St. Joe 14-1 in 5 innings. The Saints averted a shutout with a last inning error-aided run.

For the second straight game after the 2-game winning streak, the Hippo offense deserted them as The Holy Ones collected only 6 safeties, including one double by Steve Stiles. Bill Channel and Dan Wytko had 2 hits each, and Tim Beckner provided the remaining single.
