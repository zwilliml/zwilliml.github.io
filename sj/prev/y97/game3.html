1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Embarrased by Roofers 18-2

Ratini Breaks Foot

St. Joe dropped to 0-3 on the season as they were swamped by the lightly regarded D&K Roofing 18-2. The Hippos had handled the Roofers with relative ease last year.

The loss was compounded when St. Joe lost the services of Head Hippo and only pitcher Al Ratini, who was struck on the foot by a line drive. Ratini's foot was broken in two places (big toe and metatarsal), but he gamely pitched the rest of the contest, although he could not bat and the Holy Ones had to take an out at his spot in the order 3 times. Ratini was placed on the disabled list and may be lost for the season.

It mattered little to the anemic Hippo offense, which could muster only 6 hits and 1 walk against the Roofers, compared to last year when the Roofers distributed walks freely.

Bill Channel had the only extra base hit for St. Joe, a one-out triple in the first inning, but he was stranded on third in a harbinger of things to come. Mike Zwilling had 2 of the 6 hits, but also had defensive troubles at short.
