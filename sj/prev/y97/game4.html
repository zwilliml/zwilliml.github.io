1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Drop Slugfest to ITAM#30 22-20

Twenty runs and 27 hits were not enough as the Italian Americans avenged last year's defeats by staging a relentless scoring assault against the Hippos, who dropped to 0-4 on the season. Once again St. Joe could find only 9 players at game time.

The Holy Ones trailed 15-6 after 4 innings and were struggling to avoid a 12-run mercy-rule loss when they exploded for 11 runs. The rest of the contest was a see-saw affair, but ITAM could not be contained.

Chris Verbosky was pressed into service as pitcher as a result of Head Hippo Al Ratini's broken foot. Verb walked the first three batters and had control troubles , as any of us would in an unfamiliar position, but he responded on offense by going 3-for-4 with a double and 5 rbi's.

Bill Channel led the Hippo hitters with 5 ribbies on 5 hits, including 2 doubles and a triple. Chris Hanzel also tripled for one of his 3 safeties. Brian Kurtz had a night with 5 hits, including a double. Steve Stiles and Mike Zwilling also contributed 3 hits each.

But offense was not the problem.
