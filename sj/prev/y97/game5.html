1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

First-Inning Woes as Hippos Swept

Battle Back but Edged by Rood Trucking 14-13

ITAM#33 Clobbers Hippos 21-4

Rood Trucking staged an 11-run first inning explosion then held off the charging Hippos to win the first game of a doubleheader Wednesday night. ITAM #33 repeated the feat in the nightcap, scoring 9 times in the first and coasting to an easy 21-4 mercy-rule win. St. Joe dropped to 0-6 on the season.

In the lidlifter against the Truckers, the Hippos rallied for 6 runs in the 2nd and 4 more in the fifth, tieing the score on Chris Hanzel's 2-out rbi single. But Rood came back to score one in the sixth and held off the Hippos the rest of the way for the victory.

Steve Stiles, Bill Channel, and Brian Kurtz each had 3 hits for the Holy Ones, including a double apiece. Stiles also smashed the first Hippo homer of the season, a 2-run blast in the fifth that narrowed the gap to a 2-run deficit.

In the nightcap, ITAM #33 scored big every inning, especially the first, to easily handle the Hippos.

St. Joe could manage only 8 hits, one of them a homer by Steve Stiles to lead off the game.
