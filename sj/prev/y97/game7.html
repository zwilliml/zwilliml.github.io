1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Edged by Pizza Joe's 9-8

The Hippos rallied from a 9-1 deficit but fell just short as Dan Wytko's hard liner with 2 out, bases loaded, in the 7th was snagged by the Pizza Men's 3rd sacker to end the game.

Wytko and Steve Stiles blasted solo homers in the 5th, and Bill Channel's 2-run shot in the 6th narrowed the gap further, but the choking Pizza People (who issued 3 Hippo walks in the 7th) retained just enough air in their lungs to snuff out the surging Saints and secure the victory.

Hobbled Head Hippo Al Ratini could find only 8 healthy Hippos at game time (you need 9 to avoid a forfeit), so he was forced to insert himself into the lineup, broken foot and all. Pizza Joe's played catcher in his place, but his 4 plate appearances were automatic outs, probably costing the Saints their first win of the season.

Stiles paced the Holy Ones with 3 hits, including the homer plus a double. Adam Wytko also had 3 safeties. Tom Featsent was perfect with 2 hits and 2 walks, while Brian Channel added 2 hits and a BB. Tim Beckner had a double and scored a run.
