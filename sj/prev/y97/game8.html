1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Defensive Debacle Dooms Hippos

Worst Loss Ever 37-6

Yes, it's not a misprint, Olympic Inn clobbered St. Joe 37-6, the most runs ever allowed by the Holy Ones and the biggest margin of defeat in history.

When does a team allow 8 runs in an inning, twice, and call those the good innings? When the opponents score 18 times in one inning, that's when. The Olympians were already on their way to a mercy-rule laugher, leading 19-5, when they staged an 18-run eruption in the 4th inning to totally destroy the Shepherds.

The Innkeepers were aided by perhaps the most porous Saint defense ever. The errors and defensive lapses were already uncountable before the big inning, when the Olympians began to run the bases with abandon, taking extra bases at will while the hapless Hippos threw the ball away and simply dropped throws and fly balls.

It was not all the fault of the defense, however. Give the Innkeepers a lot of credit for a powerful offensive display, with oodles of clean hits and gap shots. And this record amount of runs in only 4 innings! You dare not extrapolate their score if they had batted 7 times.

A few St. Joe bright spots: Bill Channel tripled home the first run of the game and scored on Dan Wytko's sac fly to give the Hippos a brief lead. Steve Stiles burned the Innkeeper right fielder, who had moved way in, walloping a 2-run homer to close the gap to 8-4 in the 2nd inning.

Adam Wytko went 3-for-3, including a double. Chris Hanzel had an rbi double and a sacrifice fly. Mike Zwilling had 2 hits and made a fine running catch in the outfield that was relayed to home for a tag of the runner from 2nd. The double play accounted for one-sixth of the Olympian outs in the game.

The rest were harder to come by.
