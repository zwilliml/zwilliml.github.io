1997 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

St. Joe Triumphs over Ice Cream Zone 12-5

First Win of the Season

The Hippos played solid ball and were in control the whole way as they handily defeated the Ice Cream Zone 12-5. The victory elevated the Holy Ones' record to 1-8.

After Tuesday's calamity against the other division's first-place Olympic Inn, the Hippos faired much better against the West's last place Ice Cream Boys. Neither team had any wins coming into the contest.

St. Joe had solid defense behind the pitching of Chris Hanzel. The Shepherds took a 3-0 lead in the 3rd, then league-leading home run hitter Steve Stiles bashed his 5th round tripper of the year, a 3-run blast that burned the shallow-playing right fielder. An 4-run 6th sealed the victory.

Stiles lead the Hippos with 3 hits. Bill Channel and Adam Wytko had 2 safeties each, with Wytko garnering 3 rbi's. Mike Zwilling had a perfect night with an rbi single and 3 walks. Hanzel and Brian Kurtz each smacked a triple.
