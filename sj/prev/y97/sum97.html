St. Joe Softball 1997 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1997 Season Summary

After an 11-10 playoff year in 1996, several Hippo players bolted to Purdy/Keifer (Mike Slabaugh, Jim Wolfe, Sean Young, Roy Shafer, Dennis Downing, Pete Bartlett, Mike Sandor, and Donovan Garner), weakening both teams as a result (4-16 for St. Joe, 6-14 for Purdy).

The Holy Ones started the season with an 8-game losing streak and had trouble retaining players, always scrambling for more bodies in the first half. Defensive woes plagued the team all year.

Head Hippo Al Ratini suffered a broken foot in the 2nd game of the season and missed the next 16 games.

A Hippo protest was unfairly denied because the umpire changed his story when he found his first one wouldn't wash (it was not required to be in writing).

Bill Channel and newcomer Steve Stiles led the Hippo offense. Channel batted .625 to lead the team, and would have finished 4th in the league had accurate stats been kept. Stiles hit at a .571 clip, and slugged an impressive 17 hits for extra bases against 19 singles for a 1.063 slugging average, including a whopping 6 homers, which should have tied him for the league lead.

Former St. Joe veteran Bob Jadick returned and hit .583, while Mike Zwilling batted .563 and led the team in walks and sacrifices.

Newcomers Chris Verbosky and Brian Kurtz batted .468 and .464, respectively. Adam Wytko impressed with a .444 batting average.
