1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

St. Joe Drops Opener to Ohio Inn 14-8

Hippos Now in Continental League

Ohio Inn staved off a Hippo rally with a consistent offense and handed St. Joe a 14-8 setback in the first game of the season. The Holy Ones dropped down to the Continental League this season, the lowest league in Warren, in which over-the-fence homers are outs and each batter starts with a 1-1 count.

The Innkeepers scored 5 in the first and two the next frame, but the Shepherds tied the game with 7 runs in their half of the second, all runs scoring after two were out. Tim Beckner's 2-run double drove home the first two runs, and Steve Cleary knotted the score with a 3-run shot (inside-the-park, of course).

But Ohio Inn kept scoring while the Holy Ones were stymied. The Saints threatened in the fifth with men on 2nd and 3rd, no out, but squandered the golden opportunity. Then the Innkeepers used rally-squelching double plays in the final two frames, both coming with 2 on and 1 out.

Beckner led the Hippos with 3 hits, including two doubles, and 3 rbi's. Bill Channell had three safeties, while Cleary added a single to his homer. Veterans Al Ratini and Bob Jadick had two hits apiece.
