1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Gel, Beat The Rig 10-4

Win Snaps 4 Game Losing Streak

St. Joe looked sharp in a 10-4 victory over The Rig as the Holy Ones raised their season record to 4-6 and The Rig dropped to 7-3.

The Saints trailed 4-0 going into the 4th inning when a Bill Channel triple and 2 Rig errors brought home 2 runs. The Shepherds tallied twice more the next inning on 5 straight singles to tie the score.

Then came the 6-run explosion in the 6th, as the Hippos started the inning with 6 consecutive hits, highlighted by Bryan Channell's 2-run triple. The Rig could not recover from the blow as the Saint's defense looked solid.

Dan Wytko had 2 hits, each one producing a key 2-out rbi. Karl Lyngstad and Mike Zwilling each added 2 singles. Bill Channell added a single to his triple.
