Saint Joseph Church

1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Win 25-24 Slugfest Over Sherwin Williams

Heckathorn's 8th Inning Heroics Lift Saints

Troy Heckathorn smacked a bases-loaded one-out line drive single down the right field line out of the reach of the drawn-in outfielders to score Karl Lyngstad and bring a 25-24 extra inning victory to the Saints and raise their season record to 5-6.

Dan Wytko lead off the 8th with a single and advanced on Lyngstad's walk. Ron Sanders followed with a single, but Wytko was thrown out at the plate, runners advancing to 2nd and 3rd. Adam Wytko was given an intentional pass to load the bases and set up Heckathorn's blow.

Defense was nowhere in sight for either side as the offenses dominated the show, with only 3 of the 16 half-innings scoreless and plenty of crooked numbers posted on the board. St. Joe built an early 12-5 lead that evaporated under a withering 9-run Painter 4th inning. A 7-run Hippo 5th gave them back a 22-15 edge, only to be followed by a 6-run Painter 6th, who then held the Shepherds scoreless and added another 3 runs in the 7th to make it 24-22 Sherwin Williams.

But the Saints started the bottom of the seventh with a single from Ryan James, an rbi double by Brad Dangerfield, and an rbi single by Bill Channel to tie it. Al Ratini walked to put 2 on with no out, only to see the winning run die on 2nd as the next 3 batters couldn't bring him home. But that just set-up the winning 8th.

There were many offensive stars for the Hippos. Dangerfield blasted 2 home runs, including a grand slam, and had 6 rbi's. Bob Jadick had 4 hits, each producing a key rbi. Channell, Mike Zwilling, Dan Wytko, and Ron Sanders each had 3 hits. Adam Wytko had 2 hits, 2 walks, and 4 ribbies.
