Saint Joseph Church

1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Fall to Valley Containers 7-6

Rally Falls Short

St. Joe lost to lowly Valley Containers 7-6 in a low scoring affair that dropped the Saints to 5-7 on the season.

The Valley Boys used a key double play to snuff a bases-loaded one-out Hippo opportunity in the 6th. Trailing 7-4 entering the bottom of the 7th, St. Joe scored 2 runs to trail by one but could not get the tying runner on base.

Bill Channell had a single, 2 walks, and a sac fly for the Hippos, while brother Bryan had 2 hits. Brad Dangerfield doubled and tripled, and Bob Jadick had 2 sacrifice flies.
