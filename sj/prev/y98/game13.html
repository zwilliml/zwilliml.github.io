1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

St. Joe Subdues Papa Dew's 13-5

Ratini Goes 4-for-4

Al Ratini got 4 hits and 3 rbi's to lead St. Joe to a 13-5 victory over Papa Dew's and raise the Hippo record to 6-7 on the season.

Ratini's triple in the 2nd inning gave the Saints a 6-0 lead. The Dew Boys fought back with a 4-run spurt in the 3rd, but that was as close as they got. The Shepherds pulled away with a consistent offense and a steady defense.

Also leading the Hippo offense was Dan Wytko with 3 hits, while Adam Wytko, Brad Dangerfield, and Brian Spahlinger added 2 hits each.
