1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Drilled by Ohio Well 14-4

Lose in 5 Innings by 10-Run Rule

Ohio Well Management dominated St. Joe, scoring 6 times in the top of the first and coasting to an easy 14-4 victory over the Hippos. The loss dropped the Saints' season record to 6-8.

The Well Drillers' potent offense featured some savage shots off their bats, while the Shepherds' runs were produced by the Channell brothers.

Bryan Channell's 2-run triple in the first drove home brother Bill and Al Ratini, while Bill's triple in the 4th scored Mike Zwilling. Bill then scored on Bryan's single and the Hippo offense was finished.
