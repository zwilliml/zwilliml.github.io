1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Overwhelm Cape's Performance 17-12

Jadick Has 5 Rbi's

Bob Jadick led a consistent Saint offense to a 17-12 victory over higher-ranked Cape's Performance. The win improved the Shepard's season record to 7-8.

St. Joe put plenty of crooked numbers on the board as they piled up 17 hits, including 7 for extra-bases, and added 8 walks. Trailing briefly 7-6 as they entered the 4th inning, the Holy Ones put on a 5-run rally, featuring Bob Jadick's 2-out, 2-run single. Jadick also had a 2-run double in the first and added a sacrifice fly in the 6th.

Bill Channell had 3 hits, including a triple, and lined out sharply his other 2 at-bats. Al Ratini was 2-for-2 plus 2 walks. Steve Cleary had 2 doubles, while Brian Spahlinger and Bryan Channell each had 2 hits.
