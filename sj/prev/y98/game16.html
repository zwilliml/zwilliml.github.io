1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

First-Place Quality Machine Edges Hippos 8-4

Four-Run 6th Breaks Tie

Quality Machine put their 14-1 record on the line as St. Joe played well but lost the battle 8-4. The loss dropped the Saints to 7-9 on the season.

The Saints displayed a solid defense in muffling the potent Machinists, holding them to a 4-4 tie going into the bottom of the 6th. But the Q-Men broke through for 4 clean runs and held on to outlast the Hippos.

Adam Wytko was 2-for-2 for the Shepherds, and Brad Dangerfield also had 2 hits. No one else could muster more than a single as the St. Joe offense sputtered.
