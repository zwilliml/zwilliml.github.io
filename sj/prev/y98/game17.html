1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Dangerfield's Triple Spells Light's Out For GE Ohio Lamp

Hippos Post Extra Inning Win 9-8

Brad Dangerfield launched a two-out 8th inning triple to score Bryan Channell from first base and win the game as St. Joe beat GE 9-8 to raise their season record to 8-9 with one game to go.

Channell's single set the table after the first two Saints were out, and Dangerfield would have had a homer had not the winning run scored ahead of him.

The Shepherds took a 6-1 lead early in the game and seemed headed for an easy victory over the lowly 3-13 GE Men, but the Lamplighters rallied for 4 in the 6th and 2 more in the seventh to send the game into extra frames.

Dangerfield was perfect on the night, going 4-for-4. Bob Jadick had 2 hits, including a 2-out, 2-run single in the first. The St. Joe defense looked sharp, pulling off 2 double plays.
