1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Triumph Over Arch-Rivals Kiefer's 12-5

Win Puts St. Joe In Championship Playoffs

In the final game of the regular season and the year's biggest game, the Saints scored 7 runs in the first inning and coasted to a 12-5 victory over Kiefer's. The win brought the Shepherds to the .500 mark at 9-9, earning them a spot in the Championship Playoffs and avoiding the Consolation Playoffs for the below-.500 teams.

The powerful Dentists brought a 14-3 record into the game and are the only other team from Newton Falls in the Warren League. They feature several former Hippos, including Chris Hanzel, Sean Young, Roy Shafer, Pete Bartlett, Dennis Downing, Mike Slabaugh, Mike Sandor, and Jim Wolfe.

All the action in the Hippo's 7-run first inning came after two outs, as the Holy Ones had 7 consecutive hits, climaxing with Bryan Channell's 3-run homer. Kiefer's could never completely recover, although they mounted a 4-run rally in the fifth, but St. Joe came back with 4 of their own in the 6th. The Hippo defense again looked good and has noticeably solidified in recent weeks.

Bill Channell had 2 doubles for the Saints, while Adam Wytko, Steve Cleary, and Dan Wytko had 2 singles each. Bob Jadick whacked a 2-run double.
