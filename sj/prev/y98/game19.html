Saint Joseph Church

1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Shade Cape's 9-7

Win First Playoff Game

In their first playoff appearance since 1988, St. Joe survived a last inning scare to edge Cape's Performance 9-7 and advance to the second round of the championship playoffs.

Cape's threatened in the bottom of the seventh by putting men on second and third with two out. The next Cape's batter drove one down the left field line that, if fair, would have tied the game, but it landed inches foul. Head Hippo Al Ratini then struck him out looking to end the game and secure the first Saints playoff win in over 15 years.

The Hippos staged a 7-run 5th inning rally that made the difference, featuring Brad Dangerfield's 2-run homer in the midst of 6 hits and 2 walks from 8 consecutive Shepherds.

Ron Sanders was a perfect 2-for-2 for the Hippos. Dangerfield added a single to his homer, while Bob Jadick had 2 hits and 2 rbi's. Also tallying two hits each were Dan and Adam Wytko, Steve Cleary, and Brian Spahlinger.
