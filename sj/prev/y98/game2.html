1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Romp to 14-2 Win over ETI

First Win of Season

St. Joe stormed ETI Technical College with 8 runs in the top of the first, then coasted to a 14-2 victory, ended early by the 10-run mercy rule.

The College Boys never recovered from the opening frame, which began with a 2-run homer from Dan Wytko, and was followed by a 3-run blast off the bat of baseball veteran Brad Dangerfield in his first ever plate appearance in a softball game.

Other Hippo offensive stars were Bryan Channell with a double and a single and 2 rbi's, Bob Jadick with 2 singles and a ribbie, and Karl Lyngstad with two hits.
