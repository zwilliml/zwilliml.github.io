1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Blast Ohio Well 16-11

Advance in Playoffs

St. Joe unleashed a blistering 24-hit attack to upset heavily favored Ohio Well 16-11 in the second round of the Continental League Championship Playoffs. Every Hippo had at least one hit, and the Saints scored every inning but one.

Ohio Well finished the season in second place with a 14-4 record and had defeated the Saints by the 10-run rule earlier this year. The Shepherds led the entire game, but the Well Drillers stayed close until they finally ran out of gas after the Holy Ones' 4-run 7th inning provided enough insurance.

Brian Spahlinger led the Hippos with a perfect 4-for-4, including a big 2-run triple in the 7th, plus 3 rbi's. Bryan Channell walloped a bases-loaded triple in the first, as one of his 3 hits. Adam Wytko had 3 hits and 2 ribbies, while Mike Zwilling also had 3 hits.

Head Hippo Al Ratini pulled a leg muscle and is hobbled for the remaining games.
