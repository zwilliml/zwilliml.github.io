1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Upset Quality Machine 5-3

Reach Finals of Winner's Bracket in Playoffs

It looked like a mismatch on paper. Regular season champion Quality Machine, sporting a 19-1 record and a previous win over St. Joe, against the team that barely squeaked into the playoffs on the season's last day with a 9-9 record. But when the dust had settled, the Saints had upset the mighty Machinists and advanced to the finals of the winner's bracket.

The score was tied 3-3 in the low scoring affair going into the 7th inning. Ryan James started it off with a single, then scored when Adam Wytko's single skipped by the Machinists' left fielder.

The Q-Boys put a man on first in the bottom of the 7th with one out, but the next batter lined out to Mike Zwilling at third base, who's relay to first doubled off the runner for a double play to end the game.

The Hippos had only 8 hits, and Bob Jadick had 3 of those, including a double and 2 rbi's in his perfect night. Adam Wytko had 2 hits for the Shepherds.

Bill Channell pulled a muscle and is hobbled along with Al Ratini.
