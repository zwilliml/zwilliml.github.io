1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Fall to Kiefer's 11-3

Now Go To Finals of Loser's Bracket in Playoffs

St. Joe's 5-game winning streak and 3-game playoff winning streak were snapped by arch-rival Kiefer's in an 11-3 setback that sent the Saints to the finals of the loser's bracket of the playoffs.

Having defeated Kiefer's in the last game of the season to reach the playoffs, the Shepherdscould not get their offense untracked this game and could manage only 4 hits. The Dentists scored 4 runs in the 3rd and coasted to an easy win.

Adam Wytko had 2 hits, half the Holy total. Steve Cleary bashed a solo homer in the 7th, and Al Ratini had the other Hippo hit.
