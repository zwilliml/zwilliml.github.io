1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Ohio Well Eliminates Hippos from Playoffs 9-8

Saints Take Third Place -- All Time Best

It all came down to the classic situation in softball: score tied 8-8, bottom of the 7th, bases loaded, full count. Head Hippo Big Al Ratini had to throw a strike. He did, and the Ohio Well batter singled sharply up the middle to end the game and the Hippo playoff hopes.

Ohio Well thus avenged an earlier playoff loss to the Saints, and took the rubber game of the 3 games the teams played this year.

In this see-saw affair, the Saints lost a 4-0 early lead, and the Well Drillers lost an 8-5 lead late in the game. The difference was 3 key double plays that Ohio Well got just when they needed them.

The Holy Ones did well to get 8 runs from only 10 hits, plus 5 walks. Ryan James led the way with a perfect 2-for-2. Bill Channell had 2 hits and an rbi. Adam Wytko tripled a knuckler off the shortstop's glove that went through the outfield.

Thus ended the greatest year in St. Joe softball history. The Hippos reached the playoffs on the last game of the season, won 3 straight playoff games to reach the finals of the winner's bracket, beat all the top teams at least once, and finished in third place.
