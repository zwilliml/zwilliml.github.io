1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Aqua Care Edges Hippos 16-15

Intentional Walk Pays Dividends

Aqua Care scored in every inning to edge St. Joe 16-15 in a see-saw contest where the lead changed hands 4 times in the last 3 innings.

The Saints had just taken the lead 15-14 in the bottom of the 6th, and had a man on third with 2 outs. The Water Men intentionally walked the dangerous Bill Channell to set up a force, and Bob Jadick lined out to end the Hippo threat. The Aquarians then retook the lead in the top of the 7th, while the Holy Ones meekly went 3-up 3-down to end it.

Channell led the Saints with a 3-for-3, including a triple. Dan Wytko added 3 hits, while Brad Dangerfield hit a 3-run homer and doubled home another run. Steve Cleary whacked 2 triples for St. Joe.
