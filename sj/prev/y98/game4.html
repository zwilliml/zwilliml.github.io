1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippo Homers Hammer Sim's Buick

16-6 Victory Evens Season Record at 2-2

Steve Cleary's first-inning grand slam home run and his 6th inning solo blast anchored a 22-hit barrage against Sim's Buick in a game St. Joe won 16-6 by the 10-run rule.

The Car Salesmen rallied to tie the score at 6-6 in their half of the 5th, but Brad Dangerfield's solo homer leading off the bottom of the frame ignited a 7-run rally to swamp Sim's.

Bryan Channell also swatted a solo blast and was perfect on the night with 3 hits and 3 rbi's in 3 trips to the plate. Dangerfield was also 3-for-3, and Mike Zwilling added 3 hits for the Hippos. Al Ratini was a perfect 2-for-2 with 2 ribbies and a sac fly. Ryan James added 2 hits and 2 rbi's for the Holy Ones.
