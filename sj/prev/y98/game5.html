1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippo's Swamp Little Caesar's 18-5

St. Joe Now Over .500 Mark with 3-2 Record

The Saints took an early lead and sealed the win with a 10-run explosion in the 4th that brought premature thoughts of invoking the new league 20-run rule after 3 innings. As it was, the 10-run rule was implemented after the 5th to avoid further embarrassment to the Pizza Boys.

Offensive standouts for the Holy Ones included Adam Wytko and Steve Cleary, who were perfect 3-for-3's. Adam's brother Dan slugged a homer and a triple and garnered 3 rbi's, and Al Ratini was a flawless 1-for-1, adding a walk and a sac fly. Four more Hippo's had 2 hits each.
