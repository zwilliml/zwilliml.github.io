1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippo's Swallow Bitter Pill

Amerital Rally Beats Saints 18-17

Amerital Club staged a 3-run game-winning rally in the bottom of the seventh to come from behind and defeat Saint Joe 18-17. The loss evened the Hippo season record at 3-3.

Neither team could contain the other in the high scoring affair, as the Hippos tallied in every inning and Amerital scored in every frame but one. As is often the case, the team that bats last in such affairs usually wins. Neither team sparkled in the field, but the Americans turned two crucial double plays to stem Hippo rallies.

Brad Dangerfield led off the game with a solo homer, and he later added a double, single, and a walk for a perfect night, including 4 rbi's. Steve Cleary swatted a key 2-run homer in the 6th, among his 3 hits. Bob Jadick each had 3 hits and 3 ribbies, while Adam Wytko also had 3 safeties.
