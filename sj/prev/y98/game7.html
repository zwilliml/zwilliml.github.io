1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippo's Fall to UAW 13-8

Dan Wytko Injured but OK Now

UAW staged an 8-run 4th inning rally to open up a close game and beat St. Joe 13-8. The loss droppped the Hippo's record to 3-4.

Dan Wytko, shortstop for the Holy Ones, was hit on the right side of the mouth by a sharp bad hop in the 5th ining and had to be removed from the game. It took 5 stitches to close the wound. Wytko is doing well now but his status for Wednesday's game is uncertain.

Adam Wytko also needed to leave the game, to assist brother Dan. Since all 11 available players were in the starting lineup, the Hippos had to take an out each time the Wytkos' spots in the order arrived, snuffing out potential rallies in the 6th and 7th.

Bob Jadick led the Saints with a perfect 3-for-3, including a double. Bryan Channell blasted a two-run homer, and added a run scoring double. Al Ratini and Mike Zwilling each had 2 hits and a ribbie, while Steve Cleary added 2 safeties.

Brian Spahlinger was also perfect on the night with a hit, an rbi, and 2 walks. Brad Dangerfield and Bill Channell each slugged triples, while Adam Wytko had a double, a sac fly, and 2 ribbies.
