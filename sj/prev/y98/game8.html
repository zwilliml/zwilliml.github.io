1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

McDonald's Fries Hippo's 9-4

Third Straight Loss for St. Joe

With Head Hippo Al Ratini nursing a pulled hamstring, Tim Beckner received the starting nod and was roughly greeted by a McDonald's first inning 6-run rally that effectively won the game before the Saints' first at bats in this low scoring affair.

Veteran Bob Jadick relieved Beckner in the second, but The Holy Ones could generate only 4 runs on 10 hits, 9 of them singles. The loss dropped the Shepards to 3-5 on the season.

Brad Dangerfield and Mike Zwilling each had 2 singles for the Hippos, while Bill Channell added an rbi double. Tim Beckner garnered a single and a walk.
