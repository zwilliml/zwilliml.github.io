1998 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippo's Fall to Fat Wally's 11-4

St. Joe Losing Streak at 4 Games

The midpoint of the softball season arrived as the Saints lost their 4th straight, this time to Fat Wally's. Threatening skies opened up with a downpour in the 7th to end the game with one on, one out. The Hippo record dropped to 3-6 with the loss.

A hobbled Al Ratini was pressed into service as The Holy Ones began the game with only 9 players and had to take an automatic out when the 10th spot in the batting order arrived, according to the new shorthanded rule. Since there were two out when the ninth batter singled, the Saints will know next time to keep on running until put out. Dan Wytko arrived in the 4th to prevent further automatic outs.

The Shepherds' offense once again deserted them as they repeated the previous game's output of 4 runs on 10 hits. Brad Dangerfield had the only extra base hit, a triple that he tried unsuccessfully to turn into a homer. Dangerfield added a bloop single that fell in the infield because of the sudden drenching rain, after which the proceedings came to a halt. Mike Zwilling was the only other Saint with more than a lone single.
