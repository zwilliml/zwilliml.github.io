St. Joe Softball 1998 Season Summary, Standings, and Play-offs

1998 Season Summary

St. Joe had its greatest season in history as they made the playoffs, won their first ever playoff games, and reached the finals of the winner's bracket before bowing out with a third place finish. Head Hippo Big Al Ratini declared "This team has character."

The Hippos dropped down to the Continental League this year, in which each batter starts with a 1-1 count, and home runs over the fence are an out (not a factor for the Hippos). Teams from both divisions with a .500 mark or better in the regular season reached the Championship Playoffs, while the rest played in the Consolation Playoffs.

The Saints peaked at exactly the right time. Struggling with a 7-9 record and 2 games left in the season, they won those last 2 games, defeating mighty Kiefer's to edge into the playoffs as the lowest seed.

Then the Holy Ones shocked the league by winning 3 straight playoff games, including a gigantic upset of 19-1 Quality Machine. The Shepherds ended the year with a 12-11 record.
