1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

St. Joe Wins Opener 16-7 Over Leon's

Hippos Sharp; Leon's Shaky

The St. Joe Hippos easily won their season-opening game by a score of 16-7 over an outgunned Leon's team in a game played Thursday night at POW-MIA's Field (Packard #4).

Leon's appeared shaky in the top of the first as they walked the first two Hippos and had shoddy fielding as the Shepherds scored 4 runs. Another 4-spot in the 4th inning came on only 2 hits and several Leon's errors. The Saints sealed the game with a 6-run explosion in the 7th, capped by head Hippo Al Ratini's 3-run homer.

Dan Wytko and Eric Noebe blasted solo shots, while Mike Zwilling added 3 hits. Tom Davidson singled twice, while Brian Channell tripled. Troy Heckathorn and Ryan James each corked a double for the Hippos.
