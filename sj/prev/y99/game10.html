1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Drop 21-20 Heartbreaker to Lexington Connector

Another Last Inning Defeat

St. Joe lost a 21-20 slugfest to Lexington Connector, on a last play sacrifice fly to a pulled-in Hippo outfield, just like a week earlier against Chester's Lounge. The loss knocked the Saints out of first place in the Continental West Division.

Defense was nowhere to be found as offense and errors abounded, and the lead see-sawed back and forth. Lexington also issued 9 walks to St. Joe.

Bill Channell's 3-run homer in the top of the first gave the Holy Ones an early lead, which they squandered, and brother Bryan Channell's grand slam home run provided another short-lived lead in the 5th. Ryan James then corked a 3-run shot that brought the Shepherds back again, but it was not to be.

Bill Channell had a night with 4 hits, including his homer, plus 5 rbi's and 5 runs scored. Brother Bryan Channell matched the 5 ribbie total. Dan Wytko and Al Ratini had 3 hits each, while Adam Wytko, Tim Beckner, and Bob Jadick each added 2 safeties. Eric Noebe doubled and singled for the cause.
