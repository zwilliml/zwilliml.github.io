Saint Joseph Church

1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Subdue FUBAR Crew 12-7

Combine Solid Defense and Clutch Hitting

St. Joe fell behind 4-1 but then used two 5-run innings and two double-plays to bring the game under their control.

Hippo veterans Al Ratini and Bob Jadick were each perfect, going 2-for-2. Ratini added a sacrifice fly and 2 rbi's.

Bill Channel and Eric Noebe had 2 hits each, with Channell clubbing a triple over the right fielder's head, and Noebe bopping a double and garnering 3 ribbies.

Dan Wytko had 2 hits and 2 rbi's, while Mike Zwilling added 2 hits and keyed the two rally-squelching twin-killings. Ryan James corked a triple down the left field line.

FUBAR is a military acronym for "Fouled Up Beyond All Recognition", although more colorful language is often substituted.
