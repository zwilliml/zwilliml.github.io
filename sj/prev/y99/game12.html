Saint Joseph Church

1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Survive Creekside Rally 14-11

Season Record at 8-4; Need One Win to Make Playoffs

St. Joe appeared to be cruising to an easy victory, leading 10-3 in the 6th, when Creekside Golf Dome mounted an 8-run rally to take a sudden 11-10 lead. But the Saints rebounded with 4 runs in the 7th and held on to win.

The Holy Ones roared out to 6-0 advantage in the top of the first as Mike Zwilling drove home the first 2 runs with a bases loaded single. The Shepherds added 3 more the next inning and were feeling comfortable.

But second-baseman Zwilling opened the floodgates with a pair of two-out errors in the 6th (a bobble and a throw in the dirt), allowing the Golfers to take the 11-10 lead.

Adam Wytko led off the 7th with a single for the Hippos, and brother Dan chased him home with a single that skipped through the left-center fielder. With the Perkins Park fences removed and the ground hard as concrete, the ball rolled forever and both Wytkos scored to take back the lead. Two more insurance runs scored when the Golfer shortstop dropped an easy two-out bases-loaded pop-up.

Head Hippo Al Ratini led the way with a perfect 3-for-3 plus a walk and an rbi. Dan Wytko had 3 hits and scored 4 runs, upping his near-league-leading total in that category.

Ryan James doubled and singled for the Hippos, while Bob Jadick, Eric Noebe, and Zwilling each added 2 hits.
