1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Jolt UAW 11-4

Ninth Win Guarantees Playoff Spot

St. Joe played solid ball and handily defeated UAW 11-4 to raise their season record to 9-4. The two teams were tied for first place going into Thursday night's battle, so the win not only gave sole possession of the division lead to the Hippos, but also secured them a spot in the championship playoffs.

Once again, the Saints scored early to jump out in front and put pressure on the opposition. The scoring in their 4-run first inning began with Mike Zwilling's bases-loaded single, followed by a 2-run single by Bob Jadick. Another run scored on a fielder's choice. The outbreak seemed to deflate the Auto Workers, who did not look sharp.

A 5-run explosion in the 4th sealed the victory, as Adam Wytko belted a 2-run homer, and 2 more runs scored on a confusing bases-loaded sacrifice fly by Bill Channell in which all 3 Hippos failed to tag up, but all returned and then advanced as the Union Men threw the ball away.

Bill Channell was a perfect 2-for-2, both doubles, plus his sac fly. Zwilling was also 2-for-2 with a sacrifice. Adam Wytko added a single to his homer.

Eric Noebe's 2 hits included a triple, and Ryan James had 2 hits for the Holy Ones.
