1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Fall to BW3#2 7-5

Lack of Offense Dooms Shepherds

St. Joe stranded 11 men and scattered their 11 hits without getting the big blow they needed as they fell to BW3#2 7-5. The loss dropped the Hippos into a first place tie with the Wingsters.

Chris Miller was not Buffaloed as he solved the Wild Ones' pitcher for 3 hits in 3 attempts. Also having perfect nights were Tim Beckner and Karl Lyngstad, both 1-for-1.

Dan Wytko had 2 hits, while Josh Williams tripled home a run. Ryan James clouted a double for the Holy Ones.
