1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Pounce on Outback Steak House 16-6

Raise Record to 10-5

St. Joe rode a big 19-hit attack to an easy 16-6 victory over Outback Steak House.

The Saints pounced on the Aussies for 7 runs in the 2nd inning and coasted the rest of the way. Bryan Channell struck the big blow that inning with a 2-run homer. Channell later added a double and a single for a perfect night, and was just a triple away from hitting for the cycle.

Also perfect on the night were Hippo veterans Al Ratini and Mike Zwilling, both 2-for-2. Ratini had 3 rbi's and scored 3 runs, while Zwilling had 2 ribbies.

Also with 2 hits each against the Wonders from Down Under were Dan Wytko, Bob Jadick, Ryan James, and Troy Heckathorn.
