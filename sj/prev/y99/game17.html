1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Rally to Beat BW3#1 13-12

Score 5 Runs in Bottom of 7th to Win

Just one night after a great victory over Aqua Care, the Hippos posted another triumph in perhaps their greatest come-from-behind win ever, a 13-12 thriller over BW3#1.

The Saints exploded for 5 runs in the first, but the Buffalo Boys chipped away and then had their own 7-run eruption in the 4th. The Wingsters took a 12-8 lead into the bottom of the 7th.

Al Ratini led off the 7th with a walk, one of 8 free passes issued by the Buffalo pitcher. One out later, Bob Jadick singled Ratini to 2nd, and Josh Williams doubled home a run to put men on 2nd and 3rd. Bryan Channell's infield single loaded the bases, but then Tim Beckner whiffed to make it 2 outs, bases loaded, 3 runs down.

Jake Massacci drew a walk to force home a run, and Rob Bauman singled home another to make the score 12-11. Then Dan Wytko lined a sharp single up the middle to chase home Channell and Massacci, winning the game.

Dan Wytko and Rob Bauman each had 3 hits for the Hippos, while Bob Jadick and Bryan Channell each slapped 2 singles.

BW3#1 entered the game with a record of 11-6 in the East division, while St. Joe came in 11-5 in the West. The win kept alive Hippo hopes of a first place finish in their division - they need to win their last game against Valley Containers, and hope that the other BW3 team (BW3#2) loses a game. The Saints are tied with BW3#2 for first place, but lost to them in head-to-head competition.
