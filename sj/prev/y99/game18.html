1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Whip Valley Containers 15-11

End Season Second in Division

Bill Channell was a one-man wrecking crew for St. Joe as he hit 2 homers, knocked in seven runs, scored 3 times, and went a perfect 3-for-3 in leading the Hippos to a season-ending 15-11 win over Valley Containers.

The win was the 4th in a row for the Holy Ones, as they finished the season with a best ever record of 13-5, good for second place in their division. BW3#2 also finished at 13-5 as they edged UAW 9-8, so the Buffalos claimed first place by virtue of their win over the Saints in head-to-head competition.

Ryan James was also a power in this game for the Holy Ones, as he went 3-for-4, including 2 doubles. Dan Wytko, Eric Noebe, and Troy Heckathorn each had 2 hits.

The Saints also played some defense as they threw out 3 runners at 3rd and another at home.
