1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Fall to UAW 13-3

Lose First Playoff Game

When the Titanic sunk, they called it "A Night To Remember".

When the Hippos stunk, they called it "A Night To Forget".

The Hippos stunk on both offense and defense, and lost the first playoff game of the double elimination tournament to UAW 13-3 in a game called after 5 innings by the 10-run mercy rule.

St. Joe seemed to have everything going for them coming into the game: they were 13-5, while UAW was 9-9; they had won 4 in a row, while UAW had lost 3 in a row; they beat UAW 11-4 earlier in the season. But the Holy Ones were never in this game, looking bad from the beginning.

Jim Massacci was one of the few Hippos to play well. He had a 2-out rbi single both times up. Bill Channell was also 2-for-2. Dan Wytko homered in the first inning as his hit took a bad hop past the outfielder.

But otherwise, this was "A Night To Forget".
