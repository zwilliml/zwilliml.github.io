1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Gobble Up Little Caesar's 18-8

Improve Record to 2-0

St. Joe jumped to an early 9-0 lead and never looked back as they trounced Little Caesar's by the 10-run rule, 18-8.

The Saints scored every inning but one, finishing off the Pizza Men in the 6th inning with 4 consecutive hits to invoke the mercy rule.

Tim Beckner was 3-for 3 for the Shepherds, including a double. Also leading the Hippos were veterans Bob Jadick and Al Ratini, each going 3 for 4. Jadick had 4 rbi's and Ratini added 3 more.

Dan Wytko was perfect, going 2-for-2 with a double, and adding 2 walks. Also garnering 2 hits each were brother Adam Wytko and Bryan Channell.
