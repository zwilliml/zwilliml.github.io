Saint Joseph Church

1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Lose to Ohio Inn 9-6

Bow Out of Playoffs in Two Straight

St. Joe was eliminated from the playoffs by falling to Ohio Inn 9-6.

The Saints once again did not look sharp on either offense or defense. The only bright spot was a grand slam home run by Jake Massacci in the fifth, finally breaking the Hippos into the scoring column.

Massacci's blast was followed on the next swing by a solo homer from Ryan James. The Holy Ones also scored in the sixth on Bill Channell's single, but were unable to get another base runner the rest of the way.

Hippo veterans Al Ratini and Bob Jadick each had 2 hits, of only 9 the Saints could muster.
