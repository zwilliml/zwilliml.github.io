1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Jail Bail Bondsmen 14-4

Season Off to Best Ever 3-0 Start

St. Joe never trailed and exploded for 7 runs in the 5th to ice a 14-4 mercy-rule victory over Ace Bail Bonding. The win raised the high-fling Hippos to a 3-0 season record.

Eric Noebe had a night for the Saints, going a perfect 4-for-4 plus 3 rbi's. Noebe's sharp single to center in the 6th squirted through the outfielder's legs and allowed Dan Wytko to scamper home with the game-ending run.

Head Hippo Al Ratini was also impeccable, going 3-for-3 with a double and 2 ribbies. Ryan James had 2 hits for the Shepherds, as did Tom Davidson.
