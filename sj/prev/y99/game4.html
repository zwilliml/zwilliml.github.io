1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Edged by Glunt Machine 7-5

First Loss of the Season

St. Joe suffered their first loss of the season in a close 7-5 game against Glunt Machine. The loss dropped the Hippo season record to 3-1.

The Saints never held the lead in this low-scoring affair. They threatened in the last inning by putting their first two men on (by error and walk), but Dan Wytko hit into a force for the first out, then was doubled off first when a wicked line drive by Josh Williamson was snagged by the pitcher, ending the threat and the game.

The Shepherds played good defense, pulling off two sharp double plays, but did not hit well, as 15 of their 21 outs were in the air, plus two strike outs. Mike Zwilling, Bob Jadick, and Jake Massacci each managed two hits for the Holy Ones.
