Saint Joseph Church

1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Shade RD Banks 13-12

Controversy Erupts in Nail-Biter

St. Joe edged RD Banks 13-12 in a close, tense, battle marred by controversy.

The Hippos erupted for 7 runs in the second and appeared to be cruising, turning 3 double plays in the first 4 innings. But the Chevy Dealers came back with 5 runs in the 4th and 6 more in the 5th. They would have had even more, but the umpire ruled that the Salesmen third base coach left his box, apparently aiding a runner rounding third, whose run was disallowed and converted to an out instead. The Car Men bellowed in protest, to no avail.

Then in the final frame, a Banks hitter tripled home the 12th run and tried to stretch it into a homer, but was tagged out by Troy Heckathorn at the plate on a very close play, triggering more cries of disapproval. Some of the frustration might have been attributed to the inadvisable attempt to score such an important run from third with only one out. Sure enough, the next batter flied deep to right, which would have tied the game; instead, Jake Massacci snagged it to seal the Saints victory.

Mike Zwilling, Josh Williamson, and Eric Noebe were each 3-for-3 for the Holy Ones. Dan Wytko had 2 hits and 3 rbi's, while Bob Jadick and Ryan James each had 2 hits and 2 ribbies. Heckathorn also singled twice for the Hippos, who raised their season record to an impressive 4-1.
