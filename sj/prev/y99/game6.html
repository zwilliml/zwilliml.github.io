1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Dentists Drill Hippos 10-4

Offense Sputters in Loss

St. Joe fell behind early, but clawed back to pull within one run in the 6th inning, before a 5-run explosion by the Dentists sealed the Hippos' fate.

The Saints offense could not create the big inning, scattering 11 hits with only one for extra bases. Two double plays squelched Hippo rallies, and Adam Wytko was thrown out at home on a fine throw from right field in the fifth on his brother Dan's double to prevent the tieing run from scoring.

Tim Beckner was a perfect 2-for-2 for the Holy Ones, driving in a run with a key 2-out single. Bill Channell and Mike Zwilling also had 2 hits each.
