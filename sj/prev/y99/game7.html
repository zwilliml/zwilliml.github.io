1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Edge Waynco Electric 10-9

Saints Come From Behind

The Hippos fell behind 8-1 early in this game, but fought back with 9 runs in their last 3 innings, then held on to squelch a Waynco 7th inning rally for the victory.

Dan Wytko was perfect, with 4-for-4 including a double. Bill Channell had 3 hits, with a triple and 2 rbi's. Chris Miller and Tim Beckner each doubled and singled, while Al Ratini added 2 hits.
