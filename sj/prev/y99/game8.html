1999 St. Joe Softball Game Recap and Box Score

Hippos Shaded 12-11 by Chester's Lounge

Tense Thriller Goes 9 Innings

Chester's Lounge pushed across the winning run in the bottom of the 9th to edge St. Joe 12-11 in an exciting game.

Chester's threatened in the 8th, placing the winning run on third with one out, but the next batter hit a shallow fly to the pulled-in right-center fielder Mike Zwilling, and the runner couldn't score. One batter later the runner was stranded.

Chester's repeated the situation in the 9th: man on third, one out, shallow fly to Hippo right fielder Ryan James, and this time you could sense that the Loungers were going to force the issue. The runner took off and the throw made it close, but he scored to end the game.

The Hippos mounted a 6-run rally in the 2nd on 5 hits and some sloppy Lounge fielding, but the Saints returned the favor in the 5th, allowing 8 Lounge runs while displaying lackluster defense.

James doubled and singled for the Shepherds, while Dan Wytko, Bill Channell, and Bob Jadick had 2 hits each. Josh Williamson pulled off a key double play in the 6th, tagging the runner from first and then relaying to Jadick to complete the twin killing.
